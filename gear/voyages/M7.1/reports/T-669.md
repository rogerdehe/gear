# T-669 — `- restart <lane|reviewer>`

## What changed
- `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs`: `restart_reviewer` renamed `restart_seat` (caller updated). It now:
  - a `Lane` in `Active` is accepted and gets `engine.restart.<seat id>` = `{"at": now}`;
  - a lane in any other state is refused with `BadTransition` and `seat {seat_id} is {state}: only an
    active lane is restarted`;
  - a `Captain` seat is refused with `BadTransition` and `seat {seat_id} is a captain seat, `restart`
    names a lane or a reviewer`;
  - the reviewer (`Temp`) rules are unchanged.
- `crates/gear-app/src/draft.rs`: the `restart` messages are now `` `restart` needs a lane or a reviewer ``
  and `` `restart` takes only a lane or a reviewer ``; the `StaffItem::Restart` doc says a lane restarts at
  once (wrap-up, grace, exit, resume of its session).
- `skills/gear/references/captain.md` and `skills/gear/references/course.md`: `- restart <reviewer>` is now
  `- restart <lane|reviewer>`, with the lane sentence (`wrap up and commit`, then after
  `[timing].switch_grace_min` the agent restarts on the same model, resuming its session).

## Test (`crates/gear-app/tests/it/plan_staff_contract.rs`)
`restart_records_a_lanes_request`: `- restart heat` is Ok and `engine.restart.p:heat` is
`{"at":1800000010}`; with `p:wire` Held, `- restart wire` is `BadTransition` and leaves no
`engine.restart.p:wire` meta.

## Out of scope / note
- The acceptance names `run(...)` for both applies, but that helper always sends `base=0`; the second apply
  would be refused `StaleVersion` before reaching the seat check. The test uses a local `run_at` (the same
  helper the neighbouring `retry_puts_a_parked_lane_back_in_the_queue` test defines) that sends the store's
  current plan version, so the seat check is what is exercised.
- The engine's `restart_reviewers` step (release.rs) is unchanged: carrying a lane's restart out is not in
  this packet.
