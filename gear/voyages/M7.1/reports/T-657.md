# T-657 — the health reader lists orphaned processes in GEAR worktrees (reader side)

Candidate: f0b6c65f (branch hull/t657)

## What changed (crates/gear-engine/src/health.rs, plus the contract test's fake)

- `system_ps` now asks for `pid=,ppid=,pgid=,pcpu=,rss=,time=,etime=,comm=`;
  `PsProc` gained `age_s`, and `parse_ps` requires 8 fields, reads `age_s` from
  the etime column with `parse_cpu_time` (skipping a line that does not parse)
  and takes `command` from the rest of the line.
- New `Orphan` struct and `Reading.orphans` (doc: parentless processes under a
  GEAR worktree root, never touched).
- `Read::sample` takes `roots: &[String]`; `SystemRead`, `Silent` and the
  contract test's `FakeRead` updated; the direct sample call in
  `the_watch_costs_under_one_percent_of_one_core` passes `&[]`.
- New `orphans_in(table, fleet, roots, cwd_of)`: ppid 1, not in the fleet, cwd
  under a root (trailing ` (deleted)` stripped), and `cwd_of` is not called when
  `roots` is empty.
- `SystemRead::sample` calls it after the fleet is built with a production
  `cwd_of`: `proc_pidinfo(PROC_PIDVNODEPATHINFO)` on macOS, `/proc/<pid>/cwd` on
  Linux, `None` elsewhere — one syscall per ppid-1 process, no child process.
- New `worktree_roots(store, project)`: the parent directories of the values of
  `engine.worktree_path.<project>:*`, `engine.worktree_path_repo.<project>:*`
  and `engine.review_path.<project>:*`, skipping empty values, values equal to
  a repo path, `/` and empty parents; sorted and deduped. `check` computes it
  before the sample and passes it; nothing else in `check` changed.

## Tests

- `ps_lines_are_parsed_with_their_cpu_time` updated to 8 columns; asserts
  `age_s` 93784.0 and 300.0 and that `command` keeps its spaces.
- `orphans_are_parent_less_processes_under_a_root`: exactly pids 700 and 704,
  704's cwd without ` (deleted)`; with empty roots `cwd_of` is never called.
- `a_seat_tree_is_the_shell_and_its_children` literals gained `age_s: 0.0`.
- health_contract.rs: `FakeRead` ignores `roots`; the two `Reading` literals
  gained `..Default::default()`; the cost test passes `&[]`.

Check: `make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(health)'` — 15/15 pass. The
merge gate runs `make gate`.
