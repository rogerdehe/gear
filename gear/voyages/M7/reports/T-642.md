# T-642 — report

A queued remerge closes the task's merge-failed item too.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs` `close_lost`: resolves the open and waiting
  messages of `merge-failed:<task>` as well as `merge-lost:<task>`, with the
  same `remerge queued (O-n)` result. A failed retry still opens its own
  merge-failed item (the engine's path is unchanged).

## Tests

- `remerge_contract::a_queued_remerge_closes_the_merge_failed_item`: an open
  and a waiting `merge-failed:T-1` item both end Resolved with
  `remerge queued (O-…)` after `- remerge T-1`.
- `remerge_contract::a_remerge_of_a_candidate_not_on_the_integration_branch_queues_the_op`
  now asserts the failed item is resolved rather than left open.

`make check CRATE=gear-app TESTS='test(remerge)'` — 15 tests, 15 passed.

## Out of scope

- The engine's own merge-failed handling is unchanged: a failed retry opens a
  new item as today.
