# T-639 — report

The reclaim closes a workspace whose worktree is already gone.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/finished.rs` (the workspace loop of `reclaim`): when
  `herdr.worktree_remove` is Refused or Uncertain, the reclaim now calls
  `herdr.workspace_close` (T-640). Only when that is not Ok either is the
  workspace named `(could not close)`.

## Tests

- `finished::tests::a_workspace_whose_worktree_is_gone_is_closed`: with the
  fake Herdr, a workspace (`w1KM`) whose worktree remove is refused is closed
  by `workspace_close`, and the reclaim names nothing left (no item, `None`).
  The workspace list comes from a small `herdr` stub on PATH, since
  `project_workspaces` reads the real command.

`make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(finished::)'` — 16 tests, 16 passed.

## Out of scope

- The `(could not close)` path when `workspace_close` also fails is unchanged
  and not covered by a new test.
