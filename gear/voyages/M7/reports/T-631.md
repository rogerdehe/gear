# T-631 — an idle seat gets its waiting messages as text

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/doorbell.rs`
  - `DoorbellItem` carries `sender`, `body` and `needs_ack`.
  - `Ring` carries `inline`.
  - `rings` builds the inline text for a seat that is idle, holds no slot and
    has no `needs_ack` item: `GEAR: <n> message(s) for you.`, then per item a
    blank line, `[<id> from <sender>]` (`, urgent` when urgent) and the body,
    then the reply line, the no-task line for a lane with no task, and the
    captain's goal line. A joined ring (T-487) prefixes each header with its
    project and its reply line names `<project>:<id>`.
  - Over 4000 chars the text is cut at the last line break before 3800 and
    ends with `… cut here: run `<command>` to read all <n> item(s).`; such a
    ring is not inline.
  - A working seat (the at-once path), a seat holding a slot, and a ring with
    a `needs_ack` item keep the one-line text.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`: the ring inputs fill `sender`, `body` and
  `needs_ack` from the `MessageRow`.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`
  - `deliver_doorbell_now` keeps the one-line text (empty sender/body).
  - `intend_rings` writes `"inline": true` on the ring's own op only when the
    full text went out.
  - `check_op`/`aim`: an inline doorbell goes through the same composer
    held-text check as a delivery (an Enter instead of a second typing).
  - `confirm_pending_held`: a Submitted Doorbell op with `inline: true` is
    Confirmed at once, with one `ViewRecord` for its seat and generation
    (`shown_msgs` = its covers) and one for every other seat of the ring with
    its own covers, `ts` = now. `needs_ack` items stay open.

## Tests

`make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(doorbell) | test(ops::)'` passes
(107 tests). The whole `gear-engine` suite also passes (1156 tests), because
the tick-level tests that asserted the old one-line text for idle seats were
updated (`ring_contract`, `doorbell_isolation`, `day_contract`,
`feed_contract`, `slice1_messaging`, `session_dispatch_contract`).

New tests: `doorbell::tests` (inline bodies and headers, working seat,
needs_ack, no-task line, cut ring, joined ring, goal line) and
`ops::tests` (two short messages typed as one prompt and confirmed on submit
with a view listing both ids; a multi-line ring typed whole and not taken for
held text; a long ring cut and waiting for a view).

## Out of scope

- The held-text check matches the ring's first line (`GEAR: <n> message(s) for
  you.`). For a very short ring whose text is the last thing in the pane with
  no composer border below it, a previous ring with the same count could be
  read as held and get only the Enter. The test models the realistic tail
  (ring above the empty composer border); no such case was seen in the suite.
- The joined inline ring keeps input order rather than urgent-first (the old
  one-line joined text sorted urgent first); the packet says "per item in
  order".
