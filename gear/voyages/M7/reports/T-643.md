[F-4 1/7] The voyage label setting.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs`: `IssuesSection.voyage_label: Option<String>`
  and `Github.voyage_label: String` (resolved). The default is
  `{project}/{milestone}` when `[github]` is set; `voyage_label = ""` resolves
  to `""` (off). `github_issues` refuses a non-empty label that does not name
  `{milestone}` (field `github.issues.voyage_label`).
- `config::voyage_label(github, project, milestone) -> Option<String>`: `None`
  when off, otherwise the template with `{project}` and `{milestone}` filled
  in.
- `skills/gear/references/github.md`: one line documenting the setting.
