# T-644 — report

A member added to fix a red feature carrier keeps that carrier.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs` `feature_work`: `carrier_open` is also true for
  a stored carrier that is Done but has no merge
  (`crate::tasks::merged_at(tx, project, carrier)` is None). Such a carrier is
  kept and touched — its deps take the new member — instead of minting a fresh
  carrier. A Done carrier that merged keeps today's rule (a new member starts a
  fresh carrier over the open members).

## Tests

- `feature_plan_contract::a_member_added_to_a_red_unmerged_carrier_keeps_it`:
  a Done, unmerged carrier plus a new member creates no carrier; the carrier
  keeps its id and its deps hold the new member.
- `feature_plan_contract::a_member_added_after_the_feature_landed_gets_a_new_carrier`
  now merges the carrier (rather than only marking it Done), so it still
  asserts the fresh-carrier rule for a merged carrier.

`make check CRATE=gear-app TESTS='test(feature)'` — 22 tests, 22 passed.

## Out of scope

- The captain still merges the member and remerges the carrier
  (`- remerge T-<carrier>`); the apply only keeps the carrier and its deps.
