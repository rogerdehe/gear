# T-640 — report

Herdr can close a workspace for GEAR. No caller yet (T-639).

## What changed

- `crates/gear-herdr/src/lib.rs`: `HerdrAct::workspace_close(&mut self,
  workspace_id: &str) -> Outcome<()>`, documented as Herdr `workspace.close`
  (the call behind `herdr workspace close <id>`). `SocketHerdr` implements it
  over the socket call `workspace.close` with `{"workspace_id": id}`; a
  protocol refusal goes through `outcome_from_error` (`workspace_not_found` is
  already in the refused codes), anything else is `Uncertain`.
- `crates/gear-herdr/src/fake.rs`: `Call::WorkspaceClose { workspace }` and
  `FakeHerdr::workspace_close`: removes the workspace's panes and its worktree
  entry, records the call, and honours `inject_next`. An unknown workspace is
  `Refused { workspace_not_found }`.

## Tests

- `fake::tests::workspace_close_removes_the_workspace_and_records_the_call`:
  the fake removes the workspace's pane and worktree, records the call, and
  refuses a second close.
- `tests/it/workspace_close_contract.rs::the_real_client_sends_workspace_close_with_the_workspace_id`:
  a fake Herdr socket answers the protocol check (ping, protocol 22) and
  captures the request; the real client sends `workspace.close` with
  `{"workspace_id": "w1KM"}`.
