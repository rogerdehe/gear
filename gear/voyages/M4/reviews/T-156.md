Thus clicking the visible Graph tab does nothing and lane clicks select the wrong row. Breaks the v0.4.1 prototype frame/keys requirement and STABLE normal terminal usability; F2 remains unresolved and masks F1's corrected logical coordinates. Fix: in `screen::draw`, write the terminal-sized canvas without a trailing newline (about 1–3 lines), retaining flush. Verify the public PTY output with terminal cursor/scroll semantics, keeping title on row 1, tabs on row 4, footer on row 24, and clicking those visible positions.

F8 [P1, blocking] Scrolling discards off-screen rows before translating them into the viewport — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:219`, `crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs:1075`. The loop breaks when the original `first + index` reaches `last`, before subtracting `scroll`. On an 80x24 frame only indices 0–15 can ever be drawn; selecting index 20 moves some earlier rows upward but never draws the selected task. The independent 30-task graph reproduces this. Row-click handling also ignores the scroll offset, so clicking a visible scrolled row selects an earlier task. Breaks v0.4.1's graph selection/Enter behavior and STABLE use on a normal voyage larger than the viewport. Fix: iterate from the scroll offset and bound the resulting screen row, rather than bounding the original row; share the viewport offset with mouse hit testing and reject clicks outside the visible data region (about 20–30 lines in `frame` and `run_with`). Verify a graph longer than the viewport: repeated Down keeps the selected last task visible, and clicking a displayed row then Enter opens that displayed task.

F3 [P1, blocking] Panels clip their right border and can overwrite the footer — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:562`, `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:574`, `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:577`. With inner width 20 on an 80-column canvas, `left` is 59 but the top/bottom border has 22 cells, so its right edge lands outside the canvas; the body format is wider still. The independent panel output has no right border. When the panel reaches the bottom, its bottom border is drawn on `height - 1`, replacing the footer's quit hint; the reproduced last line ends in `└────────────────────` instead of `q quit`. Breaks the requested overlay/frame and STABLE normal panel usability; F3 remains unresolved despite the corrected compositing and any-key close. Fix: define one total box width including borders, calculate `left` from that width, pad/clip each interior row to exactly the interior width, and reserve the footer row when sizing the box and placing its bottom border (about 15–25 lines in `draw_panel`). Verify both a short panel and a terminal-height panel at 80x24 retain both borders and the complete footer.

F6 [P2, blocking] The PTY reader still returns partial frames rather than waiting for the footer — `crates/gear-board/tests/it/board_contract.rs:703`. `Frames::wait` checks only a new clear-screen prefix and whether the current partial frame contains the requested word; neither it nor `last_frame` checks a footer boundary. `next_without` can return as soon as the clear prefix arrives, before any body. The independent pipe reproduction containing only the clear prefix and `LANES` returned successfully without `q quit`. This violates the explicit poll-until-footer acceptance contract and leaves the new assertions vulnerable to output chunking; F6 is unresolved. Fix: buffer and extract complete frames terminated by the footer line, evaluate positive/negative predicates only on those frames, and make timeout fail rather than return an incomplete or unmatched frame (about 20–30 lines in `Frames`). Keep poll(POLLIN), no sleeps, and add a deterministic split-write check proving a prefix/body without the footer is never accepted. The existing actions must then assert within each returned complete frame and finish under 10 s.
