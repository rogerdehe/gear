# T-215 — retired lanes are not in the time report

Task: [v0.4.2] Retired lanes are not in the time report
(`ops/reviews/T-171.md` round 2, F10).

Lane: mast · Contract: v1 · Attempt: 1

## What was wrong

`gear report voyage` and `gear report time --voyage` collect the lane seats
of the project in `gear_app::time::work_of` and use them twice: as the
lines of the report, and as the denominator of the fleet stop (the wall
time during which **every** lane is parked for the quota at once).

A lane that was retired before the voyage was counted anyway. Two things
were wrong with that:

- the retired lane showed as a line of the voyage's report, although it
  did not work in the voyage at all;
- the retired lane sat in the denominator of the fleet stop, so a fleet
  that was stopped for the quota looked as if it was working: the stop
  was never counted, because a lane without a pause holds the fleet from
  ever being stopped.

## The fix

`crates/gear-app/src/time.rs`, in `work_of`:

- the lane seats are collected, and a seat is one of the fleet's lanes when
  it is **not retired**, or when it has a round or a pause **inside the
  voyage's window** (the departure until now; an open round or pause
  reaches now);
- the lanes that count are the ones that get a line of the report, and
  their number is the denominator of the fleet stop.

A lane retired during the voyage, but which worked in it, is still one of
the fleet's lanes. A lane retired before the voyage, without work in it,
is neither a line nor a part of the denominator.

## The tests

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/retired_lane_contract.rs` (new, this lane):

- the fixture of the review: heat and keel, keel retired at T0, the voyage
  departs at T0+60 with its only task on heat, heat's quota pause is open
  from the departure, the report is at T0+3660. The fleet is stopped for
  `1h00m` (1 stop), the elapsed time without the quota stops is `0h00m`,
  and there is no keel line;
- a lane retired during the voyage, after a round, still shows, with its
  work of the voyage.
