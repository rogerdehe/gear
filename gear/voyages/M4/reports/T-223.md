# T-223 hand-ins first after a direction result

The tick of `start_reviews` now rechecks the hand-ins after `finish_direction_results` has consumed a direction result. A hand-in that waited behind its own direction check takes the seat the direction check held, and no direction check starts in that tick (design §14: hand-ins come first). The hand-in loop is one function (`start_hand_in_reviews`), called for the hand-ins of the tick and for the hand-ins that became eligible when the direction result freed their seat; a task that waits is named once in the report.

Validation: the regression `a_hand_in_takes_the_seat_a_direction_result_frees` (one reviewer slot; the direction check of T-1 finishes with `ok` while T-1 is submitted; a second Started task T-2 has a new push) shows the hand-in taking the seat and no direction check for T-2; the full gate is reported at hand-in.
