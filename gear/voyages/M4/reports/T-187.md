# T-187 — a verdict that settles a task closes its review item

Scope: a `# Verdicts` line in the plan settles the attempt; the review item of
that attempt stayed open, so the captain had to resolve it by hand (M-2412,
after settling T-135 in the plan).

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs`** — the verdict loop of the apply closes the
review items of the attempt it settles: after `tasks::settle`, the open
messages of kind `review` for that task and attempt are resolved with the
result `settled in plan <version>` (the version the apply writes). The items
of the other attempts are left alone.

**`crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs`** — `resolve_review_items` is now public: it
is the helper that closes the review items of an attempt and leaves the
captain one silent line in the thread (the review's own settlement path uses
it since R-67).

## Tests

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/plan_verdicts_contract.rs`:

- `a_verdict_closes_the_review_item_of_that_attempt` — a review item for
  T-1/1 and one for T-2/1 in the captain's inbox; the verdict of T-2 (a fail)
  closes the item of T-2 with the result `settled in plan 2`, and the item of
  T-1 stays open.

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/verdict_item_contract.rs`:

- `after_the_plan_settles_it_resolving_works` became
  `after_the_plan_settles_it_the_item_is_closed`: it asserted the old
  behaviour (the item stayed open and a reply resolved it), which T-187
  changes — the plan that settles the attempt closes the item with it, and a
  reply to it is refused because it is handled.

## Open questions

The closed item keeps the plan's version as its result (`settled in plan 2`)
and the captain gets one silent line in the same thread, as every other
settlement of a review item does.

## Out of scope

The items of a *merge* failure and the other captain decisions: they are
settled by their own paths (`tasks::settle_review` and the reply).
