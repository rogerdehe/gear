# T-198: direction check hand-in

`gear send --kind result --task T-n --attempt a --direction "<text>"` accepts a running direction job from its bound reviewer and records the checked SHA and direction flag on the result. It leaves the attempt, task, and verdict unchanged. Other seats and stale or mismatched jobs are refused; ordinary result hand-ins retain their existing checks and behavior.

The CLI reads direction text from the option without invoking the lane hand-in gate or printing its completion message. Request replay remains idempotent. Focused CLI tests cover parsing, authorization, metadata, unchanged state, replay, and ordinary reviewer-result rejection. `scripts/test.sh` passed all 1,301 tests (build 287 seconds, suite 146 seconds).
