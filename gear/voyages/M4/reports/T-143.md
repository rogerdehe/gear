# T-143 — Warm reviewer seats: design §3.6 in release.rs and review.rs

Scope: T-143 — design §3.6 (the reviewer seats keep, release and quota rules):
after its verdict a reviewer seat goes to `Standby` with its job binding
cleared; each tick, with W = the lane seats whose attempt is `Started`, the
Standby reviewers beyond W are released (the idle longest first, all of them
at once when W = 0); the temps quota counts `Active`, `Standby` and `Starting`
only; a `Starting` reviewer whose job is gone is cancelled.

Branch: hull/t143 (from origin/dev; the last commit is the hand-in)

Files:
- `crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs` — `clear_seat_task`: the job binding of a
  seat ends (`seats.task_id = NULL`), with its round-trip test (the binding is
  cleared, the seat keeps everything else, a missing seat is `NoTarget`).
- `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` — `standby_after_verdict`: the reviewers
  whose verdict is in go to `Standby` (the agent stays alive, the pane stays
  open, the job binding ends) and `handed_a_verdict`: the verdict of the
  attempt the seat judged, written by that seat, is what counts. The quota:
  `occupies_quota` counts `Active`, `Standby` and `Starting`; `has_free_slot`
  counts a parked (`Unknown`) seat only while its agent still runs (B02-04),
  so the count is a fact and not a guess.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs` — the release step of the tick
  (`release_finished_reviews`): the standby step comes first, then a reviewer
  whose job is gone leaves, a `Starting` reviewer whose job is gone is
  cancelled (`cancel_start`: its start op is voided, its pane is closed, its
  binding ends, the seat is `Released`), a `Releasing` seat is finished with or
  without a job (the captain's `- release <reviewer>` ends there too), and the
  Standby reviewers beyond W are released (`release_idle_standby`,
  `working_lanes`). `release_one` closes the pane of a standby reviewer like
  any other release; the worktree stays.

The rules in words:
- A verdict ends the **job**, not the seat: the reviewer waits for the next
  one. A reviewer whose job is gone without a verdict (the task was cancelled,
  a newer attempt was handed in) still leaves.
- W is the number of lane seats with an attempt in `Started`: a lane that
  waits or that hands in does not count — no review of its work is coming.
- The idle longest goes first: the seat that has been in `Standby` since the
  longest.
- A released standby reviewer frees its seat in two steps (`Standby` →
  `Releasing` → `Released`), so its pane closes on the tick it is released and
  the seat is free on the next one — the same two steps as any other release,
  so a restart never closes a pane twice.

Tests (engine unit tests):
- `a_verdict_puts_the_reviewer_on_standby_with_its_pane_open` — after its
  verdict the seat is `Standby`, its task binding is gone, its pane is open
  and its binding is still active.
- `with_one_lane_working_the_longest_waiting_standby_reviewer_is_released` —
  with W = 1 and two Standby reviewers, the one that waits longest is released
  and the other stays.
- `with_no_lane_working_every_standby_reviewer_is_released` — with W = 0 both
  are released.
- `a_starting_reviewer_whose_job_is_gone_is_cancelled` and
  `a_starting_reviewer_whose_job_is_alive_keeps_starting` — the cancel of a
  `Starting` seat (B03-05) and the one that goes on.
- `a_standby_reviewer_holds_a_temp_slot` — the quota count: a standby
  reviewer holds a slot like an active one, a released one holds none, and a
  review does not pass the quota.
- The test that expected a released reviewer after a verdict now expects a
  standby one (`a_reviewer_whose_task_is_still_under_review_is_left_alone`),
  and the test that expected a starting reviewer never to be released now
  covers the queued reviewer only (`a_queued_reviewer_is_never_released`).
