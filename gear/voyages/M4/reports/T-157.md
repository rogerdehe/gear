# T-157 — the board's view 2 (Graph), attempt 1

Scope: T-157 — the graph view of the board as the prototype draws it (design
§8.1) and the task panel that `Enter` opens on a row.

Branch: `deck/t157` (code @ the hand-in commit; `origin/dev` merged before
verification)

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs`** (new module) — the graph of the work and
the task panel:

```rust
pub enum GraphStage { Queued, Working, Review, Merging, Done }
pub struct GraphTask { id, lane, stage, title, est_min, actual_min, rounds, deps, finished }
pub fn tasks(tx: &Tx, project: &str, now: Ts) -> GearResult<Vec<GraphTask>>;
pub fn lanes(tx: &Tx, project: &str) -> GearResult<Vec<String>>;
pub fn lines(tasks: &[GraphTask], lanes: &[String], selected: Option<usize>) -> Vec<String>;
pub fn panel(tx: &Tx, project: &str, task: &str, now: Ts) -> GearResult<Vec<String>>;
```

The layout is the prototype's, column for column:

- the head of the columns: `dev` at 2, `rev` at 8, one column per lane from 14
  (six apart);
- one row per task, in the prototype's order: the queued first, then the
  work, then the reviews, then the merged ones with the newest first;
- the symbols: `○` queued, `◉` working, `◎` in review (in the review column,
  with the line that reaches the lane's column), `●` merged;
- a merged task draws the line of the merge from `dev` to its lane, and the
  lanes it crosses are marked `┼`;
- `✓` in the review column of a merged task, `↺` next to it when the task was
  reworked;
- the text of the row: the id, the lane, the title, and the info column
  (`merged 06:02 · 44/40m`, `working 38/60m`, `in review`, `merging`,
  `queued · 35m`);
- the marks at the end of the row: `needs T-136`, and with a row selected
  `◂ needed by T-141` (the selected task needs this one) and `▸ waits for
  T-141` (this task waits for the selected one);
- the legend at the end: `○ queued  ◉ working  ◎ in review (rev column)  ↺
  reworked  ✓● reviewed and merged into dev   select a row: its deps light up`.

The **task panel** (`Enter` on a row) shows the task with its lane and its
stage, its estimate and its actual time, its **rounds** (from the rounds of
the store when there are any, else from the attempts: the dev round, the
rework round and the review with its verdict), what it **depends on** and
what **needs it**.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — the board carries the graph
(`ProjectBoard::graph` and `graph_lanes`) and the new `View::Graph` draws
it; the `d` key cycles over the three views.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/graph_contract.rs` — the prototype's fake data in
a store (the tasks of the four lanes, their dependencies, the rounds and the
merges):

- the graph has the columns of the prototype (`dev`, `rev`, one column per
  lane) and the rows in the prototype's order;
- a merged task draws the line of the merge (`●` in `dev`, the line to its
  lane, the `┼` crossings, `✓` in the review column) and a reworked task
  carries the `↺`;
- a task in review draws the line of the review (`◎` in the review column and
  the line that reaches its lane);
- the dependencies are marked (`needs T-142`), and with a row selected the
  marks of the prototype (`◂ needed by`, `▸ waits for`) light up;
- the task panel lists the rounds (the attempts, and the rounds of the store
  when they are there), the estimate and the actual time, what the task
  depends on and what needs it.
