# T-184 — the captain watches the clock (design §10.7 row B10)

Scope: besides the 25/50/75% eta stations, a **course station every 60 min
of voyage time**. Its text gives the elapsed time / eta, the time left, the
per-lane projection (row B4) and the lane that sets the finish, and the
tasks left; it is urgent when the projected finish is past the eta; it is
answered in `# Decisions` like any station.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/station.rs`**

- **The course station.** `checkpoints()` crosses a course station every
  60 min of voyage time (`(now - departure) / 3600`), for a voyage that is
  still running (`left > 0`). The hour counter is a meta
  (`{station:project:M}:course`), so a late engine tick does not replay the
  hours it slept through: the first check after a long sleep rings **one**
  station labelled with the range it missed (`60–180 min`), like the
  `3–6 done` milestones do.
- **The label.** The course station joins the other labels of the tick in
  the one snapshot of the station (`[60 min, 25% eta, 3 done]`): the design
  says simultaneous or missed checkpoints coalesce into one immutable
  snapshot per voyage/tick, so a course station and an eta station at the
  same minute are one station, not two.
- **The text.** The station line now reads

  ```text
  Station M2 [60 min] · elapsed 60m / eta 12h · time left 660m · pace 2.00x ·
  projected finish 260m from departure · tasks left 3 (100m) · lanes heat 200m ·
  slowest lane heat 200m
  ```

  - `time left` is the time left **until the eta** (the clock the captain
    watches); the work that is left stays in `tasks left N (minutes)`;
  - `lanes` is the per-lane projection of row B4: every lane's own pace and
    what it has left (`-` when a lane has no measured pace — an unknown is
    never a zero);
  - `slowest lane` is the lane that sets the finish, named with its minutes
    (as before).
- **Urgent.** A station is urgent when the projected finish is past the eta
  (`projected_min * 60 > eta`). An unknown eta or an unknown projection
  never makes a station urgent.

**`crates/gear-app/tests/it/station_contract.rs`**

- `a_course_station_every_hour_answers_like_any_station`: with a fake
  clock, one station at 60 min and one at 120 min (none in between), the
  text with elapsed / eta, the time left, the per-lane projection and the
  lane that sets it, and the tasks left; a `# Decisions` line
  (`- station M-n: time-box …`) closes it, and it names exactly one item.
- `a_course_station_is_urgent_when_the_projection_is_past_the_eta`: the
  same fixture with a 1 h eta and a 240 m projection — the station is urgent.
- The existing station tests follow the new text: the labels of the
  snapshot (`[60 min, 25% eta]`), the exact station line, the 4 h course
  station (`240 min`), and the unknown eta (an unknown eta rings no eta
  station; the course station does not need one).

**`crates/gear-engine/tests/it/station_contract.rs`** — the home page of
the captain shows the coalesced snapshot `Station M2 [60 min, 25% eta]`.

## What did not change

- The station is still a system item of the captain in the thread
  `station:<milestone>`; it is still closed **only** by a plan decision
  (`- station <id>: keep course|time-box|cut|move|land`), never by a reply.
- An unanswered station is rung again after `timing.captain_item_stale_min`
  as before (the stale captain item turns an open system item urgent).
- The 25/50/75% eta stations and the `3 done` milestones keep their rules.

## Friction

- The branch base: the home worktree `home/mast` was left on `mast/t163`
  (T-163 is still in `verifying`, its work is not on `dev` yet). The branch
  `mast/t184` was created from that head, so the candidate carries T-163's
  commits; a rebase onto `origin/dev` needs a force push, which the
  harness refuses ("push your task branch normally"). `origin/dev` was
  merged into `mast/t184` instead, so the branch holds the tip of `dev`
  and the diff `dev..candidate` shows T-163's work next to mine (the two
  `gear-cli` variants, `plan show` and `plan check`, had to be kept
  together in the merge).
- The first full-suite run failed under the load of the other lanes
  (23 failed, 19 exec failed, no `FAIL` line in the log of the next run):
  the suite is flaky under load, not broken by this change. The two runs
  after it (and the run after the merge of `origin/dev`) are green:
  1337 tests, 0 failed.
- The first build of `gear-app` took 3 m 44 s under that load; `cargo
  clippy` did not finish inside the runner's budget (advisory only).
