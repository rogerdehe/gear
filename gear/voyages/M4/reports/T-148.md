# T-148 — the quota of the providers (design §9.2)

Scope: The screen is not the source of quota facts. A quota marker or a provider error on the screen is only a signal: the engine reads the provider's API (`gear_usage::read_quota`) with the key the config names (`[[quota.sources]]`, §9.1) and the reading decides — a spent window parks the lane until the window's reset plus a minute (the round's clock paused, a `quota` pause row opened and closed, `Continue T-n: your quota is back.` in the same session at the reset), quota left is not a quota stop (the lane waits 5 min and continues; the second time in a row is a captain item), and a source that cannot be read parks the lane for today's `quota_reset_h` hours. Every source is read every 5 min, the last reading is kept in the meta `quota.reading.<provider>`, and a reading older than 15 min is marked stale.

Files: `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs` — the `[[quota.sources]]` parse of §9.1 (provider, reader, key_file, key_path) and a config finding for a reader the engine does not know (the captain's decision M-2781: the parse moved from T-147 to this task). `crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` — the sources and their readings (§9.1), the three branches of §9.2, the quota pause rows (§5.4), the round clock's pauses (§5.1), the `Continue` delivery and the captain items. `crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` — the sources parse and the finding for an unknown reader. `crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` (tests) — the three branches, the stale reading and the rework's tests, with a fake reader.

## Attempt 2 — the rework of the review of attempt 1 (F1–F4)

**F1 — a new signal reads the source at once.** The reading of the periodic
read (up to a minute old) could decide a quota stop that began after it: the
lane took the five-minute provider-error branch instead of parking to the
real reset. The engine now keeps the start of the signal's episode
(`engine.quota_signal.<seat>`): a reading taken **before** the episode began
never decides, and the read of the episode is shared by the confirmation
ticks (and by the other seats of the same provider). The periodic caching
stays a separate thing.

**F2 — an unreadable source is read every five minutes.** The periodic read
was due five minutes after the last *successful* read, so an outage left the
source due on every tick: a curl of up to 15 s on every tick, delaying the
rest of the engine. The last **attempt** is now kept (`quota.attempt.<provider>`),
success or failure, and the next read is due five minutes after it. The last
good reading is kept as it was and marked stale by its own age.

**F3 — a park and a wake are one transaction each.** The park wrote the seat
state and the pause row, then the deadline of the wait: a restart between
them left a parked lane that never woke up. The wake closed the pause, added
the round's pause, set the seat active, delivered the Continue and cleared the
wait — one write at a time: a restart in the middle left a lane awake without
its Continue, or paused twice. Both are one transaction now (the store's own
`Tx` APIs), and the accounting errors are propagated instead of discarded.

**F4 — a codex source reads the session of the reviewer.** The reader of
`codex` needs no API key: it reads the rollout of the reviewer's session. The
engine read a key file that does not exist and passed no session, so a
configured codex source could never produce a reading. The reader is now given
the session of the project's codex reviewer (its binding) and the key path is
used for the HTTP readers only.

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` (unit tests, a fake reader):

- `a_spent_window_parks_the_lane_until_the_reset` — the three branches'
  first one: the lane parks until the reset plus a minute, the pause row is
  open, the round's clock pauses, the Continue comes in the same session; the
  park carries its deadline and the wake clears it with its Continue (F3);
- `a_new_signal_reads_the_source_again` — the provider runs out between the
  periodic read and the signal: the signal reads the source again and the
  lane parks to the real reset; the confirmation shares that read (F1);
- `an_unreadable_source_is_read_every_five_minutes` — a failing reader is
  asked once at t=0 and once at t=300, not at t=4 and t=8 (F2);
- `a_codex_source_reads_the_session_of_the_reviewer` — a codex source without
  a key file is read with the session of the codex reviewer and its reading is
  kept (F4);
- `quota_left_waits_five_minutes_then_continues` — the second branch: 5 min,
  then the Continue, and the second time in a row a captain item;
- `an_unreadable_source_parks_for_the_reset_window` — the third branch;
- `a_stale_reading_is_marked` — a reading older than 15 min is marked.

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` — the sources parse and the
finding for an unknown reader.
