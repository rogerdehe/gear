# T-161: declared waits

A blocked message records its task wait and opens the seat's wait pause atomically. A contract change clears the wait and closes that pause in the same transaction. The engine suppresses pending nudges, stale overrun doorbells and stall flags while the lane waits; it counts the union of quota and wait pauses into the round.

The wake uses the awaited attempt's recorded merge time and candidate, following the captain's decision. Once the merge lands, the engine closes the pause at that time, clears `wait_on` and creates one urgent instruction with the requested text. The standard doorbell reaches the lane in its existing session. A Done task without a recorded merge, or a merge timestamp in the future, does not wake it.
