# T-193 — A review's friction clock is its own

Scope: T-193 (T-168 round 2 F1) — in `crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs`,
`check_friction` charges a review from the review round's start, never from
the writer attempt's `sent_at`. The dev and rework rounds keep the attempt's
clock.

Branch: keel/t193 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before the
final verification)

## What was built

`crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs`:

- The review branch of `check_friction` used the **writer attempt's**
  `sent_at` as the start of the review (the attempt row belongs to the
  writer; the review's delivery confirmation is not in it). The reviewer was
  therefore charged for the writer's hour: a review that started an hour
  after the writer's check-in and finished five minutes later was refused
  with `FRICTION_REQUIRED`, and its report was stored as required.
- The review is now charged from the **review round's start** — the row the
  engine writes for the review round (`rounds.start_at`), the reviewer's own
  clock. The writer's attempt keeps the writer's times, and the dev and
  rework rounds keep the attempt's clock as before.

## The tests

`crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs` (the CLI's own tests, where the §6.2
regressions live):

- `a_review_is_charged_from_the_reviewers_own_clock` — the writer checks in
  at T0 and its packet goes out at T0+10; the review round starts at T0+3600;
  the verdict at T0+3900 is accepted and no report is stored. The writer's
  attempt still carries the writer's times only (`sent_at` = T0+10,
  `started` = T0): the test never writes a review time into the writer
  attempt.
- `a_review_over_its_own_budget_needs_a_friction_report` — the same review
  round, but the verdict comes 1801 s after it started: the verdict is
  refused without a report, and with `--friction` it is stored with
  `required = true` and the role `review`.

The first test is the regression: with the old code the review's clock was
the writer's `sent_at` (T0+10), so the verdict at T0+3900 was 65 min old —
over the 30-minute review budget — and the hand-in was refused.
