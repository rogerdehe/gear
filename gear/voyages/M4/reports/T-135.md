# T-135 — schema v6 and the M4 store API, attempt 1

Scope: T-135 — the store of schema v6 (design §1) with the store API of §13.1,
including the budget of §5.1 and the pauses of §5.4.

Branch: `deck/t135` (code @ the hand-in commit; `origin/dev` merged before
verification, the gate green on the merged result)

## What changed

**`crates/gear-store/src/schema.sql`** — the schema of v6:
- `tasks` gains `repo`, `base`, `gate` (§1.1: the task keeps its own repo, the
  integration branch and the gate) and `rework_estimate_min` (§5.1);
- `attempts` gains `session_ref`, `session_cli`, `session_action` and `sent_at`
  (§3: which session the attempt ran in, what the engine did before the
  packet, and when the packet was sent);
- `messages` gains `meta` (§5.2: the structured fields of a progress answer);
- four new tables: `rounds` (§4: the rounds and their tokens), `seat_pauses`
  (§5.4: every stretch a seat could not work), `frictions` (§6) and
  `decisions` (§7: the captain's decision log).

**`crates/gear-store/src/migrate.rs`** — `SCHEMA_VERSION` is 6 and the step
`5 → 6` runs: it applies the schema (the new tables) and adds the nine new
columns, each nullable and each only when the table does not have it yet. A
migrated store keeps every row it had, and **every new column is NULL**: the
store cannot read the project config, so `tasks.repo/base/gate` stay NULL for
the old tasks and their readers fall back to the lane's current config.

**`crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`** — the store API of §13.1:
- `set_attempt_session`, `set_attempt_action`, `set_attempt_sent`;
- `insert_round` (one round per key: a second one of the same key fails),
  `open_round_of_seat`, `close_round`, `add_round_session` (no duplicates),
  `add_round_pause`, `set_round_active`, `set_round_tokens`, `rounds_of_task`,
  `rounds_of_project`;
- `insert_friction` (returns `F-<n>`), `open_frictions`,
  `frictions_of_project`, `close_friction`;
- `insert_decision_if_new` (false when the key is already there),
  `decisions_of`;
- `open_pause`, `close_pause`, `pauses_of_project`;
- `set_task_rework_estimate`, `set_task_wait`, `clear_task_wait`;
- `round_budget_min` — §5.1 in one place: a dev round takes the task's
  `estimate_min`; a rework round takes the estimate of the fail verdict, or
  `max(10, ceil(estimate_min / 2))`; a review round takes 1.5 × the median
  active time of the last ten finished review rounds of the same model (at
  least 10 min), and 30 min while fewer than three such rounds exist.
- the row types of §13.1: `RoundKey`, `NewRound`, `RoundTokens`, `RoundRow`,
  `NewFriction`, `FrictionRow`, `NewDecision`, `DecisionRow`, `PauseRow`; and
  the new columns on `TaskRow`, `AttemptRow`, `MessageRow` and `NewMessage`.
  `insert_task` and `insert_message` write the new columns; `update_task`
  writes them too, and `update_attempt` does not touch the session columns —
  they have their own writers, like the step times.

**The other crates** — the three row types grew fields, so every literal of
them in the workspace names the new fields (`None`). No behaviour changed
there: 78 files, and outside `gear-store` the diff is nothing but the added
`None` fields.

**`crates/gear-store/tests/it/v6_contract.rs`** — the tests of the
acceptance: a v5 store migrates to v6 with the new columns NULL; the budget of
a dev round, of a rework round with and without `rework_estimate_min`, and of
a review round with 0 and with 5 earlier rounds; every function of §13.1
round-trips; `insert_round` twice fails; `insert_decision_if_new` returns
false the second time; `insert_friction` returns `F-1` then `F-2`.

## Open questions

One case the design does not cover: the budget of a **dev round whose task has
no `estimate_min`**. §5.1 gives the estimate of the task; a task without one
has nothing to read. The store falls back to 30 min (the same default a
review round without measurements gets, `DEFAULT_ROUND_BUDGET_MIN`). A budget
of 0 would fire the overrun question at once, so it is not that. If the
captain wants another number, it is one constant in `gear-store`.

## Out of scope

- The writers of the new columns: the engine (hull, keel) and the plan
  (mast) fill them in their own tasks (§1.1, §3.2, §5.1, §5.3).
- `gear-usage` (§13.2), the profile fields (§2, §13.3) and the board's idle
  reasons (§13.6): the other M4 tasks of the deck lane.
- The tokens of a round are read by the engine's tick (§4.3-4.4); the store
  only holds them.
