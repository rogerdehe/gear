# T-171 — the voyage report and the landing

Scope: design §7.5 (`gear report voyage [--json] [--all]` and the landing
item), §5.4 (the work and the waiting time of a voyage) and §4.5 (the token
and cost columns of `gear report time`).

Branch: mast/t171 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before the
final verification)

## What landed

### `gear report voyage` — the captain's landing report (design §7.5)

A new command in `crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs`
(`ReportOp::Voyage { voyage, all }`); the report itself is built and rendered
by `crates/gear-app/src/voyage.rs` (a new module of this lane). It is
captain-only and read-only: it starts no engine and writes nothing.

The six parts, in the order §7.5 gives them:

1. **The voyage's own report lines** — today's `VoyageReport::lines` (the
   board's five lines: the title and the done sentence, the elapsed and the
   eta, the pace and the projected finish, the scope, the rework rounds and
   the slowest task).
2. **The `done:` sentence, clause by clause** — the sentence is a numbered
   list in one line (`(1) …; (2) …`), so it is split at its numbers and each
   clause is printed on its own line. The captain marks the clauses met or not
   met in its own landing decision lines.
3. **Time and tokens** — the wall clock of the voyage and the work and
   waiting time per lane (§5.4, below), the tokens and the cost of the
   voyage's tasks, and the five tasks with the highest actual/estimate.
4. **The ★ decisions in full; the others as one count per kind** — the ★
   decision is printed with its date, its why, its else and its changes; the
   decisions that are not ★ are counted per kind (`others: estimate 2`).
   `--all` prints every decision, not only the ★ ones.
5. **The friction reports with their outcomes** — an open report is marked
   `open`, a closed one carries the captain's outcome.
6. **Rework** — the tasks that took more than one round.

`--json` prints the same report as JSON (the six parts as fields).

### §5.4 — the work and the waiting time

`crates/gear-app/src/time.rs` gained the measurements of §5.4:

- per lane: **work** = the sum of the lane's rounds' `active_s` (the
  reviewer's round is the reviewer's work, not the lane's), **quota wait** and
  **declared wait** = the sums of the lane's pauses (kind `quota` and kind
  `wait`), **idle** = the rest of the voyage's elapsed time;
- per voyage: **elapsed** = departure → now, **fleet stopped by quota** = the
  wall time during which the quota pauses overlap (the union of the overlaps,
  counted once), **elapsed without quota stops** = elapsed − fleet stopped.

Both lines are printed, exactly as §5.4 shows them:

```
Voyage M4 · elapsed 68h00m · fleet stopped by quota 20h10m (4 stops) · elapsed without quota stops 47h50m
lane hull · work 31h20m · quota wait 20h10m · declared wait 0h40m · idle 15h50m
```

`gear report voyage` prints them (part 3) and `gear report time --voyage`
prints them too, below the time report.

### §4.5 — the token and cost columns of `gear report time`

`gear report time` shows the tokens and the cost of every task and of every
round (`crates/gear-app/src/time.rs`): the round's own tokens come from the
store's `rounds` row (the lane's dev round for attempt 1, its rework round
for attempt n ≥ 2), and the task's tokens are their sum. The cost shows
where it came from (`agent`, `prices` or `none`) in the report's JSON.

### The landing item (design §7.5)

`crates/gear-app/src/station.rs` writes the landing item the way §7.5 gives
it — the three steps (the report first, the user before the lanes, the
release last) and the closing `station <id>: land`:

```
Voyage M4 is ready to land: all 12 tasks are done (elapsed 68h00m / eta 12h).
1. Run `gear report voyage` and write the voyage report into # Decisions.
2. Review it with the user first: the report, the ★ decisions (only those), the friction reports that led to a change. Write each correction as `- <date> user: <text>`.
3. Only then ask about reclaiming the lanes and the release.
Close this with `station <id>: land`.
```

## Attempt 2 — the review's F1–F8

The review of attempt 1 (`ops/reviews/T-171.md`) failed it with eight
blocking findings. Each is fixed with the smallest fix the finding names,
plus the test the finding names. (F9 — the open rounds — is not mine; it
moved to a task on hull.)

- **F1 — the landing's default voyage.** `gear report voyage` without a name
  now asks `gear_app::plan::current_voyage` (the plan's own latest-departure
  rule) instead of picking by milestone order, so a finished voyage never
  hands the captain an older one. A project with no voyage still gets
  `NoTarget`.
- **F2 — the fleet is stopped only while every lane is paused.** The quota
  pauses keep their seat, and the stops are the stretches in which **all**
  the lanes are paused at once (the overlap of the lanes' pauses, not the
  union of one lane's).
- **F3 — a voyage measures its own work.** The rounds and pauses are filtered
  by the voyage's tasks, and the pauses are clipped to the voyage's window
  (its departure → now), so an earlier voyage's history on the same lane is
  not this voyage's work.
- **F4 — an open pause counts until now.** A quota park or a declared wait
  that has not ended ends at the report's clock, not at nothing.
- **F5 — the reviewer's round counts.** The tokens and the cost of a task
  now count every stored round of it, the reviewer's included (design §4.2:
  the review work belongs to the task it reviews). Each role is kept apart,
  and the reviewer's active time is still not the lane's work.
- **F6 — the text report prints each round.** Every stored round of a task
  is printed under the task's line with its attempt, its role, its tokens and
  its cost.
- **F7 — the voyage's tokens per lane.** The lanes carry their own tokens
  and cost of the voyage, and the report prints them next to the lane's
  work/wait row.
- **F8 — the voyage's own friction.** The friction reports are filtered
  through the requested voyage's tasks, so an earlier voyage's reports are
  not this voyage's.

## Notes for the reviewer

- `crates/gear-app/src/voyage.rs` is a new module of this lane (the lane
  owns `crates/gear-app/src/time.rs`, `station.rs` and the rest of the
  app's own paths); `crates/gear-app/src/lib.rs` gained the one line that
  declares it.
- `time::report` and `time::build` take the clock now: the elapsed time of
  a voyage and the work of its lanes are read at that moment. The callers
  (the CLI and the tests) pass it.
- The ★ decision's date, why, else and changes are the ones the captain
  wrote (the `decisions` table); the report shows them verbatim.
- The fleet stopped by the quota is the union of the quota pauses (the
  overlaps counted once), as §5.4 says.
