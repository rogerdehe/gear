# T-219 — A failed merge gate names the failing tests (contract v1)

Scope: T-219 — in `crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs`, where the gate fails, the
reason carries, before the tail, every line of the combined output that starts
(after spaces) with `FAIL`, `TIMEOUT` or `SIGSEGV`, or contains `panicked at`
or `error[` or `error:` (at most 20 such lines, in order); the 12-line tail
stays.

Branch: keel/t219 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before the
final verification)

## The bug

The reason of a failed merge was the last 12 lines of the gate's combined
output. A `cargo test` run that fails early and then walks through its slow
tests ends in timings (`test slow_39 ... ok (4.2s)`), so the captain's item
named nothing that failed — M4 06:55, T-171: the item showed only the last 12
lines, which were slow-test timings.

## The fix

- `crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs` — a new `failure_lines(text, max)`:
  it walks the lines of the combined output (stdout then stderr, as before)
  and keeps a line when it starts (after spaces) with `FAIL`, `TIMEOUT` or
  `SIGSEGV`, or when it holds `panicked at`, `error[` or `error:`. The lines
  keep their order, and at most `max` of them come back.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs` — where the gate fails, the reason is now
  `gate failed (<gate>):\n` + the failure lines (when there are any) + the
  12-line tail. The tail stays, and it stays last: the failure lines come
  before it. Nothing else about the failure changes — a clear reason (126,
  127) is still clear, a stuck one is still stuck, and the worktree still goes
  back to the integration branch.

The captain's item is written from that same reason (`tell_captain_failed`), so
the failing lines reach the captain's inbox without a second path.

## The tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs` (engine tests):

- `a_failing_gate_names_the_failing_tests_before_the_tail` — a fake gate whose
  output holds a `FAIL [t] the_broken_test` line far above 40 slow-test
  lines. The failed op's reason holds the FAIL line and the tail, and the
  FAIL line comes before the tail; the captain's item holds the FAIL line too.
- `the_failure_lines_are_the_lines_that_name_a_failure` — the lines that count
  and the lines that do not: `FAIL`, `TIMEOUT`, `SIGSEGV` (after spaces),
  `panicked at`, `error[`, `error:`; a plain test line does not count, and at
  most 20 lines come back.

## Verification

- `cargo test -p gear-engine merge::tests::a_failing_gate_names` → 1 passed.
- `cargo test -p gear-engine merge::tests::the_failure_lines` → 1 passed.
- `scripts/test.sh` → the whole workspace passes (1329 tests, 0 failed).
- `cargo clippy -p gear-engine --all-targets` → no new warning (the warnings
  in `merge.rs` are the ones that were already there).
- `git diff --check` → clean.

## Out of scope

- The tail stays 12 lines, and the failure lines are added before it (the
  packet says so).
- The number of failure lines (20) is the packet's; a gate that fails on
  everything names nothing in particular beyond that.
