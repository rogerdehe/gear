# T-162 — the imbalance item and the run-dry warning, each tick

Scope: design §8.4 (the imbalance item) and §10.7 row B3 (T-66: the warning
that a lane is one task from dry is checked every tick, not only at plan
apply), both in `crates/gear-engine/src/feed.rs`.

## What changed

**The imbalance item (§8.4).** When a lane is idle with `NoWork` (§8.2, read
through `gear_board::idle::idle_reason`) while another lane has two or more
tasks that have not started (ready or waiting for a dependency) and more than
60 minutes of estimates left, the captain gets **one** item for the project,
on the thread `balance`:

```text
mast has nothing left; deck has 7 tasks (315m) left: move some (lane ownership allowing) or plan more for mast.
```

The item is updated while the state lasts (never one item per pair of lanes,
never a repeated fact) and closed when the state ends. A lane in the
captain's tail is idle on purpose and is no imbalance.

**The one-task-from-dry warning (§10.7 row B3, T-66).** The plan apply tells
the captain that a lane has nothing to start after its current task (T-58);
the engine now checks the same fact **every tick** and tells it with the same
words: `lane heat has nothing to start after T-1 (1 queued behind deps: T-2)`.
The engine reads the lanes that are about to stand idle
(`gear_app::plan::idle_lanes`) — a lane that works on its last task and has
nothing startable behind it. A lane in that state is not "recovered" while it
is dry: the forecast alone (a task with time left) does not close the
warning. The warning is one item per state: the next tick does not repeat it.

## Attempt 2 — the rework of the review of attempt 1 (F1-F2)

**F1 — a resolved balance item was told again.** The imbalance item is now
backed by a durable incident marker in the engine's meta
(`feed-balance:<project>`): the idle lane and the lane it would take work
from. While the same imbalance lasts, nothing is reopened — the captain's
resolution stands; the marker is forgotten when the imbalance ends, so a
later imbalance is told again.

**F2 — the last-task warning masked the idle state.** Both facts had the
same severity, so a lane that stood idle after handing its last task in was
never reported. The warning about the work in flight is now given only while
the lane really works (it has an attempt in flight — `IdleLane::current`
counts a task in review too) and sits below the idle severity: when the lane
hands its last task in, a fresh item says `lane heat is IDLE`, and the next
tick is silent.

  `lane heat has nothing to start after T-1 (1 queued behind deps: T-2)`, and
  the next tick does not repeat it;
- `a_lane_that_hands_its_last_task_in_says_it_is_idle` — the lane hands `T-1`
  in: a fresh item says `lane heat is IDLE`, and the next tick is silent;
- `a_resolved_balance_item_is_not_told_again` — the captain resolves the
  balance item: the next tick opens nothing; when the imbalance ends and
  comes back, the fact is told again.

## Open questions

The imbalance item names the first lane with nothing left and the lane with
the most work left; when several lanes are idle at once, only the first is
named (§8.4 gives one example). The estimates of the tasks that have not
started are the ones the store knows (`gear_board::voyage::estimate_of`); a
task without an estimate counts as no minutes.

## Out of scope

The board's own view of the imbalance (the board shows the lanes and their
reasons, §8.2) and the `gear queue` line: other tasks.
