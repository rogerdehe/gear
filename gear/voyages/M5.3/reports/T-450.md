# T-450 — report

Working hours. With `[schedule] hours = "10:00-18:00"` the fleet works inside
those hours and rests outside them; `notice_min` before the end every lane and
reviewer with a job is told once, the captain gets one info line; at the end
every lane is parked with a wait of kind `hours` until the next start, its
round's clock takes the pause, and its agent is released whatever `on_wait`
says, while its pane, worktree, branch and session stay; a reviewer still
working is interrupted and released, and its review starts again at the next
start; at the next start each lane comes back through `bring_back` (same pane,
its session resumed, told to continue). With `depart_at` and no `hours` the end
is `depart_at` plus the voyage's eta: notice and release happen then, and
nothing comes back. While the fleet rests the tick delivers nothing and starts
no review; queued merges still run. The captain is told, never stopped.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/hours.rs` (new) — `tick` reads the project's
  `[schedule]` and, per tick: inside the hours it sends the notice once per end
  (`notice_min` before it) to every lane with an open attempt and every
  `Active` reviewer with a job, and one `Info` line to the captain; outside the
  hours it parks every `Active`/`Standby` lane with a `Wait { kind: "hours",
  until: <next start>, pause: true }`, then calls `quota::release_for_wait`
  with `force = true`, and releases each `Active` reviewer with a job through
  `release::release_one` (its pane and worktree stay, and `start_reviews`
  starts its review again at the next start); at the next start it calls
  `quota::bring_back` for each lane parked for hours whose agent was released.
  With `depart_at` and no hours the fixed end is `depart_at` plus the voyage's
  eta and the final wait is endless (`i64::MAX`), so nothing comes back.
  `resting(store, project, now)` says whether the fleet rests (outside the
  hours, or after that final end); a final rest does not log an edge case.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` — the smallest reuse changes: `Wait`,
  `load_wait`, `park_seat`, `bring_back`, `meta_key`, `meta_get` and
  `meta_set` are `pub(crate)`; `release_for_wait` takes a `force` flag that
  skips the `on_wait` policy and names "working hours end" in its line;
  `wake_from_source` says "the working hours are back" for a `hours` wait
  instead of the quota words. Quota behaviour is otherwise unchanged.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs` — `hours::tick` runs in the per-project steps
  before quota; the dispatch step prepares no delivery and the review step
  starts no review while `hours::resting` says the fleet rests; the merge step
  is untouched. `TickReport` gains `hours`.
- `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs` — next to `departs_at`, the captain asked
  for `pub fn off_hours_until(config, now) -> Option<Ts>` (the next start while
  `now` is outside `[schedule].hours`, `None` without hours or inside them),
  plus `hours_end` and `hours_next_start` for the notice and the park; the
  local-time math lives here. `crates/gear-app/src/station.rs` makes
  `eta_seconds` public so the engine reads the voyage's eta with the plan's own
  units.
- `gear` and the board do not show "off hours until" yet: by the captain's
  answer (M-6468) this task is engine only, and a follow-up mast task shows the
  line after T-449 and this task merge.

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/hours.rs` (injected local clock): at end minus
`notice_min` each lane and reviewer with a job gets the message once and the
captain one info line, and a second tick sends nothing; at the end the lane is
parked with a `hours` wait until the next start, `pause: true`, released once
with its binding, pane and session kept, and the reviewer released; at the next
start the lane comes back in the same pane with `--session <session>` and a
Continue; with `depart_at` and an eta the notice and the release happen at
`depart_at` + eta with an endless wait and nothing comes back later; a full
tick at the end starts no reviewer for a hand-in and delivers nothing.
`crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` covers `off_hours_until`,
`hours_end` and `hours_next_start`, including an overnight window.

tests passed.

## Out of scope

- The `gear`/board "off hours until HH:MM" line is the follow-up mast task the
  captain named.
- A lane already parked for another wait (quota) keeps that wait; it is
  resting anyway, so the hours step does not overwrite it.
- If the engine is down through the whole notice window it sends no notice for
  that end; the park and release still happen when it comes back.
- A CLI that cannot resume a session keeps its agent through the hours
  (the `release_for_wait` rule); it wakes in place through `wake_from_source`.
