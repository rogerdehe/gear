# T-444 — report

A reviewer is told when its direction check is closed without its result.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`, `fn finish_direction_results` only:

- Inside the existing `store.write`, before the job is closed (`tx.set_meta`
  with `{"checked_sha":…}`), a running job with no result (`!live &&
  result.is_none()`) whose seat still holds it (the same test as the Standby
  step below: seat `Active`, the job's generation, `task_id == task`) inserts
  one message to that seat: thread `direction:<task>:<attempt>:<sha>`, sender
  `engine`, kind `Instruction`, state `Open`, body
  `The direction check of <task> attempt <n> is closed: the attempt was handed
  in or ended. Send nothing for it; end your turn.`
- The job is closed in the same transaction, so the message is sent once. A job
  closed with a result, or a live job, sends nothing new.

Tests in the `review::tests` module, next to
`a_hand_in_closes_a_direction_check_that_never_answers`:

- `a_closed_direction_check_tells_its_seat_once`: the lane hands in while the
  check runs and the check never answers; after `finish_direction_results` the
  seat `p:review-1` has exactly one engine message with that body (kind
  `Instruction`, state `Open`); a second `finish_direction_results` adds none.
- `a_direction_check_that_answered_before_the_hand_in_tells_its_seat_nothing`:
  the check answers `ok` before the hand-in; the job closes with its result and
  no engine message goes to the seat.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

  generation, `task_id == task`). A seat already moved to another task by the
  hand-in's review hears nothing, as the packet says.

  `LEAK` in the first run; it does not touch this change and passed on the
