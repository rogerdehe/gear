# T-448 — report

A voyage can depart at an agreed time. A new optional `[schedule]` section on
the project config names the departure moment, the hours of the day and the
notice time; before the departure the engine starts no lane and delivers
nothing, at it the engine starts the lanes and gives them their tasks by
itself. Reviews, merges and the captain's items are untouched.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs` — `ProjectConfig` gains
  `#[serde(default)] schedule: Option<ScheduleSection>` (absent = today's
  behaviour). `ScheduleSection` is `#[serde(deny_unknown_fields, default)]`
  with `depart_at: Option<String>` (RFC3339), `hours: Option<String>`
  (`"HH:MM-HH:MM"`, local time) and `notice_min: u32` (default 15). `validate`
  adds an issue at `schedule.depart_at` when `depart_at` does not parse with
  `crate::plan::parse_rfc3339`, and at `schedule.hours` when `hours` is not two
  real `HH:MM` times of the day with a start different from the end.
  `pub fn departs_at(config, now) -> Option<Ts>` returns the parsed departure
  moment while `now` is before it, and `None` at and after it (and when the
  config names none). `hours` and `notice_min` are parsed and checked here
  only; T-450 uses them.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/onboard.rs` — in the per-project loop, right after
  the config and profiles are read, a project whose `departs_at(&config, now)`
  is `Some` onboards no lane this tick (`continue`).
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs` — in dispatch (step 3), after
  `recover_open_rounds` (reviews untouched), a project whose latest config
  (`gear_app::config::latest`) has `departs_at` `Some` prepares and intends no
  delivery this tick. The planned actions and escalated flags are still
  computed, so the captain's items are untouched.

## Tests

- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` — a scheduled config parses
  (`depart_at`, `hours`, `notice_min` default 15) and `departs_at` is `Some`
  before the named time and `None` at and after it; a config without
  `[schedule]` parses as today; a bad `depart_at` and a bad or equal-start
  `hours` are init issues at `schedule.depart_at` / `schedule.hours`, while
  `"09:00-18:00"`, `"00:00-23:59"` and `"18:00-09:00"` are valid.
- `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/onboard_run_contract.rs` — one tick-level test:
  with `depart_at` an hour ahead, a queued lane stays `Queued`, no agent
  starts, the ready task stays `Ready`, no attempt exists and no prompt goes
  out; at the departure time the engine onboards the lane and, once it is idle,
  delivers its task (attempt `Delivered`, one packet, nobody asked).

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- `departs_at` fails closed when `depart_at` does not parse (it returns
  `None`), so a reader that skipped `validate` behaves as today. A config that
  does not parse at all also lets dispatch go on (`latest` returns `Err`, the
  guard treats it as no departure): the existing onboarding path parks the
  lanes for that project, as before.
- `hours` and `notice_min` are parsed and checked only; nothing reads them
  yet (T-450).
