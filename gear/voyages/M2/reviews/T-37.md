- [P3] Date-only reset hints (the `7d … ↻Oct23` window) fall back to `now + quota_reset_h` — `captain_quota.rs` `reset_time`. If the 7d window is the exhausted one, the single wake is rung at the fallback time while the captain is still out, and no second ring happens when the real reset comes. Safe (dispatch only resumes on a real view, so nothing runs ahead of the captain) and the contract allows `quota_reset_h`, so logged: the user would have to nudge the captain after a weekly outage, or set `quota_reset_h` to the weekly horizon.
- [P3] While paused, ordinary captain doorbells (lane results, escalations) are suppressed (`lib.rs` `doorbell_inputs`); they accumulate and ring after the wake. Intended by the design (the captain cannot act), noted so the captain knows items queue silently for the outage length.
- [P3] After resume, the same 100% signature is ignored (`captain_quota.rs`, `state.resumed && state.bar == reading.signature`) until the bar goes healthy or changes. Correct for the stale-bar case; if the captain returns but is actually still capped (fallback too short), dispatch continues unfrozen. Bounded: the captain's view proves it is working.
