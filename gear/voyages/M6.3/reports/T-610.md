# T-610 — report

A nudge to a lane closes itself (M-8211: hull got "No push to … for 20 min"
after it had handed T-601 in; a lane may not resolve it and the captain may
not either, so it stayed open).

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/quiet.rs`: `resolve_nudges(tx, project, now)` runs
  at the start of the no-push tick. It resolves an open `quiet-lane:<seat>`
  item (the no-push nudge) when the attempt it names was handed in
  (Submitted/Accepted/Rejected), has a newer attempt, or its task is terminal,
  with the reason as the result. It also resolves an open `quiet:<seat>` item
  (the captain's "no word" follow-up) when a Result from the lane after the
  item was opened answers it. A nudge for a still-open attempt stays.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lane_nudge.rs`: `resolve_nudges(store, project, now)`
  runs at the start of the stopped-lane nudge. It voids an open `Deliver` op
  carrying a `nudge` payload when the attempt it names is closed by the same
  rule, with the reason as the op's result.
- `attempt_closed` is one `pub(crate)` helper in `quiet.rs`, shared by both.

## Tests

`make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(quiet::) | test(lane_nudge::)'` —
24 tests, 24 passed. New tests:

- quiet.rs: a no-push nudge closes on the hand-in of its attempt; a nudge for
  a still-open attempt stays; a no-push nudge closes on a cancel of its task;
  the captain's "no word" item closes when the lane's hand-in answered it.
- lane_nudge.rs: a stopped-lane nudge closes on the hand-in of its attempt; a
  nudge for a still-open attempt stays; it closes on a cancel of its task and
  on a newer attempt; the captain's item closes when the lane hands in.

`make gate` — 2596 tests, 2596 passed (34 s, under the 80 s limit).

## Out of scope

- The stopped-lane nudge op is voided only while it is still open
  (Intended/Executing/Submitted/Unknown); an op already Confirmed is left as
  it is.
- The no-push clock itself is unchanged: a resolved nudge does not restart the
  clock; the existing `base`/`reply` marks do.
