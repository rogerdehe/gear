# T-607 — report

A project tick that keeps failing rings the captain.

## What changed

### `crates/gear-engine/src/failure.rs` (new)

- `engine.project_failed.<project>` holds the current spell as
  `{"count":N,"first":T}`: consecutive failed ticks and the first failure's
  time.
- At 15 ticks in a row **and** 60 s since the first (the later of the two, as
  the packet says), one urgent `System` item is opened to the project's
  captain on thread `engine-failed:<project>`, naming the error code, its
  detail, the tick count and how long the spell has lasted.
- Every later failed tick rewrites the item's body (count and duration) while
  the item is open; it never opens a second one.
- The first good tick resolves the item (result names the failed-tick count)
  and resets the counter, so a new spell needs 15 ticks again.
- `settle` logs a store write that fails in the engine log
  (`release::log_line`) and goes on with the next project: the failure item is
  a report, never a reason to stop the engine.

### `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`

- Every `report.project_failed` push now also records the project and the
  first error of the tick (`note_failed`); the report lines are unchanged.
- The tick settles the bookkeeping at its end: failed projects are counted,
  the others resolved and reset.
- A tick that returns early on a failed observation counts only the projects
  that failed; no project is called good on a partial tick.

### `crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs`

- `log_line`: an engine-log line from a step that is not a release.

## Checks

`make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(project_failed) | test(engine_failed)'`:
3/3 pass (14 failed ticks open nothing, the 15th opens one urgent item naming
the error, more failures keep one item and update its body, a good tick
resolves it and resets the count). The whole `gear-engine` suite: 1116/1116
pass. `cargo fmt --all --check` clean.

## Out of scope

- The item is opened only when the project has a captain seat; without one the
  spell is still counted and resolved, but nobody is told (the same as the
  other engine items).
- A project that is paused is not settled (it is not in the tick's active
  project list); its counter stays as it was until it runs again.
