# T-623 — report

A lane's paths and words are the captain's, not staffing (Lawrence M2: adding
15 files to a lane's `owns` and fixing a command in a charter were refused with
STAFF_LOCKED until the user confirmed).

## What changed

- `crates/gear-app/src/admin.rs` `staffing_changed`: now compares `[quota]`,
  `[models]`, the lanes with each lane's name, kind, profile and models, and
  `[temps]` kind and profile. A change only to a lane's `duty`, `owns`, `not`,
  `charter` or `repo`, or to `[temps].charter`, is not a staffing change and
  applies without `--confirmed`. The lane order stays staffing (it is the
  board's order).
- `staffing_changes` keeps listing every difference; it now also lists a lane's
  `charter` and `[temps].charter`.
- New `captain_changes(changes)`: the non-staffing lines (a lane's `duty`,
  `owns`, `not`, `charter` or `repo`, and `[temps].charter`).
- `InitResult` carries `captain_changes`, filled by `re_register`; the apply
  output prints them under `changed (captain's):`.
- Today's ownership checks (overlap between lanes, open tasks' paths) still run
  on the new `owns` — `check` and `re_register` are unchanged there.

## Tests

`make check CRATE=gear-cli TESTS='test(admin_init)'` — 33 tests, 33 passed.
New `a_lanes_paths_and_words_apply_without_confirmation`: mid-voyage, a change
of a lane's `owns` and `charter` applies without `--confirmed` and is printed
under `changed (captain's):`; a change of the lane's model or of `quota.lanes`
is still STAFF_LOCKED and writes nothing.

`make check CRATE=gear-app TESTS='test(staffing_lock)'` — 6 tests, 6 passed,
including the reorder-is-staffing case.

`make gate` — 2638 tests, 2638 passed (38 s, under the 80 s limit).

## Out of scope

- A lane's `repo` is not part of the staffing comparison (the task names name,
  kind, profile and models), so a `repo` change applies without `--confirmed`
  and is printed under `changed (captain's)`.
- The `--check` output still prints the whole line diff; only the apply prints
  the captain's lines separately.
