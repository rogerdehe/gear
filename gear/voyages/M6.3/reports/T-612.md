# T-612 — report (attempt 2)

Rework of attempt 1, F1: the Esc never fired in the scenario the task exists
to fix. `free_busy_lanes` guarded with `view.attempt.is_some()`, but a delivery
that waits already has a `Delivered` attempt (`intend_dispatch` runs
`start_attempt` before the Deliver op), so the guard skipped the lane.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/lane_nudge.rs`: the guard now skips only an attempt
  the lane is working — `Started` or `Stopped`. A `Delivered` attempt is the
  delivery that waits: the lane is not working on it yet, so it does not block
  the Esc.
- The test fixture `intended()` now writes what `intend_dispatch` writes: the
  `Delivered` attempt first (via `gear_app::tasks::start_attempt`), then the
  Deliver op naming it. The Esc test now exercises the real state.
- The Started-attempt case is kept: `a_busy_lane_with_an_open_attempt_gets_nothing`
  uses the `world()` fixture with a `Started` attempt and gets no Esc and no
  item.

## Tests

`make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(lane_nudge::)'` — 8 tests, 8 passed.
The renamed `a_busy_lane_with_a_waiting_delivery_is_freed_with_one_esc` proves
the Esc fires with the `Delivered` attempt the real dispatch writes, and the
captain's item follows after 6 minutes. `a_busy_lane_with_an_open_attempt_gets_nothing`
still proves a `Started` attempt gets nothing.

`make gate` — 2604 tests, 2604 passed (46 s, under the 80 s limit).
