# Goal
GEAR never stops and holds its course by itself: lanes, reviewers and the captain keep working with nobody prodding them, and the captain finds drift from facts early in the voyage and corrects it on its own, with no approval step anywhere after kickoff.

# Milestone M2: Never stop, hold the course
done: through all of M2 the user never has to point out a stopped lane or an unhandled result; the captain found drift from measured facts in the first half and re-scoped by itself; at the end the signals and actions that worked are written into the captain guide
bar: stability and holding the course: an error in normal use is fixed at its root; everything else is done simply, details are logged, never reworked
eta: 20h
