# T-455 — report

A review seat is free only when its agent is idle and its last job's verdict or
direction result is recorded; until then the next job waits or takes another
seat. A job whose binding the seat loses is told at once.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` only.

- `free_seat` (the warm seat a new hand-in or direction check takes) keeps its
  preference order (V1: the seat of the same task's earlier review on the same
  CLI; V2: a Standby seat before a Released one) and now filters the ordered
  candidates through two checks before returning one:
  - `agent_idle`: the seat's binding pane holds no agent, or its agent is
    `Idle`/`Done`. `Working`, `Blocked` and an unreadable pane are not idle
    (design/08: a pane that cannot be read is not an absence), so the next job
    waits or takes another seat instead of having its tree prepared under a
    live review (F-215).
  - `last_job_recorded` (Standby seats only): the seat's last `Deliver` packet
    named a task/attempt whose outcome is recorded — the review verdict of
    that attempt by this seat, or the direction result of that check by this
    seat and generation on the packet's sha. A seat that never got a packet has
    no job to wait for. A direction check the engine closed without a result
    (T-258) counts as a recorded ending: its seat was told the check is closed
    (T-444) and comes free with it.
  - A `Released` seat is free as before: its job is over and its agent is gone
    (the release rules decided that), so the recorded check does not apply.
- `take_seat` takes the new job's attempt as well, and when the seat still
  holds a job binding (its `task_id`) that differs from the new job — a
  different task, or the same task's other attempt — it inserts one
  `Instruction` to the seat in the same write that moves the binding:
  "The seat is bound to a new job: <task> attempt <n>. Stop the review of
  <old> attempt <m>; end your turn." The old job learns it lost its binding at
  once instead of judging the old candidate and failing its hand-in with
  `NO_TARGET` (T-283). A rebind to the same job says nothing.

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`, `mod tests`:

- `a_reviewer_that_still_works_is_not_free_after_its_verdict` (F-215): the
  reviewer hands its verdict in, `standby_after_verdict` parks the seat, and
  its agent is set `Working`; the next hand-in waits (`report.waiting` names
  it, no `Deliver` packet for the new task, the seat holds no new job). When
  the agent ends its turn the seat takes the job.
- `a_rebind_tells_the_old_job_to_stop` (T-283): a seat bound to `T-1`
  attempt 1 is rebound to `T-1` attempt 2; the seat gets one `Instruction`
  naming both jobs, in the `review:T-1:1` thread, and a second rebind to the
  same job says nothing more.
- `judged()`: a fixture helper that records the verdict of the warm seat's
  last job, so the fixtures describe a seat that really handed in.

Changed fixtures (the new rule changes their old shortcut — each set a warm
seat `Standby` by hand without a verdict and expected the seat to take the
next job): `standby_reviewer_releases_before_moving_to_another_repo`,
`standby_reviewer_after_model_restart_restores_the_selected_model`,
`standby_reviewer_changes_model_before_a_rereview`,
`warm_reviewer_of_a_changed_chain_is_released_and_restarted_on_the_new_model`,
`warm_reviewer_of_an_unchanged_chain_is_adopted`,
`a_seat_whose_checkout_fails_three_times_is_retired_not_the_task`,
`a_hand_in_failing_on_two_different_seats_is_marked_stuck`,
`second_warm_seat_is_free_after_attempt_becomes_stuck`. Each now records the
verdict of the job it judged (passed for a settled `Done` task, a rework
finding for the rework fixtures).
`reviewer_selection_prefers_the_prior_same_cli_then_standby` passes a
FakeHerdr to `free_seat`; `a_hand_in_closes_a_direction_check_that_never_answers`
(T-258) passes unchanged with the closed-check rule.

## Friction

The code change is small, but the 10k-line `review.rs` keeps eight fixtures
that set a warm reviewer `Standby` by hand without recording its verdict; under
the new rule each had to record it. The crate suite takes ~60 s, so the task
ran past its 40-minute budget on fixture work, not on the rule.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- `agent_idle` treats an unreadable pane as busy: a Herdr read failure holds
  the seat back instead of risking a switched tree. The existing review-stall
  notice (T-376) covers the wait.
- The rebind notice fires only where a seat still holds a job binding. A
  `Standby` seat's binding is cleared with its verdict, and a `Released` seat's
  job is over, so those take the next job without a notice — correct, nothing
  to stop.
- The review packet itself still does not open with the seat's binding and
  still accepts a hand-in for a candidate that is not the attempt's current
  one; that is T-456.
