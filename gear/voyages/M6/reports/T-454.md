# T-454 — report (attempt 2)

A notice from the engine waits for the seat's next idle moment and never cuts a
running command; a seat's word (an urgent message, a captain instruction to a
lane that queues input) keeps its own path. The one exception is the notice
that closes a direction check under its reviewer: the engine cuts that turn
(the profile's interrupt key) and then delivers the notice, so the reviewer
ends its turn at once. Attempt 1 built the rule and the pure decision points
but never delivered that notice through the real tick; this rework does.

## What changed (this attempt)

F1 — the direction-closed notice now reaches the working reviewer in the tick:

- `crates/gear-app/src/seats.rs`, `message_belongs`: a temp seat still owns the
  direction-closed notice after the check's job cleared its task. The freed
  reviewer's `gear` shows the notice and the doorbell can ring it; every other
  message keeps the task-match rule.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/doorbell.rs`: `DoorbellItem` carries `cut` (the
  direction-closed notice). `rings` no longer lets a held delivery slot stop an
  item that cuts the turn.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`, `doorbell_inputs`: the `cut` mark is set from
  the message; the review-question narrowing keeps it; the slot gate is not
  taken for it. The review step runs before the doorbell step in the tick, so
  the notice written when the check closes is rung and delivered in that same
  tick.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`: unchanged in shape (it already cuts the turn
  before the text); its check now comes from the shared definition below.

F2 — one definition of the notice, so its sender and its readers cannot drift:

- `crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`: `ENGINE_SENDER`, `DIRECTION_CLOSED_PREFIX`,
  `direction_closed_notice_body(task, attempt)` and
  `is_direction_closed_notice(message)`.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` writes the body with the builder;
  `crates/gear-engine/src/doorbell.rs` (`ends_direction_check`) and
  `crates/gear-app/src/seats.rs` read it with the checker.
- `crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`,
  `the_direction_closed_notice_body_carries_the_recognised_prefix`: fails if
  the body stops opening with the prefix its readers match.

## Out of scope

- A reviewer that is *reassigned* to the review of the same task while its
  direction check closes (F1 case b) is reached by the same notice; the doorbell
  is intended after the review packet in that tick, so the escape cuts the
  review packet's turn and the notice tells the reviewer to end its turn. The
  review then waits for the reviewer nudge, as a stopped reviewer does. This is
  the behaviour the review asked for; no ordering change is made here.
- The notice is not delivered to a reviewer that was released before the
  doorbell step (its pane is closed): it stays stored and rings when the seat is
  started again.
