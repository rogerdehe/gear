# T-460 — report

`gear queue` and the board now name an attempt whose review is in and waits for
the captain as `waits for your verdict (M-<id>)`, with the id of the item that
waits in his inbox, instead of leaving the captain to read a finished review as
a stall (M5.1).

## What changed

- `crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` — new public `verdict_wait_of(tx, project,
  task, now)`: the newest open item in the captain's inbox about the task that
  means "the verdict is yours" (a reviewer's `review` item, or the engine's
  `verdict:<task>:<attempt>` / `review-stall:<task>:<attempt>` notice). It
  returns `T-n waits for your verdict (M-<id>)`; `None` without a captain or
  without such an item. The board's "to handle" list (T-360) uses it for a task
  at the verdict stage; without an item the plain words `waits for the
  captain's verdict` stay (a passed review is information and leaves no item).
- `crates/gear-app/src/queue.rs` — `Queue` gains `waiting` (JSON `waiting`):
  one line per attempt whose review is in and that waits for the captain, in
  plan order. The text rendering prints them after the lanes and before the
  overlaps, so the reviewers stay on the last line.

## Tests

- `crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs`, `queue_names_a_review_that_waits_for_the_captain_with_its_item`:
  a Verifying task with a Submitted attempt, a recorded fail verdict and an
  open `review` item in the captain's inbox gives
  `T-1 waits for your verdict (M-1)` in the text and `["T-1 waits for your
  verdict (M-1)"]` in `--json`; the lane still shows its idle reason.
- `crates/gear-board/tests/it/graph_contract.rs`,
  `a_verdict_that_waits_for_the_captain_names_the_item`: the stage fixture's
  T-31 carries a recorded fail verdict; with no item the board says the plain
  words, with the reviewer's open item it says
  `T-31 waits for your verdict (M-2)`.

`make check CRATE=gear-board` (235 tests) and `make check CRATE=gear-cli` (184
tests) pass on the branch with `origin/dev` merged in.

## Out of scope

- A passed review leaves no inbox item (`gear send --kind review --verdict
  pass` is stored as `Info`), so its verdict stage keeps the plain words
  `waits for the captain's verdict`; naming the pass verdict with an item id
  would need an engine change, not made here.
- The idle reason of a lane (`gear_board::idle`) is unchanged: the lane line
  still says `nothing left planned`; the verdict line is its own line.
