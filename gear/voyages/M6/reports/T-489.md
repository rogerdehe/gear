# T-489 — report (attempt 2, rework)

F1 from the review of attempt 1: with the project bar shown, a click on a
voyage label did nothing and a click on the bar could spuriously pick a
voyage, because the click handler compared the row with the hardcoded 0 while
`header` drew the labels at row `shift`.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`:

- `Layout` carries `voyage_row` (the row the voyage labels are drawn on), and
  `header` sets it to the header shift: 0 with one project, 1 under the
  project bar with several.

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`:

- The label click compares `row == layout.voyage_row` instead of `row == 0`.
  With the bar shown the labels are clickable on their own row and a click on
  the bar picks no voyage; with one project the behaviour is unchanged.

## Test

`board_contract::a_click_on_a_voyage_label_works_with_the_project_bar_and_the_bar_picks_none`:
two projects draw the bar, `layout.voyage_row` is 1, a click on the bar at a
column overlapping the M1 label leaves the shown voyage M2, and a click on
the M1 label on row 2 picks M1.
