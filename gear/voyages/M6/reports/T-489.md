# T-489 — report

A store with several projects gets a project bar at the top of the board: one
line naming every project with its state, voyage, station, time used of its
eta and open items, the shown one bracketed. `p`, `[` and `]` switch projects,
and every tab below (Now, Tasks, Cost, Log) shows only the selected project.
One project draws no bar and the board is as today.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`:

- `project_bar` builds the one line: per project its name, `working` or
  `off hours` (from the measured voyage's schedule), the shown voyage's
  milestone, the station the voyage is on (or `✔` when all are reached), the
  time used of its eta, and its open items; the shown project is bracketed.
- `header` draws the bar on row 0 when the board holds more than one project
  and moves the title, voyage labels, clock, goal box, header rows and tabs
  down one row; with one project `shift` is 0, so every golden frame is
  unchanged.
- The footer says `[ ] previous / next project` when there are several, and
  keeps `[ ] older / newer voyage` with one.

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`:

- With several projects `[` and `]` switch the project (`[` previous, `]`
  next) instead of picking a voyage; with one project they keep picking the
  voyage. `p` stays the next-project key. A project switch clears the picked
  voyage, so the new project shows its own measured voyage.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/multi_project_contract.rs` (3):

- two projects draw a bar naming both with their state, the shown one
  bracketed, and the title moves under it;
- `]` selects the second project and the Now tab shows only its lane;
- one project draws no bar and the title stays on row 0.

`board_contract.rs` and `quiet_contract.rs`: the two tests that walk projects
with `p` now read the title on row 1 and assert the bar brackets the shown
project.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- The bar's state is the two words the contract names (`working`, `off hours`):
  a voyage still waiting for its `depart_at` reads `working`.
- The board's plain-text `render` (the non-terminal output) still lists the
  projects one block each; the bar is the interactive frame's.
- `[`/`]` keep their voyage meaning with one project; the footer names that,
  so the two meanings are never ambiguous on the same screen.
