# T-502 — report (attempt 1)

The Tasks tab's rows now stand out by stage, on the rendered tab. Change only
in `crates/gear-board`, no other design.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs`: `GraphRow` carries styled spans
  (`Span { text, style }`) instead of one text style; `text` stays the plain
  concatenation for the picks and the tests. `git_info` builds the pieces:
  - the id and the title are **bold** for a live stage (working, asking,
    rework, handed, review, verdict, merging, merge failed), **dim** for a
    merged task, dim for a waiting/queued row, and plain for the other open
    stages (delivered, passed, sent back);
  - the stage words carry `Stage::color()` (working/rework yellow,
    handed/in review/passed/merging accent, sent back/merge failed/failed
    red, merged green, waiting/queued dim), **bold** while the stage is live;
  - the time is dim on a merged row and the `merged <clock>` suffix of the
    merge row is dim too.
  `is_live` is the one list of live stages.
- `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`: the Tasks view writes the row's spans at
  their columns one after the other instead of one `put_styled`; the narrow
  layout (the `tw` clamp and the 36-column stage field) and the selection
  highlight (`light_row` still lights the whole row) are unchanged.

## Tests

New `crates/gear-board/tests/it/tasks_style_contract.rs` on the rendered Tasks
tab (registered in `main.rs`):

- a working row: the stage cell is yellow and bold, the id and the title bold;
- an in-review row: the stage cell is accent and bold;
- a reworking row: the stage cell is the rework colour and bold;
- a merged row: id, title and time are dim, the stage words green;
- the selected live row still shows the reverse-video highlight across the
  width.

`graph_contract.rs`'s style checks follow the spans (a waiting row stays dim;
a started task whose stage is queued again is dim, since queued is not a live
stage; a working row keeps a bold id/title and its stage colour).

## Golden snapshots

The prototype's golden files are unchanged and the gate passes: the Tasks
frames' text area (columns 25..159) is already excluded by the T-494 entry
(the deps column moved the text), and this change only styles cells in that
area; the lane glyphs left of it keep the stage colour they had.
