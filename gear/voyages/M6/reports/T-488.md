# T-488 — report

`[schedule] tz = "<IANA zone>"`: `hours` and a zone-less `depart_at` are read in
that zone; without `tz` the machine's local time and UTC (as today); an unknown
zone is refused at config load naming `schedule.tz`. Two projects whose hours
overlap both run in the overlap.

## What changed

`crates/gear-app/src/config.rs` (`[schedule]`):

- `ScheduleSection.tz` (IANA zone; absent = today's behaviour) and its
  validation: an unknown zone is one `ConfigIssue` on `schedule.tz` naming the
  field and the examples.
- `hours_end`, `off_hours_until`, `hours_next_start` and `departs_at` read the
  minute and the day boundaries in the zone (jiff, one new workspace
  dependency) instead of the machine's local time when `tz` is set.
- `pub fn departure(config, text)`: the absolute departure moment. A moment
  with its own zone (`Z`, `±HH:MM`) stays absolute; a zone-less one is read as
  a wall clock in `tz`, and as UTC without one (today's rule). A wall clock in
  the spring-forward gap is read as the time after it, an ambiguous one as its
  first occurrence (the zone's compatible rule).

`crates/gear-engine/src/hours.rs`: the departure of a voyage with `depart_at`
and no `hours` (its end is `depart_at` + eta) is read with
`gear_app::config::departure`, so the zone applies there too.

`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs`: the board keeps its own copy of the same
rule (it cannot depend on gear-app). It now reads `tz` too, and its voyage
line shows the wait on the project's clock rather than the machine's; the
report carries the zone name for that line.

## Tests

App (`config_contract.rs`):

- `hours_in_a_named_zone_are_read_in_that_zone`: 10:00-18:00 Asia/Shanghai in
  at 03:00 UTC (11:00), off at 11:00 UTC (19:00), the next start 02:00 UTC
  (10:00 Shanghai), the end 10:00 UTC (18:00).
- `the_same_hours_in_another_zone_are_judged_there`: America/New_York, 14:00
  UTC = 09:00 (off, start 15:00 UTC), 16:00 UTC = 11:00 (inside, end 23:00
  UTC).
- `a_config_without_tz_keeps_local_time`: no `tz` parses, the machine's clock
  judges the hours as before.
- `a_zone_less_departure_is_read_in_the_projects_zone`: 14:00 in New York is
  19:00 UTC; without `tz` it stays 14:00 UTC; a moment with its own offset is
  absolute even with a `tz`.
- `an_unknown_zone_is_refused_at_load_naming_the_field`: `Mars/Olympus` is one
  issue on `schedule.tz`; known zones validate; a gap wall clock is resolved
  (03:30 EDT), not refused.

Engine (`tz_hours_contract.rs`):

- `both_projects_deliver_in_the_overlap_of_their_hours`: Shanghai and Tokyo
  hours overlap; at 05:00 UTC both lanes are handed their task.
- `only_the_project_inside_its_hours_delivers_outside_the_overlap`: at 09:30
  UTC only Shanghai delivers (Tokyo off); at 01:30 UTC only Tokyo delivers
  (Shanghai off).

Board (`depart_contract.rs`):

- `the_board_says_the_rest_on_the_projects_clock`: Shanghai hours, inside at
  08:00 UTC, resting at 12:00 UTC with "off hours until 10:00".

## Out of scope / noted

- The board was not named in the packet, but it duplicates the hours and
  departure rule and its clock; leaving it would have shown a `tz` project's
  wait on the machine's clock, so it reads the zone and shows the project's
  clock too.
- A `depart_at` wall clock in the spring-forward gap is resolved to the time
  after the gap (jiff's compatible rule) rather than refused; logged here as
  the edge case it is.
- The zone is read per tick from the stored config; a zone name the running
  build does not know is refused at load, and a reader that skips the check
  falls back to the machine's time (fail closed, as today).
