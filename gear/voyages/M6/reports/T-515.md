# T-515 — report (attempt 1)

A direction check's round now ends when the check ends, so a reviewer that hands
its direction result in and goes Standby no longer keeps an open round the
overrun watch rings the captain about for hours (M-5705: T-407's direction round
on review-4 open 91+ minutes after its result).

## The change

`crates/gear-engine/src/rounds.rs` `tick` gains two endings beside the two it
had (the next round on the seat, the seat's release):

- **1a, a direction round ends with its check.** For an open round with role
  `direction`, the job at `review::direction_key(project, task, attempt)` is
  read. A job that is still `running` (or `starting`) keeps the round open.
  Otherwise — a result recorded, or the job closed — the round is closed at the
  time of its direction result message: the last one its own seat sent for that
  attempt, else the last of the attempt, else now. `close_with_clock` still runs
  the overrun pass up to that moment, so the round's active time stops at the
  result rather than at the last tick.
- **3b, a round of a seat that moved on ends when its generation changed.** For
  an open round whose seat is at a newer generation than the round's and which
  holds no job for that round's task — the seat's `task_id`, a pending review
  of that task, or a direction job of it that still runs — the round is closed
  at the current generation's binding time (else now). A round whose attempt is
  still in progress is skipped: a restart inside a task keeps its round, which
  is the "dev round of a lane still on its attempt" case.

`review::direction_key` and `review::pending_review_key` became `pub(crate)` so
the bookkeeping reads the same keys the review step writes.

Overrun items of a closed round stop as they do today: `overrun::tick` only
rings for live rounds, and closing runs its accounting first.

## Tests

In `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/rounds_contract.rs`:

- `a_direction_round_ends_at_its_results_time`: the round is closed at the
  result's time (T0+40, not the tick's T0+200), and the overrun watch at T0+600
  leaves no item on `overrun:T-7:direction`.
- `a_direction_round_still_running_stays_open`: a `running` job keeps it open.
- `a_round_of_an_older_generation_on_another_task_is_closed`: generation 168's
  round on T-407, seat at 169 on T-420, closed at the result's earlier time.
- `a_dev_round_of_a_lane_on_its_attempt_stays_open`: a restart inside T-1
  (seat generation 2, attempt in progress, seat task T-1) closes nothing.

## Noted

- A direction round whose job meta is gone entirely (an older round from before
  the job key existed) closes at `now`: there is no result to date it by, and
  leaving it open is the bug this task fixes.
- Rule 3b uses the generation's binding `created` as the generation change
  time; a seat whose current binding is missing falls back to `now`, never to a
  time before the round's start.

# T-515 — rework (attempt 2)

The review of attempt 1 found that the fix did not hold for a **live** direction
check (F1), that step 1a could close a round twice (F2), and that rule 3b had no
test of its own (F3).

- **F1**: `rounds::tick` step 1b (T-458's parked-attempt reopening) now skips a
  round with role `direction` — a direction round is never a parked attempt to
  resume. A live check's round (attempt still `Started`, the M-5705 shape) is
  closed once by step 1a and stays closed, so the overrun watch no longer sees
  it live on the ticks that reopened it. New test:
  `a_live_direction_checks_round_stays_closed_over_three_ticks` — three ticks
  with the overrun watch between them, the round closed at its result's time
  every time and no item on `overrun:T-7:direction`. Verified to fail without
  the guard and pass with it.
- **F2**: step 1a reads the rounds again after step 1 (`tx.rounds_of_project`),
  so a round step 1 already closed is skipped instead of being closed a second
  time at `now`, which could push its end past the next round's start.
  Step 1b's loops read the same fresh rows.
- **F3**: new test
  `a_dev_round_of_an_older_generation_with_no_job_closes_at_the_change` — a dev
  round of generation 168, no result, seat at generation 169 holding T-9 —
  closed by rule 3b at the generation change (the current binding's time).
