# T-494 — report

The Tasks tab draws the dependencies: a `deps` column right of the lanes puts
a line from every task not yet merged to each direct dependency it waits for
that is shown and not merged. The stage says `waits for N` as a count, the
right-hand `needed by` text is gone, the selected row's lines are lit, and a
frame too narrow for the lines caps them and shows the rest as `+N` on the
task's row.

The captain set the column's place in M-6845: `deps_x = lane_end + 4` (one
space after the last lane label), the deps columns from there, and `info_x`
right after the drawn columns plus one space. The code follows that; the task
text moves right only by the columns the lines need.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs`:

- `Stage::words_of` for `Stage::Deps` now says `waits for N to merge` with the
  count of the task's dependencies instead of their ids.
- `DepsRow` (new): the cells of the deps column on one row and the number of
  lines left out (`more`).
- `deps_needed` (new): the minimum number of columns the lines need.
- `deps_rows` (new): places the lines in columns and returns the cells per
  row. Only direct dependencies are drawn; a dependency that is merged or not
  shown draws nothing. Two lines share a column only when their rows do not
  touch, so two lines that meet on a row stay two lines. The lines of the
  selected task are `Warn` and bold; the others are `Dim`.
- `edges_of` and `place` (new, private): the edges and the column layout; a
  line that finds no free column is counted on its task's row.

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`:

- `view_tasks` draws the `deps` column at `deps_x = 9 + 6 * last_lane + 4`:
  the header label cut to the drawn columns, the lines and the `+N` count, then
  `info_x = deps_x + drawn columns + 1`. The column is capped at four columns
  and at the width that keeps 40 columns for the task text; the rest of the
  lines are counted as `+N` in the column after the drawn lines.
- The right-hand `◂ needed by` / `▸ waits for` text is removed; the selected
  row's lines are lit in the deps column instead (the selected row itself is
  the board's reverse bar, so its own endpoint is reverse and the line's other
  rows carry the `Warn` style).
- Every other column of the tab (dev, the lanes, the task, what, stage, time)
  keeps its content and colour.

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/golden_contract.rs`: the prototype has no deps
column, so its Tasks frames cannot match the view's right side; two `T-494`
PENDING entries skip the columns from the deps column to the edge of the
folded and open Tasks views, and `view_differences` skips them too. The lane
graph left of the deps column is still compared with the prototype.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/tasks_deps_contract.rs` (new module, registered in
`tests/it/main.rs`), on the rendered Tasks frame:

- `a_task_waiting_for_a_task_waiting_for_another_draws_two_lines_and_no_transitive_one`
  — T-3 waits for T-2 and T-2 for T-1: two `┌` starts, two `└` ends, no third
  line for T-3-T-1.
- `a_task_waiting_for_four_tasks_draws_one_line_to_each` — four `┌` on the
  release's row and four `└` ends.
- `a_dependency_on_a_merged_task_draws_nothing` — no deps glyph on the row and
  no `waits for` in its stage.
- `the_selected_rows_lines_are_lit_and_the_others_are_not` — the selected
  task's line is `Warn` bold on the rows it crosses; another task's line is
  `Dim`.
- `a_frame_narrower_than_the_lines_caps_them_and_shows_the_rest_as_a_count` —
  at 55 columns the release's four lines become one line and `+3`.

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/graph_contract.rs`: the stage word assertions now
expect `waits for 1 to merge`.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- The plain-text graph (`graph::lines`, the no-terminal and JSON output) keeps
  its `◂ needed by` / `▸ waits for` marks; the packet removes the right-hand
  text of the Tasks tab only. Its stage words follow the count because they
  share `Stage::words_of`.
- The deps column is capped at four columns: a board with more than four
  concurrent lines shows the rest as `+N` even on a wide terminal.
- The golden Tasks frames are compared to the prototype from the deps column
  leftward only, because the prototype has no deps column.
