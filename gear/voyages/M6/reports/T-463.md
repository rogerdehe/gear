# T-463 — report

An instruction the captain sent while the lane worked on a task no longer
pins the lane as "helping the captain" once that attempt is handed in, merged
or ended: the lane is free for its next packet. An instruction sent while the
lane held no attempt still counts, until the lane answers it or the 30-minute
help limit passes.

The captain answered the one open reading question (M-6612): **B — any
instruction sent while this seat had an attempt in progress on any task,
ended or handed in since**. The rule therefore does not look at the task the
instruction names.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-board/src/idle.rs`:
  - `helping` skips an unanswered instruction whose `created` lies inside a
    work span of the seat (start < created < hand-in/merge/end).
  - `work_spans` (new, private): the lane's tasks (`tasks_of_project` filtered
    by the lane, as the captain's answer says), every attempt of them by this
    seat, from the attempt's start to the earliest evidence of its end after
    the start — hand-in, merge or settle (the settle of a closed attempt, with
    the row's `updated` as the end of an abandoned one). No new store method.
  - The reply rule, the 30-minute limit, the captain-only and lane-only rules
    and every other idle reason are unchanged.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/idle_contract.rs`:

- `an_instruction_sent_during_an_attempt_handed_in_later_is_no_help` — the
  captain's instruction (about the lane's own task) sent mid-attempt, the
  attempt handed in after it: `helping` is None and the lane is `NoWork`.
- `an_instruction_without_a_task_sent_during_a_handed_in_attempt_is_no_help` —
  the same with an instruction that names no task (the captain's B).
- `an_instruction_sent_before_the_attempt_started_is_still_help` — the lane
  held no attempt when the instruction came: it still helps.
- The existing reply, limit, captain-only and in-flight-attempt tests still
  pass unchanged.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- `work_spans` reads `tasks_of_project` and `attempts_of_task` for the lane on
  each board build of an idle lane that has unanswered instructions. That is a
  few prepared queries per lane; no new store read was added because the packet
  said `fn helping` only.
- The span starts at the attempt row's `started` when no Started step time was
  written (a delivered attempt): that is the attempt's own start column, not a
  new rule.

## Attempt 2 — rework F1

The review found the work span ended at the latest of hand-in, settle and
merge instead of the earliest: an instruction sent after the hand-in was
skipped once the attempt was settled or merged, though the lane held no
attempt when it arrived.

- `crates/gear-board/src/idle.rs`: `work_spans` now ends the span at the
  earliest of hand-in, settle and merge (`.min()` instead of `.max()`), with
  the closed attempt's row time still standing in when no step wrote one. The
  during-work skip and every other idle rule are unchanged.
- `crates/gear-board/tests/it/idle_contract.rs`: added the reviewer's case,
  `an_instruction_sent_after_the_hand_in_is_still_help_after_settle_and_merge`
  — task `T-2` lane `heat`, attempt `T-2` n=1 Accepted with Started=T0,
  HandedIn=T0+120, Settled=T0+240, Merged=T0+300; captain instruction at
  T0+180; `helping(T0+400)` is Some and reads "helping the captain: Check the
  quota line". The three attempt-1 tests and the existing reply, limit,
  captain-only and in-flight tests still pass.
