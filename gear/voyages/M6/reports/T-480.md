# T-480 — report

A rework's review is a short continuation, not a new review.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` (the seat choice, the direction-check start,
the review packet) and `skills/gear/references/review.md`.

- `start_direction_checks`: an attempt with `n >= 2` starts no direction check.
  Attempt 1 keeps its check; the mechanical-task skip and every other rule stay
  as they are.
- `start_hand_in_reviews` (the reviewer seat choice): for an attempt `n >= 2`,
  `earlier_review_seat` reads the review round of attempt `n-1` from the store
  and returns that seat while it still exists (a `Retired` seat is gone and the
  ordinary seat choice applies). `rework_seat_free` says whether it can take
  the job now — `Standby`/`Released`, no other job reserved, its agent idle,
  and (Standby) its last job's verdict recorded. A free earlier seat is taken
  with `take_seat`; a busy one is waited for: the task is reported waiting and
  the captain is told once per attempt ("the seat that reviewed attempt n-1 is
  busy"). No `free_seat` and no new seat is used while the earlier seat lives,
  so rule V1 (`session.rs`, unchanged) resumes the review's own conversation
  instead of a new reviewer reading the whole change again.
- `review_packet_for` / `review_packet`: for an attempt `n >= 2` the packet
  carries, above the goal, the captain's rework (`tasks.rework_note`) under
  `Rework (attempt n, from the review of attempt n-1):` and the change to judge
  first:
  ``The change to judge first: `git fetch <remote> && git diff <candidate n-1>..<candidate n>` ``.
  The whole-change diff against the integration branch is still named below it.
  The packet of attempt 1 is byte-identical to before.
- `skills/gear/references/review.md`: the reviewer is no longer "one fresh
  agent session"; a re-review continues its own earlier review on the same seat
  and conversation, judges the fixes of the earlier findings and the delta
  first, and does not read the whole change again.

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`, `mod tests`:

- `a_rework_attempt_starts_no_direction_check`: attempt 1 gets its direction
  job; attempt 2 (Started, a pushed branch) gets no job and no direction round,
  and the tick starts no seat.
- `a_rework_review_waits_for_the_seat_that_reviewed_attempt_one`: attempt 1 is
  reviewed on `review-1` (its session kept); the review fails and the seat is

  the round of attempt 2 opens on `review-1`, its recorded action is `resume`
  of the earlier review session, and the seat is reserved for the rework.
- `the_rework_review_packet_leads_with_the_rework_and_the_delta`: attempt 2's
  packet holds the captain's rework above `Goal:`, the
  `git diff abc1234..def5678` line, and still names the whole-change diff;
  attempt 1's packet has no rework block and no delta.

## Friction

The acceptance asks for a Resume, but V1 legitimately picks `Keep` when the
seat's agent still runs the earlier review's session — a continuation either
way. The test therefore makes the seat's current conversation differ (it ran
another job in between), so V1 resumes the review's own session; the resume
then waits in `session_runner` for the fake agent to exit, and the test asserts
the round and the recorded action rather than the packet (as the existing
`a_rereview_resumes_the_jobs_session` does).

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- A hand-in that already has a seat bound to the task keeps it even if it is
  not the seat of attempt n-1's review (an upgrade or a lost-seat edge, not the
  normal flow where the earlier seat is parked with its job cleared).
- A busy earlier seat is not reserved: if another hand-in in the same tick
  takes it first, the rework waits for that job too, as the packet asks
  ("never another seat while it lives"), and only then continues its own
  review.
- The packet still opens with `Review <task> attempt <n>` and not with the
  seat's binding, and the stale-candidate refusal is T-456 (unshown M-6732).
