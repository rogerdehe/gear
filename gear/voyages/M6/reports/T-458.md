# T-458 — report

Five smaller M5 fleet bugs, one fix and one test each, on `mast/t458` with
`origin/dev` merged in.

## What changed

- **A lane that reports blocked gets its next startable task** (M5: mast
  reported T-367 blocked on T-366 and sat idle about 40 minutes).
  `crates/gear-engine/src/dispatch.rs` (`lane_view`): an attempt whose task
  carries a declared wait (`wait_on`) is parked — it is neither the lane's
  current attempt nor a reason to hold the next task — and among the open
  attempts the newest comes first, so a lane that holds a parked attempt and a
  new one works on the new one. `crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs`
  (`start_attempt`): a seat whose in-progress attempts are all on waiting
  tasks may start another. The parked attempt stays `Started`, keeps its wait
  and comes back when the wait ends (the existing wake path).
  `crates/gear-engine/src/rounds.rs` and `crates/gear-engine/src/session.rs`
  (`create_round`), `crates/gear-engine/src/overrun.rs`,
  `crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs` (`reopen_round`): per the captain's decision,
  one seat has one live round. The parked round ends when the next round on
  its seat starts, with its clock accounted up to that moment
  (`rounds::close_with_clock`, so the time since the last tick is not lost),
  and when the parked task resumes its round opens a new window (the resumed
  work is its own time; the parked window's wait is not work).
- **A held seat may still report** (M5 F-176: while the user's stop held mast,
  every `gear send` was refused, so a finished task could not be handed in).
  `crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs`: `Held` is among the seat states that may
  send (and replay a stored receipt). New work stays held: dispatch still
  assigns nothing to a `Held` seat.
- **A repeated confirmed goal/bar/eta change records its second decision**
  (M5, T-410 review F1). `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs` (`record_voyages`): the
  decision key and the decision's `plan_version` carry the applied plan
  version, so the same confirmed transition a second time (8h → 10h → 8h →
  10h) is a decision of its own instead of a dropped duplicate.
- **The lost-merge scan runs before the old filters** (M5, T-408 review F1).
  `crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs` (`tell_captain_lost`): the merged-time,
  merge-in-flight and merge-failed filters now only decide whether a **new**
  item is opened; the finished-voyage / gone-candidate skip is checked first,
  so an open merge-lost item still closes while a merge is queued or a
  merge-failed item is open.
- **The slow-step report includes a failed observation** (M5, T-386 review
  F1). `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs` (`tick`): the `observe` step is timed
  before the early return of a failed Herdr snapshot, so a slow failed read is
  named in `TickReport.slow` like every other step. `crates/gear-herdr/src/fake.rs`:
  `FakeHerdr.agents_delay`, so a test can make that read slow.

## Tests

- `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/waits_contract.rs`,
  `a_blocked_lane_gets_its_next_startable_task`: a lane that reports T-1
  blocked on T-2 keeps the parked attempt (`Started`, wait on T-2) and is
  assigned and prompted `New task T-2`.
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/result_contract.rs`,
  `a_held_lane_still_hands_in_its_finished_task`: a `Held` lane submits its
  attempt and its question reaches the captain.
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/voyage_contract.rs`,
  `a_repeated_confirmed_change_records_its_second_decision`: three confirmed
  eta transitions record three starred `user` decisions.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs`,
  `an_open_lost_item_closes_while_a_merge_is_in_flight`: an open merge-lost
  item closes with `LOST_SKIPPED` once its voyage finishes, even with a merge
  queued for the task.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs` (`t386_tick_tests`),
  `a_slow_failed_observation_is_named_in_the_report`: a failed `agents()` that
  takes 250 ms puts `observe: <ms> ms` in the tick's slow list.
- `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/waits_contract.rs`,
  `a_parked_round_opens_a_new_window_when_its_task_resumes`: after the parked
  task's wait ends, its round is live again with a window starting at the
  resume time.
- Two existing waits tests follow the new behaviour: a parked lane now takes
  its next task, so their "no prompt" asserts name the continue of the waiting
  task instead, their fixtures put the awaited task on another lane, and
  `declared_wait_stops_the_live_overrun_clock` now expects the parked round to
  end with the next round (its clock stopped there) instead of counting the
  wait for ever.

`make check CRATE=gear-engine` (868 tests), `make check CRATE=gear-app` (589
tests) and `make check CRATE=gear-cli` (191 tests) pass on the branch with
`origin/dev` merged in.

## Out of scope

- `Parked` seats are still refused by `gear send`: the M5 item names the held
  seat, and a parked lane is parked by the plan rather than by the user's
  stop. One line to add if wanted.
- A lost-merge item stays open after its remerge lands; nothing in the merge
  path resolves it (the captain resolves it). Unchanged here.
- A resumed parked round opens a new window, so the row's clock shows the
  resumed work only: the parked window's numbers (nearly all pause) are not
  kept in the row. The captain's decision says a wait is not work, so this is
  the record of work only.
- The feed's "one task from dry" warning still counts the parked attempt as
  work in flight; with the lane now taking the next task it is not visible
  here.
