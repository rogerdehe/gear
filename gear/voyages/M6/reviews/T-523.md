They run every tick (called from `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs:600`, `:635`, `:858`):
  - `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs:1534` — `resubmit_unsent`: `for op in tx.ops_in_state(project, OpState::Submitted)` then `if op.kind != OpKind::Deliver { continue; }`;

  - `crates/gear-engine/src/quiet.rs:584` — `prepare_pending_deliveries`: `for op in tx.ops_in_state(project, OpState::Intended)` then `if op.kind != OpKind::Deliver { continue; }`.
  Reproduction: `grep -n 'ops_in_state' crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs crates/gear-engine/src/quiet.rs` shows the three walks, each immediately guarded by the Deliver-kind filter. The results are identical to today (same rows; the extra conditions are applied after the kind filter), so there is no error in normal use and, under the DELIVERABLE bar, this is logged rather than blocking. Smallest fix, ~1 line each: replace the `ops_in_state(...)` walk with `tx.ops_of_kind_in_states(project, OpKind::Deliver, &[OpState::Submitted])` (resp. `&[OpState::Failed]`, `&[OpState::Intended]`) and drop the `op.kind != OpKind::Deliver` guard, leaving the other conditions intact.
