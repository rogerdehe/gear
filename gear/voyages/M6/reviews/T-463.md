## Findings
- F1 [P1, blocking] `work_spans` ends a work span at `max(handed_in, merged, settled)` instead of the earliest of them, so a span stretches past the hand-in to the merge/settle. An instruction the captain sends after the hand-in — when the lane holds no attempt — still makes the lane helping while the attempt is under review, but the moment the attempt is merged or settled the same instruction falls inside the span and is skipped, so `helping` returns `None` though the lane never replied and the 30-minute limit has not passed. This breaks the acceptance's second case ("an instruction sent while the lane held no attempt still does [make the lane helping]") and the contract's "every other rule stays". Repro (scratch test, since removed): task `T-2` lane `heat`, attempt `n=1` by `p:heat` in `submitted`, step times Started=T0, HandedIn=T0+120, Settled=T0+240, Merged=T0+300; captain instruction at T0+180; `helping(now=T0+400)` returns `None` (expected `Some`). The report itself says "from the attempt's start to the *earliest* evidence of its end", so this is a code bug, not a chosen design. Fix in `crates/gear-board/src/idle.rs` (one line): change `.max()` to `.min()` at line 266, i.e. the end of the span is the earliest of `handed_in`/`merged`/`settled` (with the existing terminal-`updated` fallback for an abandoned attempt). That keeps the during-work skip and restores the post-hand-in instruction.
