# T-593 — report

The skill says what a feature is to a captain and to a reviewer: three lines in all.

## What changed

`skills/gear/references/captain.md` — the `feature` field of the `# Queue`
bullet, rewritten in place (no new line):

- a member's hand-in is **accepted at once** and merges into the feature branch
  with no CI; deps between members release on that merge;
- the feature's **one reviewer** gives each member a light check of direction
  and plain breakage that **blocks nothing**, reminded at 5 minutes that it is a
  stage check and free to go on with a reason;
- GEAR creates one integration task per feature, which reaches the integration
  branch with the **full review and the full gate**, a fail of either being
  **one rework of the owner lane on the feature branch**;
- the rest of the field is unchanged (cap, a dep from outside names the
  integration task, members share the issue).

`skills/gear/references/review.md` — one paragraph after §1, before
`## 2. Delivering`, the one place a reviewer looks before handing in:

**A feature's member is a stage check, not a review.** Its hand-in is accepted
and merges into the feature branch: you write no review and hand in no verdict
on it. The feature's one reviewer gives each member a light check instead — does
it go the feature's way, and is plain breakage there? — and blocks nothing. At
5 minutes you are reminded it is a stage check: report what you found, or reply
one line with the reason and go on. The full review and the full gate come once,
on the feature's integration task; a fail of either is one rework of the owner
lane on the feature branch.

Nothing else is added: the field list already carried stage 1's feature text
(T-583), and the light check's own mechanics (seat kept per feature, the packet
wording, the 5-minute reminder) stay in the engine and the packets (T-590,
T-591) where they are enforced.

Lines written: 1 changed line in `captain.md`, 1 new paragraph in `review.md`
(2 lines with its blank) — three lines in all, inside the six allowed.
