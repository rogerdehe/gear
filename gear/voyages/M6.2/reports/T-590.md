# T-590 — report

One reviewer per feature, with its conversation kept, runs a light check on
every accepted member of the feature.

## What changed

`crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs` — four accessors beside `feature_carrier`
(M6.2):

- `feature_reviewer:{project}:{feature}` → seat id: the feature's one reviewer,
  written with its first light check and read by every later one and by the
  feature's integration task's review.
- `feature_seat:{project}:{seat}` → feature (reverse): how the session rules
  know a warm seat keeps its conversation.
- `stage_check:{project}:{task}:{attempt}` → `{"feature":F,"state":"running"}`
  while a member's light check runs, `{"feature":F,"state":"done"}` once its
  result is in. It is what tells a direction job on an accepted attempt (never
  a verdict) from one that runs while the lane still works; it survives the
  direction job's own meta being rewritten by the start and closed by
  `finish_direction_results`.

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`:

- `start_stage_checks` (new, called from `start_reviews_with_tips` after the
  hand-in and direction passes): every task of the project that carries a
  feature (M6.2), is not that feature's integration task (`feature_carrier`),
  and whose latest attempt is `Accepted` with a candidate gets one light check
  — also for attempts >= 2 (a captain's rework of a member). Its job is
  `start_direction_checks`' job and branch (`direction_key`, the project's
  `direction_branch`), with the member's candidate as its sha, so
  `finish_direction_results`, `rounds` and `overrun` see it as a direction
  check.
- The seat: the feature's recorded reviewer is reused, waited for while it is
  busy (`feature_seat_free`: `Standby`/`Released` and idle, a pending
  reservation for this job allowed, `Queued`/`Starting` while its own start is
  in flight). A retired or missing seat falls back to a new one, which is
  recorded in place. The first check of a feature takes a seat as a review does
  today (`free_seat`, else `create_temp_seat` while the quota has room) and
  records it. A hand-in that still waits for a seat and a seat another feature
  already reviews on are left alone.
- `start_hand_in_reviews`: when the hand-in is a feature's integration task
  (`carrier_feature`), its one review goes to the recorded feature seat and
  waits for it; the seat it ends up on is recorded, so the feature's whole
  conversation stays on one seat.
- `stage_packet` (new) and `job_packet`: the packet of a stage check is named
  by the marker, so a start, a recovered delivery and a nudge all rebuild the
  same one:
  `Stage check of <T> attempt <n> in feature <F> (goal of the feature: <the
  integration task's contract goal>). Check the direction and plain breakage
  against the feature's goal; the full review comes once, on the feature's
  integration task. Report findings; you block nothing.` plus the binding, the
  diff range and the exact `gear send --kind result --direction` command.
- `finish_direction_results`: a stage check is a live check (its attempt is
  `Accepted`, never `Started`), so its job waits for its result instead of
  being closed on the next tick; its result — pass or findings — reaches the
  captain only, as `System`/`Info` (never a lane instruction, never an urgent
  item, never a review verdict); closing it writes `state: done` on the
  marker. A running stage job whose seat was released, retired or rebound is
  restarted by `start_stage_checks` (`stage_job_live`).

`crates/gear-engine/src/session.rs` — `review_facts`: a seat recorded as a
feature's reviewer, when it is `Standby`/`Released` with a live agent, names its
own session as the earlier work, so rule V1 keeps (or resumes) the seat's
conversation for the next light check and for the feature's one full review
instead of starting a fresh one.

`crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs` — `under_review`: a direction job held by
the seat is its live job while the job runs, also when the member's attempt is
`Accepted` (the seat was otherwise released mid-check, because the member's
task is already `Done`).

`crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs` — `send_direction`: a direction result is taken
on an `Accepted` attempt when its stage-check marker exists and the job is
running; every other attempt state keeps the old refusal.

## Tests

`review.rs` `mod tests`, `make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='…'` (all pass):

- `two_feature_members_get_their_light_checks_in_the_same_seat` — the first
  member's check runs in `p:review-1` and `feature_reviewer` records it; the
  second member's check has no job (it waits); after the first result the
  second starts in the same seat with its own candidate.
- `the_integration_tasks_review_lands_in_the_features_seat` — with the check
  running, a handed-in integration task gets no review round; after the check's
  result it starts its review on `p:review-1`.
- `a_members_light_check_opens_no_item_and_is_no_verdict` — a failing stage
  result leaves attempt `Accepted` and task `Done`, writes no review verdict,
  puts the marker `done`, tells only the captain (`System`/`Info`, not urgent),
  and leaves no actionable item for the lane or for the member.

## Notes for the merge gate

- The branch is `origin/gear/feature/f-1` plus this task; T-589 and T-591 are
  merged in, so the light check, its reminder and its result path run together.
- T-591 reads a stage check from the round (`direction` role + `task_feature`);
  the round `start_stage_checks` creates is exactly that, and its clock starts
  at the round.

## Out of scope (logged, not fixed)

- A recorded feature seat that is parked (`Unknown`, `NeedsCheck`,
  `Unavailable`) is waited for for ever; only `Retired` (the packet's case) and
  a seat gone from the store fall back. A human owns a parked seat.
- A restart of a stale stage job on the same attempt opens a second round row
  for the same `(task, attempt, direction)` key (the first round is closed by
  `create_round`); the stores and `rounds` tolerate it, but it is a duplicate.
- A feature's recorded seat can still be taken by an ordinary review through
  `free_seat` (deliberate: with `quota.temps = 1` excluding it would deadlock
  other hand-ins behind a reserved idle seat). Such a review joins the seat's
  conversation; the feature's next check waits for the seat as required.
- The stage check takes a temp slot like any review; on a project at its quota
  it waits, and `tell_captain`'s "the review of <T> waits" wording names a
  review where the check is not one.
