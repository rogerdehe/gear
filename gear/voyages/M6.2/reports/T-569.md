# T-569 — report

An item about a settled task closes itself (user, 2026-11-05: M-10318, a lane's
note on T-715 from 11:12, still rang the Prometheus captain hours after T-715
was done; five such items were open, and 7 merge-failed/merge-lost items named
tasks whose work had merged).

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs`:

- New `close_settled_task_items(store, project, now)`, called in the periodic
  lost-merge scan (step 1b) right after `close_landed_failures`, so it runs once
  per scan interval / right after a merge op settles, as that scan does.
- **Items on a settled task's threads.** For every task that is `Done` or
  `Cancelled`, every item still `Open` or `Waiting` on the task's own threads —
  `T-<n>:<attempt>` for each attempt, and the captain's `T-<n>:cap` — is
  resolved with `the task is <done|cancelled>`. An item of a task still running
  stays open: it is the captain's to act on.
- **Items whose work landed.** For every task with an attempt that has a merge
  time, the open `merge-failed:<task>` and `merge-lost:<task>` items are
  resolved with `its work landed` (`LOST_LANDED`, the same word
  `close_landed_failures` and the lost scan use).

## Acceptance

- `make check CRATE=gear-engine TESTS='test(merge::)'` — 119 tests run, 119
  passed.
- An open `T-5:1` item closes once T-5 is done and stays open while T-5 runs:
  `an_item_on_a_task_thread_closes_when_the_task_is_done` (also covers
  `T-5:cap`, and asserts the `the task is done` result).
- An open merge-failed item of a task whose attempt has a merge time closes:
  `a_failed_item_closes_once_its_attempt_has_a_merge_time` (open before the
  merge time, resolved after).

## Out of scope

- Only task threads in the packet's shape (`T-<n>:<attempt>` and `T-<n>:cap`)
  are swept; other threads (`balance`, `review-*`, `seat-pane:*`, …) are not
  touched here.
- The sweep runs inside the existing scan gate (`LOST_SCAN_EVERY_S`, or at once
  in the tick after a merge op settles), not on every tick.
- A `Waiting` item is treated as open and resolved too; the packet says "open",
  and `close_landed_failures` already treats the two together.
- No release note was written: the packet does not name one.
