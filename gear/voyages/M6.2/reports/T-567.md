# T-567 — report

The board's time line never shows an impossible forecast, and names what can
never merge; the goal box shows every clause (user, 2026-11-04, prometheus M4:
"expected 16716h25m of 50h00m, 16666h25m behind").

## What changed

1. **The forecast says which jobs can never start**
   (`crates/gear-board/src/forecast.rs`): `NO_DEP` (1,000,000 min — the
   prototype's `10 ** 6`) is public now, and `LaneEnd` carries
   `blocked: Vec<(String, String)>`: for every queued job whose dependency no
   job of the queue speaks for (a done-but-unmerged task whose merge keeps
   failing, say), the job and that dependency, in queue order. The forecast of
   the lanes themselves is unchanged: a blocked lane still ends past `NO_DEP`,
   which is what marks it.

2. **The header leaves impossible ends out and names the blocker**
   (`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`): `expected_of` takes the max of the lane
   ends **below** `NO_DEP` (the other lanes' forecast, as today) and builds the
   clause `blocked: T-570 waits for T-665, which nothing will merge`, with
   ` (+N more)` when several jobs are blocked — the first such job and
   dependency. The clause is appended (` · …`) to the time line of the folded
   header and of the unfolded one (`with_blocked`). The unfolded header's
   "slowest lane" also ignores lanes the forecast cannot reach, so the lane it
   names is a lane of the `expected` end.

3. **The goal box shows every clause** (`frame.rs`, `goal_lines`): the done
   sentence is word-wrapped to the box's columns and, in the folded header, cut
   at `GOAL_BOX_LINES` = 8 rows (it was 3) — the last row ending in `…` only
   when the goal needs more than the box; `d` still shows all of it.

## Acceptance

- `make check CRATE=gear-board` — 271 tests run, 271 passed (the golden frames
  are unchanged: the fixture's goal wraps to two rows, under both limits).
- A lane whose task waits on a dep no job speaks for leaves `expected` at the
  other lanes' max and the time line names the blocked task and its dep:
  `frame::tests::a_lane_only_a_dependency_nothing_merges_holds_is_named_not_forecast`
  (`expected` = used + the reachable lane's 2h; the clause exact, with `+1
  more`, and absent when no lane is blocked) and
  `forecast::tests::a_job_waiting_on_a_dependency_nothing_merges_is_named_as_blocked`
  (both blocked jobs named, the lane end past `NO_DEP`, the other lanes
  forecast as before).
- A board with eight done clauses shows clause (8):
  `frame::tests::the_goal_box_shows_all_eight_clauses` (eight 28-character
  clauses in a 30-column box make eight rows, all read; a longer goal still ends
  in `…` at row 8 and `d` shows more). `header_contract`'s fold test is updated
  from three rows to eight.

## Out of scope

- A blocked lane still contributes its waits to `LaneEnd.waits`; only the end is
  left out of `expected`, so the lane panel reads as it did.
- The engine's own run-dry forecast (`crates/gear-engine/src/feed.rs`) is a
  different forecast and is untouched.
- The blocked clause names the first blocked job of the first blocked lane in
  `forecast` order (lane order); a lane blocked by two jobs is counted in
  `+N more`.
