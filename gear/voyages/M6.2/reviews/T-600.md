## Findings
- F1 [P2, logged] The bisect request meta is marked `started` before the worktree is obtained and the thread spawned — `merge.rs:2063` (`set_meta(&key, BISECT_STARTED)`), with `Err(_) => continue` at `merge.rs:2069` and `if spawned.is_ok()` at `merge.rs:2097`. If `bisect_worktree` fails (seat-branch settle, herdr open/create, `check_worktree`) or the thread spawn fails, the request is consumed, never retried, and no line or note reaches the merge-failed item: a red feature gate is then never named and the captain cannot tell "could not start" from "still running". Edge case outside normal use, so it does not block, but it is silently dropped rather than logged. Smallest fix (~6 lines): move `set_meta(BISECT_STARTED)` to after a successful worktree open and a successful spawn, and on a worktree-open failure either leave the key `pending` for a bounded number of ticks or append a one-line "bisect could not start: <reason>" note to the item.
