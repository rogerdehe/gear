# T-427 — report

A hand-in takes its reviewer in hand-in order: `fn tasks_to_review` returns the
waiting hand-ins sorted by the attempt's `handed_in_at` (oldest first; an
attempt without one after those that have one), then by plan rank, then by
task id. The free seat of `fn start_reviews` therefore goes to the oldest
hand-in, not to the task that stands earliest in the plan. Consumers are
unchanged.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` only:

- `fn tasks_to_review` — the list it builds now carries its sort key
  `(Option<Ts> hand-in time, rank, task id)` beside the `ReviewTask`, read
  with `tx.attempt_times(project, task, attempt)?.handed_in`, and is sorted
  before it is returned:
  `a.0.is_none().cmp(&b.0.is_none())` (a hand-in with a time first), then the
  time, then the rank, then the id. The plan-ranked walk of
  `tx.tasks_of_project` is still the source of the tasks; only the order of
  the result changed.

## Tests

New in `review::tests`:

- `the_oldest_hand_in_takes_the_free_seat` — `T-1` (rank 1) handed in at
  `T0`, `T-2` (rank 2) at `T0-60`, one free temp seat: the seat's job is
  `T-2` and `T-1` waits.
- `equal_hand_in_times_fall_back_to_the_plan` — both handed in at `T0`: the
  seat goes to `T-1` (rank 1) and `T-2` waits.
- `an_attempt_without_a_hand_in_time_loses` — `T-2` (rank 1) has no recorded
  hand-in time, `T-1` (rank moved to 2) has one: `T-1` takes the seat.

Helper `another_hand_in(store, task, rank, at)` hands a second task in beside
the module's `T-1` fixture (the `gear_app::tasks` pipeline, or a raw
`Submitted` attempt for the no-time case); `served(store)` names the task the
one free seat served and checks that the other hand-in waits.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- `fn tasks_to_review` now reads `attempt_times` once per waiting task: two
  queries per hand-in instead of one. A hand-in waits at most a few ticks and
  the list is short (the other project peaked at 26); a `JOIN` would save it,
  but that is a store query the packet did not ask for.
