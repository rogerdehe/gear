# T-407 attempt 2 — the two probe gaps of the review

## F1 — a retried merge step logs its own time

`crates/gear-engine/src/merge.rs`, `Probe::begin`: the unfinished-step early
return stays (an engine restart in the middle of a step keeps that durable
beginning), but a step whose earlier boundary already ended no longer reuses
it. The retry pushes a boundary at `now_ms()`, so `merge_with_retries`'
second attempt measures its fetch/merge/gate/push/close from its own start, not
from the first attempt's.

New test `merge::tests::a_retried_step_logs_its_own_time`: a `Probe` reopened
from a durable record whose fetch began at 1000 ms and ended at 1200 ms starts
a new unfinished fetch boundary (begin > 1200), logs the retry's fetch line
with its own few milliseconds (not the 1000 ms since the old beginning), and
keeps the earlier completed boundary in the record. The old code finds no new
boundary (it reuses 1000 ms), so the test is discriminating.

## F2 — a delivery that fails while it is aimed logs its lines

`crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`, `execute_intended`: the `Prepared::Run` probe
handling now matches on the written state. `Submitted` keeps the `typed` and
`enter` lines; `Failed` (the `Aimed::Gone` and `Aimed::Refused` arms) writes
`failed` and `done` from the op's durable intent to now, with the same total.
No successful `typed`/`enter` line is fabricated.

The agent-gone fixture
(`targeting_contract::a_prompt_to_a_pane_with_only_a_shell_fails_as_agent_gone`,
a pane that is only a shell) now asserts the two lines:
`probe deliver T-1 failed 10000 total=10000` and
`probe deliver T-1 done 10000 total=10000`. It reads the probe log without
draining, as the crate's other probe tests do, so a test running beside it
cannot take its lines.
