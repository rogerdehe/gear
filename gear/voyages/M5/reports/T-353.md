# T-353 — report

The head of the board is the prototype's (README changes 1 and 2): every
voyage on row 0 with the shown one lit, the shown voyage named on row 1, and
the goal with its quality bar in a yellow bold box. Rows 0-6 of all eight
golden frames match the prototype cell by cell, so the eight `T-353` PENDING
entries are gone.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` and `lib.rs` only; tests where the crate
keeps them.

- `fn header` (frame.rs) now draws the prototype's head and returns a
  `Layout`. Row 0: `"GEAR · <project>  "` bold at x 1, then one
  `" <milestone> ⛵ "` / `" <milestone> ⚓ "` label per `project.voyage_list`
  entry from `x = len(project) + 8`, reverse+bold for the shown voyage and
  dim for the others, `x` advancing by the label's characters + 1; then
  `"[ older   ] newer"` dim at `x + 1`, the engine-down warning (bad) after
  it, and `idle::clock(board.now)` dim at `W - 8`. Row 1: `"<milestone>:
  <title> · ⛵ sailing"` (or `⚓ landed`) bold at x 1. Then the box at
  `bw = W - 3`: `╔═ GOAL ` + `═`·(bw − 9) + `╗` warn+bold, the voyage's
  `done` sentence wrapped to `bw - 4` (the prototype's `wrap`) with each line
  `"║ " + padded + " ║"` warn+bold, the bar line padded in warn (not bold),
  and `╚` + `═`·(bw − 2) + `╝` warn+bold. The tabs follow the box directly
  (row `tab_row`), and `top = tab_row + 2`.
- `pub fn layout(board: &Board, screen: &Screen) -> Layout` draws that head on
  a scratch canvas and returns it; `Layout { top, tab_row, tabs, voyages }`
  holds the view's top, the tab row, the inclusive column ranges of the tabs
  and of the voyages' labels (with the milestone). `canvas()` uses the
  layout's `top` for the view and hands it to `draw_panel`.
- The prototype's export draws ⛵ and ⚓ in two cells (the glyph and its
  continuation, a space with the same style), and the golden frames are the
  standard: `fn put_wide` puts such a text the same way and returns the cells
  it took. The prototype's `x` still advances by characters, so the labels
  sit at the golden's columns.
- The old time, tasks and done-sentence rows go, with `duration_minutes`;
  `TAB_ROW`, `VIEW_ROW`, `FIRST_ROW`, `tab_at` and `tab_ranges` go.
- `VoyageEntry` (lib.rs) gains `done` and `bar`, filled from the voyage row in
  `voyage_entries`. The head reads the shown voyage (`project.shown`), not
  `project.voyages.first()`: the reports leave a landed voyage out, and
  change 1 says a landed voyage keeps its data.
- `run_with` takes the tab row, the tab ranges and the view's top from
  `frame::layout`, so the page size (`height - (top + 2)`), the view's picks
  and the hit testing move with the head's height. A click at the terminal's
  (x, y), counted from 1, is the screen's column x − 1 and row y − 1.

  is returned for the task that adds it, and the footer already promises
  "click a voyage". The engine-down warning keeps the columns the packet
  gives it whatever the labels do.

  box — is not drawn (the packet names the box and its bar only). `dev` now
  carries `ProjectBoard::goal_changes` (T-335); the line that shows it belongs
  to the task that draws it.
- The goal box's lines are padded by characters, as the prototype's `ljust`
  pads them; a done sentence or bar holding a wide glyph would shift its line
  inside the box by one cell (none does today, and a text longer than
  `bw - 4` is cut by the canvas, not wrapped twice).
- The folded header rows (7-9), the framed tab bar and the views below it are
  the queued tasks' rows and stay pending in the golden gate; the view now
  paints from `top = tab_row + 2`, two rows under the tabs, where the packet
  puts it.
