# T-432 — report

T-426's skip of the `Standby` arm of the agent-gone sweep now holds only while
the seat's session step is fresh: a `Standby` reviewer whose step sits
unfinished at `wait_exit` (or any other switch step) is released as T-399 did
once `now − step_at` reaches 120 s, so a switch that stopped on its way no
longer holds a temp slot for ever.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs` only:

- `const SESSION_SWITCH_GRACE_S: i64 = 120;` — how long the session step of a
  `Standby` seat is trusted on its own. Nothing drives that step for a
  `Standby` seat (`session_runner` does not run for it), so an unfinished step
  would otherwise read as a switch for ever.
- `fn a_session_switch_is_in_flight(store, seat, now) -> GearResult<bool>` — as
  T-426 wrote it, plus the age: after the generation check and the
  `done`/`failed` check, the step is in flight only when `now − step_at <
  SESSION_SWITCH_GRACE_S`. A step without a readable `step_at` says nothing
  about its age (like a step of an older generation), so it is no switch and
  the seat is released.
- The `SeatState::Standby` arm of `release_finished_reviews` passes `now` to
  that helper; nothing else in the arm changed (`STANDBY_AGENT_GONE_S`, the
  `herdr.agent_get` reading, `release_one_ending_its_review(…, "agent-gone")`).

## Tests

In `release::tests`:

- `a_standby_reviewer_with_a_fresh_session_step_is_not_released` (new) —
  Standby since `T0−300` (past the 60 s grace), no agent in the pane, session
  step `wait_exit` of generation 1 `step_at = T0−30`, checked at `T0`: the seat
  stays `Standby`, its `engine.review_pending` review is kept, no pane closed.
- `a_standby_reviewer_stuck_in_a_session_step_is_released` (new) — the same
  seat with `step_at = T0−300`: `Releasing`, one pane closed, its pending
  review cleared with its transition.
- `a_standby_reviewer_switching_its_session_is_not_released` (T-426, timing
  updated) — its step was `step_at = T0` checked at `T0+120`, which is no
  longer a fresh step; it is checked at `T0+100` (still past the 60 s grace),
  so it still tests the switch skip and asserts the same thing.

Every other `release::` test is unchanged and passes.
