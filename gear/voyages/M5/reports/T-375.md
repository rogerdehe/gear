# T-375 — report

A reviewer starting the review of a rework is not put back to `Standby` by its
own verdict on the earlier attempt.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`** — `fn handed_a_verdict` only:

- The job is named by the seat's **open review round** first:
  `tx.open_round_of_seat(&seat.id)?` filtered to `role == "review"` and
  `task == task`; its `attempt` is the job's attempt.
- Otherwise, as today: the seat's last `Deliver` op (`None` job when its task is
  not the seat's task), and otherwise the task's latest attempt.
- The doc comment states why (T-375): while round 2's session is being resumed
  no attempt-2 packet exists yet, the last packet is round 1's, and the round-1
  verdict would park the seat in the middle of the rework review.

No other production code changed.

## Tests

Both live in `review.rs`'s test module.

- `the_earlier_verdict_does_not_park_the_seat_of_the_open_round` (unit): a seat
  that gave the pass-or-fail verdict of T-1 attempt 1 (its last `Deliver` op is
  attempt 1's packet) and holds T-1 again with an open review round of attempt 2
  and no attempt-2 packet stays `Active` through `standby_after_verdict`; once
  the verdict of attempt 2 by that seat exists it goes `Standby` with its job
  binding cleared.
- `a_rework_hand_in_keeps_the_seat_that_judged_round_one_working` (engine, two
  ticks, through `start_reviews` and `standby_after_verdict`): tick 1 starts the
  reviewer of attempt 1, whose packet the pane refuses (`agent_not_ready` — the
  seat keeps its activation, which is the state `recover_missing_packets`
  serves); the verdict of attempt 1 lands and the lane hands in attempt 2. Tick
  2 has `start_reviews` take the same seat for the rework: the open round of
  attempt 2 exists, no attempt-2 packet is sent yet (the session is brought up
  first), and the seat is `Active` holding `T-1`. `standby_after_verdict`
  between the ticks parks nothing and the seat keeps its job; the verdict of
  attempt 2 then parks it.

Both tests fail with the old `handed_a_verdict` (checked by disabling the round
lookup: the seat was parked in the middle of the rework review) and pass with
the change.

## Out of scope

- The seat's state while round 2's session is resumed is `Standby` on the
  "warm seat" start path (`start_reviewer`'s `Standby` branch sets
  `Active` + task + packet together once the session is ready); the rework tick
  that motivated T-375 goes through the activation that is already standing
  (`recover_missing_packets`), which is the state the engine test builds. If a
  soak shows the `Standby` path can also leave the seat Active mid-resume, the
  start path is a task of its own — outside this packet's "change
  `handed_a_verdict` only".
- No behaviour change to `under_review` (`release.rs`) or `recover_missing_packets`,
  which read the latest attempt as before.
- `cargo fmt --check` is not clean on this repo before the change, so no
