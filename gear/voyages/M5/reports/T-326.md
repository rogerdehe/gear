# T-326 — report

With `[models].on_wait = "release"` a lane parked on a long quota wait no longer
keeps its agent in the pane for hours: the agent exits, so it uses nothing until
the reset, and its pane, worktree and session are kept for the comeback
(`crate::restart`, the next hull task).

## What changed

**`crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` only.**

- New `const RELEASE_MIN_WAIT_S: i64 = 900`: a wait shorter than 15 min is not
  worth an exit.
- New `fn release_for_wait(store, herdr, project, &config, profile, seat, now)
  -> GearResult<Option<String>>`, called in `apply_quota_policy_with` inside the
  `if seat.state == SeatState::Parked` branch, **before** the wake checks; a
  `Some(line)` is pushed to the report's events and the seat `continue`s.
  It releases only when all of these hold:
  - `config.models.on_wait == WaitPolicy::Release`;
  - `seat.role == SeatRole::Lane`;
  - the meta `engine.wait_released.<seat>` is empty or missing (one release per
    wait);
  - the wait ends at least `RELEASE_MIN_WAIT_S` after now. The wait is
    `load_wait` with `pause` true (a quota park, an unreadable source, a
    `wait`-policy park), else `parked_until` (a policy park's deadline). A
    provider-error wait pauses no round and is 5 min, so it is never released;
  - `profile.resume_args.is_some()` and `profile.session_ref`: a CLI whose
    session cannot be resumed keeps its agent, and the event line says
    `<seat>: kept through the wait: its CLI cannot resume a session`;
  - the seat's binding (`tx.get_binding(&seat.id, seat.generation)`) is
    `BindingStatus::Active` and `herdr.agent_get(&pane)` is `Ok(Some(_))` — a
    pane that is already a shell has nothing to release.
- The exit goes the way `crate::restart`'s stop phase sends it:
  `crate::names::ensure(herdr, &pane, &crate::names::desired(seat))`, then
  `profile.exit_command` as a prompt to `crate::names::live_name(herdr, &pane)`
  when it is set, else `herdr.send_keys(&pane, &profile.exit_keys)`. On
  `Outcome::Ok`/`Outcome::Uncertain` the meta is set to `"1"` and the captain is
  told `<seat>: released for the wait until <until> (on_wait = release): its
  agent exited; its pane, worktree and session are kept`; on
  `Outcome::Refused` nothing is written and the next tick tries again.
- The seat stays `Parked`, its binding stays `Active`, the wait row and the
  pause row are untouched. With `on_wait = "keep"` (the default) the park is
  exactly as today.
- Test fixtures only (`#[cfg(test)]`): `config` now delegates to
  `config_full(chain, policy, on_wait, reset_h)`; `source_world` delegates to
  `source_world_on_wait(key, on_wait)`; `tick_quota`/`tick_source` delegate to
  `tick_quota_with`/`tick_source_with`, which take the profiles;
  `cfg_resumable(bool)` patches the lane's profile (`session_ref = true`, and
  with `false` no `resume_args`).

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs`, on the fixtures of
`a_spent_window_parks_the_lane_until_the_reset`:

- `on_wait_release_exits_the_agent_of_a_long_wait` — after the park of a spent
  window the next tick sends the profile's exit keys once (`ctrl+c`), writes the
  `wait_released` meta and the captain's line, leaves the seat `Parked` and the
  binding `Active`; a third tick sends nothing more and reports nothing.
- `on_wait_keep_leaves_the_agent_in_its_pane` — with `on_wait = "keep"` no exit
  is sent and nothing is marked.
- `a_short_wait_is_not_released` — a wait ending 630 s from now is under the
  900 s bar: no exit.
- `a_policy_park_is_released` — a `fallback` policy park (`parked_until`, no
  wait row, 2 h) is released on the next tick.
- `a_profile_without_resume_args_keeps_its_agent` — no exit, no meta, and the
  event line "kept through the wait: its CLI cannot resume a session".

## Out of scope

- **`profile.session_ref` vs the opencode lane**: the packet's condition
  `profile.resume_args.is_some()` **and** `profile.session_ref` cannot fire for
  the fixture's opencode lane, whose builtin profile has the resume args but
  `session_ref = false` (Herdr does not report opencode's session). I asked
  (`gear send --kind question`, thread T-326:1) and went with the literal
  reading: the tests patch the lane profile to `session_ref = true`. A real
  opencode project on `on_wait = "release"` will therefore keep its agent. If
  the release is meant to work for opencode too, the gate needs another signal
  (the binding's `session_ref`, or dropping the profile flag) — a captain
  decision, not mine.
- The "kept through the wait: its CLI cannot resume a session" line is returned
  (and logged) on **every** tick of such a long wait until it ends, because the
  packet gives that case no meta to make it once. Harmless for the tick log, but
  it is a repeated line.
- The comeback — starting the CLI in the kept pane and resuming the session — is
  `crate::restart`, the next hull task; nothing here restarts anything.
- The `TESTS='…'` variable in the packet's acceptance command is not a knob of
  `make check` (the Makefile's `check` passes no filter), so I ran the nextest
  filter directly with the same caps. `make gate` runs the whole gate.
