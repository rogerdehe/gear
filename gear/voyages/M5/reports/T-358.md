# T-358 — report (contract v1)

A confirmed goal change (T-410's record) now draws a red line under the goal
box: when, before and after; every row below it moves down one. Golden frames
unchanged (their fixture has no goal change).

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` (model):

- `ProjectBoard.goal_changed: Option<(String, String, String)>` — the *latest*
  confirmed goal change of the shown voyage as (time, before, after), `None`
  without one.
- `fn goal_changed_of(changes: &[GoalChangeBoard]) -> Option<(String, String, String)>`:
  takes the last entry of the already-built `goal_changes` (T-335, oldest
  first) and reads the words of the record T-410 (C6) writes,
  `<field> of <M> changed from "<old>" to "<new>"`; a record whose text is not
  that shape yields `None` rather than an invented before/after. Time is the
  record's own `at` (`2026-10-27 12:40`).
- Filled in `fn board` right after `goal_changes`.

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` (fn `header`):

- After the goal box (and before the folded/unfolded rows): when
  `goal_changed` is `Some`, `Bad+Bold` at (1, row) of
  `"Goal changed  "` (label padded to 14) + `"<time>: <before> → <after> (the
  user agreed; Decisions shows both)"`, then `row += 1`; the header rows, the
  blank row and the tab row follow one row lower. The time is the record's
  clock (`clock_of`, the same shortening the decisions log uses).

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/header_contract.rs`, new
`a_goal_change_puts_a_red_line_under_the_box_and_moves_the_rows_down`:

- without a change: `goal_changed` is `None`, no "Goal changed" row, the box's
  bottom sits five rows above the tabs;
- with two records: `goal_changed` is the latest, with its time and its two
  texts;
- the red line is at column 1, `Bad+Bold`, the label padded to 14, the full
  sentence with before and after; the folded header starts on the next row and
  the tab row is exactly one row lower than without the line.

Golden tests pass unchanged (`golden_contract`): the fixture has no `user` /
`gear` decision, so no line and no shift.

## Acceptance

`make check CRATE=gear-board` — 217 tests, all pass.

## Out of scope

- The record itself (C6, T-410) already exists in `plan.rs`; it is read here,
  not written.
- A goal change of a field other than `done` (`bar`, `eta`, `title`,
  `stations`) also counts as "the latest goal change" and is drawn with its own
  before/after, as the packet's "latest goal change" reads; the label stays
  "Goal changed". If the board should draw only `done` changes, that is a
  smaller contract.
- The `at` is the full stamp in the model (`2026-10-27 12:40`) and only the
  clock is drawn, like the decisions log.
