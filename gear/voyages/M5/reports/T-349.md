# T-349 — report

The board can play each lane's queue forward: when the lane ends, how long it
sits idle on the way, and what it waits for. The engine's run-dry forecast is
not this (it knows no dependency) and the board cannot call it, so the board
has its own, exactly the prototype's `fn forecast`.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/forecast.rs`** (new; `pub mod forecast;` in `lib.rs`)

- `pub struct Job { id, lane, stage: graph::Stage, est_min, actual_min, deps }`
  and `pub struct LaneEnd { lane, end_min, waits: Vec<(i64, String)>, pace }`.
- `pub fn forecast(jobs, lanes, review_merge_min) -> Vec<LaneEnd>`, one end per
  lane in `lanes` order, the prototype's algorithm and nothing else:
  - pace of a lane = Σ actual / max(1, Σ est) over its Merged jobs, clamped to
    0.5..=3.0; 1.0 when it merged none;
  - the `merged` map starts Merged → 0, Handed/Review/Verdict →
    `review_merge_min`, Passed/Merging → 5;
  - four passes over the lanes in order, `free`/`waits`/`last` reset per lane
    and pass: the lane's held jobs (Working, Asking, Delivered, Rework,
    SentBack) then its Queued/Deps jobs, each group in `jobs` order. A
    Queued/Deps job starts at `free` unless its dependencies merge later: then
    it starts at that merge and the gap is a wait naming the *first*
    dependency with the latest merge (10^6 minutes for a dep no job speaks
    for, 0 with none). `left = max(est − actual, 10)` when `actual > 0`, else
    `est`; `free = start + floor(left × pace)`; `merged[id] = free +
    review_merge_min`; the lane's end is the largest of `last` and the `merged`
    of its Handed/Review/Verdict/Passed/Merging jobs.
- `pub fn review_merge_min(tx, project, milestone)`: over the milestone's Done
  tasks, each task's newest attempt that has both a hand-in and a merge time
  (`attempt_times_of_task`, highest `n`), the mean of `merged − handed_in` in
  seconds divided by 60; the constant 15 when no task has both. The constant is
  exported as `REVIEW_MERGE_MIN`.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`**

- `ProjectBoard.forecast: Vec<forecast::LaneEnd>`, filled from the tasks of
  `project.lanes` (plan order, never `project.graph`, which is in display
  order) for the measured voyage while any of its lane tasks is not terminal,
  and empty otherwise. `est_min`/`actual_min` are 0 when unknown; the
  `review_merge_min` the forecast uses is measured from the voyage's own
  merges.
- `TaskBoard.deps: Vec<String>` — the task row's deps, which is where the jobs
  get them.

## Tests

**`crates/gear-board/src/forecast.rs`** — unit tests on the prototype's M5 jobs
(the twelve of `VOYS[0]["T"]`, stages done→Merged, review→Review, rework→
Rework, working→Working, passed→Passed, deps→Deps, queued→Queued;
`review_merge_min` 15; lanes hull, mast, keel):

- `the_m5_voyage_ends_when_the_prototype_says`: hull ends at 134 with a wait of
  (91, "T-266") and pace 1.18, mast at 102 with no wait, keel at 61 with a wait
  of (15, "T-262") and pace 0.9 — the prototype's own output;
- `a_lane_with_nothing_planned_ends_at_once`;
- `a_dependency_nobody_speaks_for_never_merges`;
- `the_pace_stays_between_a_half_and_three` (both clamps, and an all-zero
  estimate dividing by one).

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/forecast_contract.rs`** (new; registered in
`tests/it/main.rs`) — the store side:

- `the_review_and_merge_takes_the_mean_of_the_merged_tasks`: two merged tasks
  that waited 10 and 20 minutes → 15;
- `a_voyage_that_merged_nothing_takes_the_default`: a Done task whose merge
  never landed, and a voyage with no Done task → 15;
- `the_newest_attempt_that_reached_the_branch_counts`: an earlier attempt with
  both times an hour apart is not measured (the mean would be 30, it is 13);
- `the_board_plays_each_lane_forward_while_the_voyage_has_open_work`: one end
  per lane in the lanes' order, hull idle on keel's T-3 which is still open,
  `TaskBoard.deps` carrying T-3, and no forecast once every task is merged.
