# T-266 — a lane that hands in after a fail is told that a rework waits

`mail::send_inner` (crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs) now answers the hand-in of an
attempt whose work was already sent back. The `crate::tasks::submit_result`
call is no longer `?`-propagated: on any error the send asks the task for an
attempt **newer than the one the message names**, belonging to the sender's
seat, and **not handed in yet** (`Delivered`, `Started`, `Stopped`, `Unknown`).

- Such an attempt exists: the send fails with
  `GearError::new(ErrorCode::BadTransition, "a rework of <task> (attempt <newer>)
  is waiting for you: end your turn now and its packet arrives; do not hand in
  attempt <named> again")`. The transaction aborts as before: no message, no
  attempt change.
- No such attempt (including a hand-in of the rework itself, whose own attempt
  is not older than the named one): the original error is returned unchanged,
  and every failure that happens before `submit_result` (owner/generation,
  contract, friction) keeps its own text too.

When several qualifying newer attempts exist, the newest is named.

## Tests (crates/gear-app/tests/it/result_contract.rs)

Fixture `failed_with_rework_open`: attempt 1 handed in, fail verdict with a
blocking finding, `settle_review`, then the engine's `start_attempt` opens
attempt 2 on `p:heat` (state `Delivered`, packet not yet read).

- `hand_in_after_a_fail_names_the_rework_that_waits`: a result for attempt 1
  gets `BadTransition` with exactly the rework text naming attempt 2; attempt 1
  stays `Rejected`, attempt 2 stays `Delivered`, no captain message is written.
- `hand_in_of_the_rework_itself_keeps_its_own_error`: attempt 2 handed in with
  the candidate attempt 1 failed on fails on that (`BadFormat`, "already failed
  on attempt 1") and carries no rework line — the named attempt is the newest.
- `hand_in_with_no_newer_attempt_keeps_todays_error`: one attempt, handed in
  twice, still reads `attempt: submitted -> submitted` and no second attempt
  exists.
