# T-364 — report

The Log view is the prototype's (`prototype.py` `view_decisions`, README change
10): what changed the course first, then every decision, then the friction
reports. M4's "DECISIONS MADE — starred only, one `by kind:` line, frictions
with open/closed" is gone, and `fn counts_of_kind` with it.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`** (no other code file touched)

- `fn view_decisions(project, screen, canvas, top)`, exactly the packet's rows,
  the prototype's fixed columns (`put_styled`, never a pushed line, so a long
  task id cannot move the later columns):
  - `WHAT CHANGED THE COURSE  (★)` Bold at 1 on `top`; each starred decision
    `<clock> ★ <what>` Warn at 3, or `nothing` Dim at 3 when none is starred.
  - one blank row; `ALL DECISIONS (<n>)` Bold at 1.
  - each decision is a pick: `›` (selected) or ` ` in Acc at 1,
    `<clock> <★ or ' '> <kind:<9>` at 3 (Warn when starred, else plain), its
    what plain at 22, and `why: <why>` Dim at 22 on the next row when it has
    one. The reason is still not a pick (T-342's rule, `hl` is not on it).
  - one blank row; `FRICTION REPORTS FROM AGENTS` Bold at 1; each friction
    `<task> <lane:<5>` in the lane colour at 3, `unread` (open) Warn or `read`
    (closed) Dim `<7` at 15, its body plain at 23.
  - `clock_of` is unchanged and gives the clock of the decision's `at`.
- `fn counts_of_kind` removed (it had no other caller).

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/golden_contract.rs`**

- `the_log_view_paints_the_prototype_rows` (new): paints `Tab::Log` at the
  prototype's own origins — the folded view's `TOP[0] = 14`, the open view's
  `22` — and compares rows 14-46 and 22-46 cell for cell (character, colour,
  reverse) with `log-folded.*` / `log-open.*`, excluding only the fixture's
  `prototype data` cells and the declared `u`/`m` lane-colour swap, as
  `the_tasks_view_paints_the_prototype_rows` does. It passes with no other
  exclusion, so the view's own rows are the prototype's. Row 13 is the
  header's last row and row 47 the footer, so the comparison starts at the
  view's first row and ends at 46 (the packet's "rows 13-46 / 21-46" counted
  from the row above the view; the log frames are blank down to 46, unlike the
  Tasks frames whose legend sits on 46).
- `view_differences` gains the `tab` parameter (it was hard-wired to
  `Tab::Tasks`); the Tasks test's two calls take `Tab::Tasks`. T-363 made the
  same change on its branch, so `origin/dev` (`T-363` merged) conflicted only in
  this file: the merge keeps one helper, the Cost and Log tests side by side,
  and the cost entries' `T-363 (T-356 removes it)` label.
- The two golden exclusions stay, relabelled
  `T-364 (T-356 removes it)` with a comment, exactly as T-363's `cost-folded`
  / `cost-open` entries did: the board's header is four rows until T-353..T-356
  merge, so its view starts on row 5 and cannot match the prototype's rows by
  absolute row. Once the header is the prototype's height the anchored test's
  rows are already covered by the frame comparison and the two entries go with
  T-356. For these two frames the `PENDING` list now holds only the
  `prototype data` entries beside that one skip entry.

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/decisions_contract.rs`**

- `the_log_shows_what_changed_the_course_then_every_decision_and_the_frictions`
  (was `the_decisions_view_shows_the_log_the_star_and_the_frictions`): the
  summary heading and `12:05 ★ light procedure moved to M5`, `ALL DECISIONS
  (2)`, the unstarred decision's own row (`11:40   scope`) and what, its
  reason, no `by kind`, and the friction rows — the open one reads `unread`,
  the closed one `read` and not `unread` (the old `open`/`closed` are gone).
- `a_log_without_a_starred_decision_reads_nothing` (new): a voyage with one
  unstarred decision, its summary row reads `nothing` and the decision is
  still `ALL DECISIONS (1)` below it.
- `down_moves_over_the_reason_of_a_decision`: every decision is a pick now, so
  two `j` presses reach the third decision (12:20) over the why line of the
  second; the picked row's mark is read in column 1 (where the prototype puts
  it) instead of column 0.

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/colour_contract.rs`**

- `a_long_lane_name_never_moves_the_text_columns`: the friction status is
  `unread` at column 15 for a long lane name and for a short one, as the
  packet's fixed column says (it read `open` before).
