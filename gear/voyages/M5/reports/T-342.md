# T-342 — report

A view paints on a canvas, at columns, as the prototype does, and the selected
row is lit across the width in one colour (README change 13). `ViewRows`,
`scroll_of` and the per-cell `reversed_if` of the views are gone.

## Rework (attempt 2, F1 of the review of attempt 1)

- **`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — in `run_with` the click's lower bound is
  `frame::VIEW_ROW`, not `frame::FIRST_ROW` (one line, plus the comment that
  says why): the view's own first row is a row the view draws, and scrolling
  can put a pick there. The picks lookup already rejects a click on the
  heading row (row `top - 1`) and everything below the viewport, so no other
  bound changed.
- **`crates/gear-board/tests/it/board_contract.rs`** — new
  `a_click_on_the_top_row_of_a_scrolled_view_picks_the_task_there`, the
  review's own sequence: `world_with_a_long_graph` on an 80x12 screen, `2` to
  the graph, sixteen `Down`, a click at x 1 y 6 (the view's first row, where
  T-13 is drawn once the viewport is offset by 13). The final frame has
exactly one lit row, it holds T-13, and T-17 is no longer drawn. Checked to
fail with `FIRST_ROW` restored and to pass with the fix.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`**

- `Canvas::copy_rows(&mut self, from: &Canvas, src: usize, dst: usize, count: usize)`:
  copies rows cell by cell, styles included; both canvases are cut at their own
  edge.
- `pub fn view(board: &Board, screen: &Screen, canvas: &mut Canvas, top: usize) -> Vec<usize>`:
  dispatches on `screen.tab`, paints the heading at row `top` and every line
  with `put_line(0, row, …)` from `top + 1`, and returns the canvas row of each
  selectable row, in order — the picks.
  - `view_now`, `view_tokens`, `view_decisions`, `view_tasks` take
    `(project, screen, canvas, top)`; the existing lines are ported as they
    were, with every `reversed_if(picked)` removed (the mark column stays).
  - `view_decisions`: only the decision lines are picks; a `why:` line, the
    by-kind count and the friction reports are drawn but not picked.
  - `view_tasks`: paints `graph::styled_lines(…, Some(screen.selected))` as
    before (the graph keeps its dependency marks); the reversed cells it draws
    are the row the canvas lights on its own. Picks are the task lines.
- `fn canvas(board, screen, panel)`: paints the view on a tall canvas (the
  screen's width, 1000 rows), copies its rows from `top + offset` to the screen
  rows `top .. height - 2` (the footer row and the row above it stay the
  frame's), then `light_row`s the selected pick's screen row.
- `pub fn offset_of(picks: &[usize], selected: usize, top: usize, height: usize) -> usize`:
  0 while the selected pick's row is above `height - 2`, else the smallest
  offset that shows it. `scroll_of` is deleted, as is `ViewRows`.
- `top` is `VIEW_ROW` for now.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`**

- `run_with` paints the view on a tall canvas to learn the picks; the number of
  picks bounds the selection (Up/Down, and the clamp after a redraw).
- A click on a screen row adds the viewport's offset (`offset_of`) and selects
  the pick drawn on that row; a row without a pick selects nothing.
- `open_panel` is unchanged (the pick's index is still the lane's or the
  task's).

**`crates/gear-board/src/style.rs`**

- `lane_color` cycles `[Acc, Mag, Warn, Blu]` (cyan, magenta, yellow, blue, the
  M4 prototype's lane colours in its lane order hull, deck, keel, mast).

`crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs` is untouched: `styled_lines` keeps its
selection argument, and only its caller in `frame.rs` was ported.

## Tests

- `src/golden.rs` (the crate's own canvas tests) — `copy_rows_copies_the_cells_and_their_styles`:
  cells, styles and the lit row are copied; a row off either canvas is not
  copied and does not panic.

  follows the lit row.
- `tests/it/decisions_contract.rs` — new `down_moves_over_the_reason_of_a_decision`:
  with two starred decisions that both have a why, `4` then `j` puts the `›`
  on the second decision, not on the why line between them; both reasons are
  still drawn.
- `tests/it/board_contract.rs` — new
  `a_view_scrolls_to_its_selected_pick_and_a_click_keeps_it`: on an 80x12
  screen the seventeenth task (more picks than rows) is drawn on the last row

  `a_click_on_the_top_row_of_a_scrolled_view_picks_the_task_there` (the same
  walk, a click on the view's first row, which picks T-13). The long-graph pty
  test drains its frames by the footer and syncs the no-op clicks on any new
  frame: the heading now scrolls away with the rows above the selected one.
