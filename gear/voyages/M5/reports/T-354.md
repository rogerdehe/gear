# T-354 — report (contract v2)

Under the goal box the folded header now gives, in three rows, the goal check,
the milestone the voyage is on, the time against the plan and the tasks, with
bold labels; `d` flips `screen.detail` and keeps the tab and the selection.
Rows 7–9 of the four folded golden frames are compared again: their `T-354`
skip entries are gone.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` (model and keys):

- `ProjectBoard.goal_check: Vec<(bool, String)>`, the shown voyage's check. It
  is the meta `goal-check:<project>:<milestone>`, a JSON array of
  `{"met": bool, "text": str}`; without it (or with a meta that is not that
  array) the clauses of the voyage's done sentence stand in, every one unmet.
  `frame.rs`'s `done_clauses` moved here (`pub(crate)`), now over the done text
  itself; the Now view's `DONE SENTENCE` list still reads the measured voyage's.
- `run_with`: `d`/`D` flips `screen.detail` — the tab and the selection stay.

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` (the header):

- `folded_rows`, drawn from the row after the goal box, in the prototype's put
  order (a later put over an earlier one): row 1 "Goal check" / `<met> of <all>
  met` / `next: …` at 1 / 15 / 30 and "Milestone" at `mx = max(90, W/2 + 10)`
  with the station that is Now (`▶ S2 … (1 of 3 reached)`), "✔ all N reached"
  or "none set" Dim; row 2 "Time" with the bar at 15 and the words at `16 + n`,
  both Ok when `diff ≥ 0` else Bad, then "Tasks" with the three parts at
  `mx + 11` (Ok / Warn / Dim) and `<merged>/<tasks> merged · …` at `mx + 12 + n`;
  row 3 the `d:` key in Dim. The tab row follows after one blank row.
- The numbers as the contract states them: `used` (elapsed, or
  `ended − departure` once landed), `eta` via the new `duration_minutes` (the
  units of gear-app's `eta_seconds`: `8h`, `1d 4h`), `expected = used +` the
  largest `end_min` of `project.forecast` (`used` when empty),
  `n = max(20, min(40, (W − 60)/2))`, `span = max(eta, expected, used)`, the
  bar with `f64::round_ties_even` cells of `█`, the plan's `│` and the sailing
  voyage's `◆`, the sailing/landed `short` texts with the new `hm` (`0h25m`).
  `hours` stays for the other texts.
- Tests: `tests/it/header_contract.rs` (new, `mod` in `tests/it/main.rs`) — the
  check from the meta and from the done sentence, the three rows' texts, columns
  and styles, the landed voyage with eta 1h that took 1h15m ("took 1h15m of
  1h00m, 0h15m over", Bad), and `d` keeping the selection; `frame.rs`'s unit
  test for `duration_minutes` and `hm`. `tests/it/board_contract.rs`'s
  scrolled-view test draws its view on a screen four rows taller (the head is
  thirteen rows now); `tests/it/golden_contract.rs` drops the four `T-354`
  entries.
