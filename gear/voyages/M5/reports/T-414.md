# T-414 — report

A voyage past its eta keeps getting eta stations until it lands: the markers
no longer stop at 75%, so at 150% of its eta a still-sailing voyage is told
plainly that the eta is passed and by how much.

## What changed

`crates/gear-app/src/station.rs` `fn checkpoints` only, plus the tests the
crate keeps in `crates/gear-app/tests/it/station_contract.rs`.

**The quarters.** The old `for quarter in 1..=3` loop is now
`let max_quarter = i128::from(elapsed) * 4 / i128::from(eta);` and
`for quarter in 1..=max_quarter`: every quarter `q >= 1` the elapsed time has
crossed rings, each once through the same `{key}:eta:{q}` meta (so a late
engine tick still cannot replay a quarter). The `elapsed * 4 >= eta * quarter`
test inside the loop is implied by the loop bound; the i128 arithmetic is
unchanged.

**The label.** `q >= 4` pushes
`"{q*25}% eta (past eta by {elapsed - eta} min)"` instead of `"{q*25}% eta"`
(`elapsed - eta` is in seconds, `/ 60` to minutes; for `q >= 4` the elapsed
time is at or past the eta, so it is never negative). A crossed `q >= 4` sets
`past_quarter`, which makes the station `urgent` whatever the projection says,
and appends the line
`Past the eta: speed up (rebalance lanes, cut waiting, fix rework fast); the eta does not move.`

**Nothing else moves.** The station still needs `left > 0`, so stations stop
when the voyage lands (the "ready to land" item is unchanged), and the
25/50/75% crossings are byte-identical to before.

## Tests (`crates/gear-app/tests/it/station_contract.rs`)

- `a_voyage_past_its_eta_keeps_getting_quarter_stations` — eta 8h: at 9h 0m one
  station, urgent, naming `100% eta`; a second tick at 9h and one at 9h 0m 1s
  ring nothing (one station per quarter); at 10h 0m one station naming
  `125% eta (past eta by 120 min)`, urgent, carrying the "Past the eta: …"
  line; a second tick at 10h rings nothing.
- `a_landed_voyage_gets_no_quarter_station` — watched, then all 5 tasks done:
  the stations opened at 10h (the "ready to land" item, and the `3 done`
  milestone) contain no `% eta`; at 11h nothing rings.
- `the_first_three_quarters_are_unchanged` — eta 4h: 25/50/75% stations still
  open once each, with no `past eta by` and no "Past the eta:" line.
- Every existing station test passes unchanged.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- A late engine tick labels every missed quarter with the same overshoot
  (`elapsed - eta` at the tick), not the overshoot at each quarter's crossing:
  that is what the packet writes, and one station is opened for all of them.
- A hallmark edge, not normal use: an eta of seconds on a voyage days old
  crosses hundreds of thousands of quarters, one `get_meta` per quarter and a
  very long `crossed` list in one body. The course station compresses such a
  run into `a–b min`; the eta quarters do not, per the packet.
