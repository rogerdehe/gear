# T-319 — report

The case trial B04-01 broke (the captain's doorbell named an open urgent item
its own `gear` did not list) now has a test with more items than one batch
holds. The cause was already fixed in T-254 (`crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`,
fn `doorbell_inputs`, retains only the ids of `gear_app::mail::select_batch`);
this task adds the proof and changes no source: the fix held, so
`doorbell_inputs` is untouched.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/tests/it/doorbell_isolation.rs` only, in its T-319 section:

- `world`, `question`, `home_of` and `tick` are used as they are. `engine_item`
  now delegates to a new `item(from, to, thread, body, urgent, at)` helper:
  the batch groups threads per sender and counts urgent threads, so the test
  needs three senders and an `urgent` flag, which the file's helpers did not
  carry. Every existing caller keeps its signature and behaviour.
- `inbox_threads(home)` reads INBOX as the home page prints it: one entry per
  thread, its `[urgent]` mark and the ids it lists.
- `urgent_in_doorbell(text)` reads the `(<k> urgent)` count of a doorbell.
- `an_urgent_item_past_the_batch_is_not_rung_and_enters_it_later` — eight open
  captain items in eight threads from three senders (`p:lane-a`, `p:lane-b`,
  `p:lane-c`), three of them urgent, one thread per message. The reserved slot
  goes to the oldest normal thread (`p:lane-a`), so `per_sender` 2 keeps the
  first urgent thread of `p:lane-a` and pushes its second urgent thread past the
  batch; `p:lane-b`'s third thread falls out as well. After one tick:
  - the doorbell names six items and not the urgent one past the batch;
  - every id it names is listed in the INBOX of `home_of` for the captain, and
    the INBOX holds six threads;
  - the doorbell's `(k urgent)` equals the number of those INBOX threads marked
    `[urgent]` (2).
  After the six shown items are resolved (and the doorbell op confirmed, as the
  T-254 test does), the next tick's doorbell names exactly the two threads that
  then come into the batch — the urgent one and the normal one — and both are in
  the home page under INBOX; its urgent count is 1.

## Out of scope

- `make check` in this tree ignores a `TESTS` filter (`Makefile` line 15: no
  `NEXTEST` filter arg), so the acceptance command ran the whole crate, 707
  tests, ~291 s. Added to the report rather than the M6 backlog: it costs lane
  time, not the product.
- The home page's INBOX header counts threads, not urgent items, and prints no
  `(k urgent)` of its own; the test therefore compares the doorbell's count with
  the `[urgent]` marks, which is what the contract asks.
- `BatchLimits::default()` values (6 threads, 2 per sender) are used as they
  are; the test does not pin the constants, only that the doorbell and the home
  page agree on the same batch.
