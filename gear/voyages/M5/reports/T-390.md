# T-390 — report

The voyage forecast measures each lane by its throughput, so a station's pace and
projected finish are true. On 2026-10-29 the station said pace 0.98x and a finish
at 1248 min at 488 min elapsed, while the lanes had really done 2265 estimated
minutes in those 488: hull 755 at 0.65 min per estimated minute, mast 1510 at
0.32, i.e. a finish near 845 min.

## Cause

`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs` `fn build_reports`: a lane's `done_min` was the
sum of its done tasks' spans, each from the first attempt's `started` to the last
step (`merged`). Review and merge run while the lane is already on its next task,
so those minutes were counted once for the task being merged and again inside the
span of the task the lane had already started: the lane looked slower than it is
and the station projected a finish far behind reality.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs`, `fn build_reports` only; no other design.

- A lane's `done_min` is now the lane's own window: from the **earliest
  `started` of any attempt of the lane's voyage tasks** (done or open) to the
  **latest `handed_in` of the attempts of its done tasks**. A done task with no
  `handed_in` closes the window at its last step (`AttemptTimes::last`), so a
  lane whose attempts go straight from started to settled still measures its
  work. The window is collected in a local `BTreeMap<String, (Option<Ts>,
  Option<Ts>)>` while the tasks are walked and applied to the lane in the pace
  pass; `LaneProjection` keeps its fields.
- A lane with no done task has no window end: `done_min` stays 0, its
  `done_estimate_min` stays 0 and it has no pace, exactly as before.
- `r.measured_actual_min` is now the sum of the lanes' `done_min`, not the sum
  of the task spans.
- Each task's own span (first `started` to its last step) still sets `slowest`,
  the per-task figure the voyage line prints; the burn-up, the scope, the rework
  rounds and the slowest-lane finish are untouched.

## Tests

New, in `crates/gear-board/tests/it/workload_contract.rs` (where this crate keeps
its lane-projection tests):

- `a_lane_is_measured_by_its_throughput_not_the_sum_of_its_spans` — the packet's
  acceptance fixture: one lane, two done tasks of estimate 30, task 1 started at
  0, handed in at 20, merged at 40; task 2 started at 20, handed in at 40, merged
  at 60. `done_min` 40 (the sum of spans would be 80), pace `40/60` = 0.67, the
  voyage pace 0.67, `measured_actual_min` 40. A second lane with open work and no
  done task has `done_min` 0, no pace, and the voyage's `slowest_lane` and
  `projected_min` stay unknown. `slowest` is still T-1 at 40, its own span.

Updated, same file:

- `the_slower_lane_sets_the_finish_and_is_named` — its `deck` fixture hands in at
  30 min and merges at 60. Under the new measure `deck.done_min` is **30**
  (was 60, the span up to the merge) and `deck.pace` **1.0** (was 2.0), so it
  needs **60** at its own pace (was 120); `slowest_lane` is deck at **60** (was
  120) and the projected finish is `elapsed + 60` (was `elapsed + 120`). The
  fixture and the narrative comments now say that deck's minute 30 to 60 was
  review and merge, not lane time; the burn-up expectations are unchanged since
  they still follow the merge time.

Checked and **unchanged**, because their expectations already agree with the new
measure (they encode sequential, non-overlapping lane work where the window
equals the old sum):

- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/voyage_contract.rs`
  `voyage_report_starts_home_and_matches_both_board_views`: T-1's earliest start
  is `NOW` and its attempts never hand in, so its last step (`NOW`+120) still
  measures 120 minutes; the pace 2.0, projected 360 and slowest 120 assertions

- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/station_contract.rs`
  `three_done_uses_exactly_the_shared_pace_projection_and_slowest_lane`: the

  hand in, so the lane's window is still 60 minutes and the station body
  (`pace 2.00x`, `lanes heat 180m`) is unchanged.
