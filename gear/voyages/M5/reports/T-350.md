# T-350 — report

The board can be built for any voyage of the project, and shows that voyage
only (README change 1 and change 6). A landed voyage keeps its data.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` and `crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs` only (no
change to `voyage.rs` was needed: `reports_including_finished` already
measures a finished voyage).

- `Options` gains `pub milestone: Option<String>`; `None` keeps today's choice.
- New `pub fn project_for(tx, project, now, agents, milestone: Option<&str>)`
  holds the body of the old `project_with_agents`, which now calls it with
  `None` (signature and its caller in gear-app `home.rs` unchanged).
- `build_with_agents` (the board) always passes a voyage: `opts.milestone`,
  else the milestone `fn workload_voyage` measures, `None` only for a project
  without a voyage.
- With `Some(m)`: the measured report is `m`'s from
  `voyage::reports_including_finished`; the tasks are every task of `m` and
  nothing of another voyage. A `Cancelled` task stays in its lane's queue
  (T-415: it was cut, not deleted) and is left out of the totals; the graph
  leaves it out too. Decisions, frictions, stations and the forecast follow
  the measured report, i.e. `m`'s.
- With `None` (the captain home page): today's measured voyage and today's
  task rule, unchanged.
- `pub struct VoyageEntry { milestone, title, sailing, departure, ended }`;
  `ProjectBoard` gains `pub voyage_list: Vec<VoyageEntry>` (every voyage of
  `tx.voyages_of_project` with a departure, newest departure first) and
  `pub shown: Option<VoyageEntry>` (the measured one). `sailing` = some task
  of the milestone is not Done, Failed or Cancelled; `ended` = for a landed
  voyage the latest `Merged` time of its tasks' attempts, else `None`
  (helper `fn voyage_entries`, one `attempt_times_of_task` read per task).
- Lanes: a landed voyage gets the lanes of its tasks, retired ones included,
  in `seats_of_project` order, a lane with no seat after them, each with
  `idle_text = Some("retired at the landing")` and no idle reason of today;
  a sailing voyage keeps today's lanes (active seats, retired lanes skipped).
  The graph is filtered to the shown tasks as today (local set renamed
  `graphed` to leave `shown` its new meaning).

## Out of scope

- `ProjectBoard.voyages` (the reports the home page shows) is still
  `voyage::reports`, i.e. the sailing voyages only: the packet did not change
  it, and `voyage_list` is the new complete list.
- The JSON of `to_json` does not carry `voyage_list`/`shown`: nothing shows
  them yet (no frame), and the packet names no output change.
- gear-cli has no `--milestone` flag yet: the board always shows
  `opts.milestone`, which only the API can set today.

## Rework (attempt 2)

`origin/dev` (`3efa195b`, with T-415) merged into `mast/t350`. Attempt 1 had
been written before T-415 and took `Cancelled` tasks out of the task list, which
also took them out of the lane's queue; the merge kept T-415's totals guard, and
`board_contract::cancelled_tasks_are_not_counted_in_the_totals` then failed on
the missing `T-6`…`T-9`.

Fix, in `crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` only: with `Some(m)` the tasks are every
task of `m` again, cancelled ones included, so a cancelled task reaches its
lane's queue; the T-415 guard in the totals loop (already there after the
merge) leaves it out of `totals.tasks`/`done`, and `is_terminal()` keeps it out
of `left_count`/`left_min`. `fn graph::tasks` still leaves it out of the graph.

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/voyage_select_contract.rs`,
`the_board_built_for_a_landed_voyage_keeps_its_data`: the lane of M1 now reads
`["T-1", "T-4"]` (the merged task and the one it cut), while `totals.tasks` stays
1 and the graph stays `["T-1"]`.
