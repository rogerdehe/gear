# T-348 — report

A task's actual minutes on the board are the time its lane worked on it, not
the clock since its first delivery.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — a new private helper `worked_s_of(rounds)`:
the sum of `active_s` over the rounds whose `role` is `dev` or `rework`, and
`None` when the task has no such round (so a task nobody worked on is not a
measured zero). `actual_min_of` reads `tx.rounds_of_task` first and returns
`worked / 60` — the integer division of the summed seconds — when the helper
answers; a task with none of those rounds keeps the old span, from its first
`started` to its merge or now.

**`crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs`** — `History` now carries the task's rounds
(fetched in `History::of`), and `actual_of` uses the same helper: with a dev or
rework round the actual is `worked / 60`, otherwise the unchanged span. The
pair it returns is still `(actual, merged)`, so `GraphTask.finished` stays the
merge time.

`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs` (the home page's pace) is untouched, and the
Time and tokens view needed no change: it reads `TaskBoard.actual_min`.

## Test

New, in `crates/gear-board/tests/it/board_contract.rs`:
`a_task_is_measured_by_the_time_its_lane_worked_on_it`. Two tasks delivered
three hours ago on the same lane. T-1 has a dev round of `active_s` 1500 and a
rework round of 600, so its `TaskBoard.actual_min` and its
`GraphTask.actual_min` are both 35 (2100 s / 60), not the 180 of its span. T-2
has no round and keeps the span: 180 in both.

One existing fixture followed: `decisions_contract::the_time_and_tokens_view_
shows_the_work_of_every_task` inserts a `dev` round for its 44-minute task but
never set its active time, so the board began reading 0 minutes. The fixture
now seeds `set_round_active(&key, 44 * 60)`, which is what that round worked;
its assertions are unchanged.
