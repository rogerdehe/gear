# T-401 — report

A staffing change after departure is authorized by a confirmation, not by
pasted words (user, 2026-10-29). T-392 made `gear admin init` refuse it
without `--user "<the user's words>"`, and GEAR is not to store the user's
words: the flag is now `--confirmed`, and the starred `staff` decision says
`confirmed by the user`. A sensitive change (goal, bar, staffing) happens only
when the user asks for it, and then the captain confirms it with the user.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs` (`AdminOp::Init`): `--user <words>` is gone
  and `#[arg(long)] confirmed: bool` ("The user asked for this staffing change
  and confirmed it") is passed through as `confirmed: bool` in place of
  `user: Option<&str>`. The blank-words normalisation of T-392's rework is
  gone with it, and `--check` reports the same refusal unless `--confirmed`
  is passed. `gear admin init --help` shows the new flag.
- `crates/gear-app/src/admin.rs`: `init`, `init_with_home`, `init_checked` and
  `re_register` take `confirmed: bool`; a mid-voyage staffing change is
  refused with `StaffLocked` unless confirmed, and confirmed it writes one
  starred `staff` decision whose `why` is `confirmed by the user` (no words
  stored). `staffing_refusal` now ends
  `— change staffing only when the user asks; confirm it with them, then run init again with --confirmed`.
- `skills/gear/references/captain.md`: T-392's sentence in the kickoff section
  becomes "Staffing (`[quota]`, `[[lanes]]`, `[temps]`, `[models]`) is agreed
  at kickoff; mid-voyage init refuses a change to it until you pass
  --confirmed.", and the section on talking to the user gained the rule "Ask
  the user to confirm (a dialog) only when they ask you to change the goal,
  the bar or the staffing; never ask anything otherwise, and never change
  those on your own." (placed at the end of step 3, "Confirm with the user, in
  chat, in two steps", which is the section on talking to the user).

## Tests

- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/staffing_lock_contract.rs`: the T-392 staffing
  tests run with `confirmed: true` in place of the user's words;
  `a_confirmed_change_applies_and_leaves_one_starred_decision` asserts the
  decision's why is exactly `confirmed by the user`; the refusal test asserts
  the text ends with the `--confirmed` sentence. T-392's blank-words
  regression is removed, along with the code it covered.
- `crates/gear-cli/tests/it/admin_init_contract.rs`: `check_cmd` takes
  `confirmed: bool` and passes `--confirmed`;
  `check_reports_a_staffing_change_of_a_departed_voyage` asserts the
  `--confirmed` ending of the refusal and that `--check --confirmed` exits 0
  as `config ok`; new
  `the_flag_is_confirmed_and_the_users_words_are_gone` asserts `--confirmed`
  parses and `--user` is refused by clap (exit 2).
- The `init_with_home` call sites of the other crates' tests (gear-engine,
  and the remaining gear-app ones) pass `false` where they passed `None`.

## Acceptance
