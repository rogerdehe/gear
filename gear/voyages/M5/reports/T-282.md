# T-282 — report

A reviewer is never left to guess which contract governs after a mid-task bump
(M4.1 T-227, M-3956): the direction packet now carries the task's current
contract as text, and the hand-in review packet says when the task's contract
moved past the version the hand-in was made against.

## What changed — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` only

**`fn job_packet`, direction branch**

- The task row (`saved`) is already read there; its `contract_version` is
  taken from that row and `tx.get_contract(project, task, row.contract_version)`
  is read for `goal` and `accept` (a missing row or missing task leaves them
  empty; the version is then `1`).
- The sentence `Read the contract (gear --show {task}) and the diff from …`
  becomes `Read the contract below and the diff from {remote}/{integration}`.
- The packet ends with:

      Contract v{n} (the task's current contract; the lane works to it — judge
      against this text, not an older version `gear --show` may name):
      Goal: {goal}
      Accept: {accept}

  (one line for the heading, then `Goal:` and `Accept:` on their own lines),
  after the existing hand-in sentence.

**`fn review_packet_for`**

- Reads the task's `contract_version` (from the task row it already loads for
  the repo) and passes it to `review_packet` as `current: i64`.

**`fn review_packet`**

- Takes `current: i64` after `version: i64`. When `current > version` it adds,
  directly after `Contract: v{version} (the version this hand-in was made
  against)`:

      Newer contract: v{current} was written after this hand-in; judge against
      v{version} and log what v{current} changes.

  (one line). At `current <= version` the packet is byte-for-byte as before;
  nothing else in either packet changed.

## Tests — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs` (where the crate keeps them)

- `the_direction_packet_carries_the_tasks_current_contract` — the world's task
  moved to `contract_version = 3` with a v3 contract (`the third goal` /
  `the third criteria`), then `job_packet(…, direction = true)`: the packet
  carries `Contract v3 (the task's current contract`, `Goal: the third goal`,
  `Accept: the third criteria`, and no longer contains
  `Read the contract (gear --show`.
- `the_review_packet_says_when_the_task_moved_to_a_newer_contract` — attempt
  adopted at v2 on a task at v3: the packet carries the v2 line and
  `Newer contract: v3 was written after this hand-in; judge against v2 and log
  what v3 changes.`; with the task back at v2 it carries no `Newer contract:`.

Existing packet tests needed no change: none pinned the old
`Read the contract (gear --show …)` words, and the review packet's text is
unchanged when no newer contract exists.
