# T-335 — report

Every confirmed goal change is now in the board's model (for S4's red header
line) and listed in the voyage report (M5 kickoff, S3: "every change shows in
red on the board and is listed in the voyage report").

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`:

- `GoalChangeBoard { at, what, why }` (`Debug, Clone, PartialEq, Eq`), the
  measured voyage's confirmed goal change as the board carries it.
- `ProjectBoard.goal_changes: Vec<GoalChangeBoard>`, filled by
  `goal_changes_of`: the measured voyage's `tx.decisions_of(project, voyage)`
  of kind `user` and source `gear`, in the order they appeared (oldest first,
  the store's `ORDER BY seq`); empty without a measured voyage.
- The S4 header draws them red; this task only fills them, nothing draws yet.

`crates/gear-app/src/voyage.rs`:

- `Report.goal_changes: Vec<DecisionLine>`: the same decisions of the reported
  voyage.
- `render` prints a part `Goal changes (<M>)` right after `Done (<M>)`: one
  `  <decision_line>` per change, or `  no goal change`, then an empty line.
  The part count in the doc comment is seven now (the later parts renumbered).

Tests:

- `crates/gear-board/tests/it/decisions_contract.rs`:
  `the_goal_changes_are_the_gear_user_decisions_of_the_measured_voyage` — the
  measured voyage's two gear `user` decisions are listed oldest first, a gear
  decision of another kind is not a goal change, and the older voyage's goal
  change is not on the board.
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/voyage_report_contract.rs`:
  `the_report_lists_the_goal_change_of_its_voyage` — `Goal changes (M1)` after
  `Done (M1)`, with the before and the after, another kind not listed, and
  `goal_changes` in the JSON; `a_voyage_without_a_goal_change_says_so` — the
  part says `no goal change`, once.
