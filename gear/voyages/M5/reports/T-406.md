# T-406 — report

A tick now looks at the rounds that can still change — the open ones and the
ended ones whose overrun item is still owed an answer — not at every round the
project ever ran. The store is held for the work of a handful of rounds, not
of 492.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/overrun.rs` and one query in
`crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`, as the packet says.

**`Tx::rounds_to_process(project)`** (new, next to `rounds_of_project`) is the
filtered list:

    WHERE project=?1
      AND (end_at IS NULL OR EXISTS (
        SELECT 1 FROM messages m
         WHERE m.project=rounds.project
           AND m.thread='overrun:'||rounds.task||':'||rounds.role
           AND m.attempt=rounds.attempt AND m.kind='system'
           AND m.state IN ('open', 'waiting')))
      ORDER BY start_at, task, role

The subquery is the same thread and attempt the settled-round resolution in
`fn process` matches (`overrun:T-381`), so a round is selected exactly while
T-381's resolution still has something to do: the tick that looks at it
resolves the item, and the next tick no longer selects the round.

**`fn tick`** reads `tx.rounds_to_process(&project)` instead of
`tx.rounds_of_project(&project)`. Nothing else in the tick changed;
`reconcile_quota_pauses` still runs first on every project, and `fn process`
is untouched except for the test counter below.

**Counting hook.** `#[cfg(test)] static PROCESSED: AtomicUsize` is bumped at
the top of `fn process`; tests only, no release-build cost or behaviour.

## Tests

`overrun.rs`, its own module:

- `a_tick_looks_only_at_the_rounds_that_can_still_change` — a project with 300
  ended rounds and 2 open ones (the seeded dev round plus a second open round):
  one tick calls `process` twice, not 302.
- `an_ended_round_is_looked_at_while_its_overrun_item_is_open` — an ended
  review round with an Open item on `overrun:T-1:review` is looked at (2
  rounds processed, the item is resolved by the settlement path as today) and
  the next tick looks at 1 round: the resolution runs once.
- The existing overrun tests are unchanged and pass, including
  `a_settled_attempt_ends_the_review_round_and_resolves_its_item_once`.

## Out of scope

- A round the engine never closed (`end_at` NULL) is still looked at on every
  tick, even after its attempt was settled — the query keys on `end_at`, as the
  packet says, and `rounds_of_project` below is unchanged. In practice the
  engine closes a round when its attempt settles and the store in the
  incident had 4 open rounds of 492, so this is a bounded cost.
- The two unshown messages in this seat's mailbox (`M-5360` "The reopened-task
  case: leave it, log it in your report", `M-5361` "overrun:T-408:dev ok,
  finish and hand in") concern T-408, not this task. Left as they are: T-406
  changes no reopened-task handling.
- No index was added for `messages(project, thread)`; the `EXISTS` uses the
  `messages_recipient` index prefix for the project filter. An index would be a
  schema change and the packet allows one query, not a migration.
