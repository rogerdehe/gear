# T-355 — report (contract v2)

With `d` the header now unfolds under the goal box: one row per clause of the
goal check, one per station, the time against the plan and the tasks each with
their legend, and `d: fold`. The four `T-355` skip entries left the golden gate
and rows 7–17 of the four open frames are compared. One deviation, from the
packet's station minute: it is asked as a question (M-6058) and handled as the
default stated there.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` only (the crate's tests with it):

- `header` dispatches on `screen.detail` to `folded_rows` (T-354, unchanged) or
  the new `unfolded_rows`, so `d` now changes the frame.
- `HeaderNumbers` + `header_numbers` + `time_bar` hold the numbers and the bar
  T-354 computed, so the two headers read one set: `used`, `eta`, `expected`
  (used + the largest forecast `end_min`), `sailing`, `diff`, and the task
  counts (cancelled ones excluded). `folded_rows` keeps its own `n =
  max(20, min(40, (W − 60)/2))`; `unfolded_rows` uses the contract's
  `n = max(20, min(50, W − 95))`.
- `unfolded_rows`, from the row after the box, in the prototype's put order:
  `Goal check` Bold at 1, then every clause at 15 (`✔ …` Ok, `✗ …` Bad);
  `Milestones` Bold at 1, then every station at 15 (Reached `✔ <id> <what>` Ok,
  Now `▶ <id> <what> — now` Bold, Next and Later `· <id> <what>` Dim); `Time`
  Bold at 1 with the bar at 15 (Ok/Bad as folded) and the words at `17 + n`
  Bold ("used … · planned … · expected … (slowest lane: <lane>), … ahead of
  plan" or "behind plan"; landed: "took … · planned … · … under plan" or "over
  plan"); the legend `█ used   ◆ expected finish   │ planned end` Dim at 15;
  `Tasks` Bold at 1 with the three parts at 15 (Ok / Warn / Dim) and
  `<merged> of <tasks> merged · <going> in progress · <waiting> not started`
  plus ` · moved out: <ids, ", ">` at `17 + n` Bold; its legend Dim at 15; and
  `d: fold` Dim at `W − 20`. The tab row follows after one blank row, as T-354
  left it.
- `tests/it/golden_contract.rs`: the four `T-355` entries removed (rows 7–17 of
  the open frames are compared); four `prototype data: station times` entries
  added for the milestone rows (see below).
- `tests/it/header_contract.rs`: the new acceptance test
  `an_unfolded_tasks_row_names_the_tasks_that_moved_out` (a `move` decision
  `T-1 moved to M6` in the voyage's log, `detail: true`, the Tasks row ends with
  `· moved out: T-1`); `the_d_key_keeps_the_selection_and_draws_the_same_rows`
  became `the_d_key_unfolds_the_header_and_keeps_the_selection` (the frames now
  differ, the selection is still the one lit row).

## The station minute (question M-6058)

The contract asks `hm(at)` on every milestone row. No board data gives it, and
no store either: T-352 v4 (M-6026) reverted `StationBoard.at_min`,
`stations::StationProgress` has no time, the store's `VoyageStation` is
`(id, needs)`, `draft::milestone_stations` parses no time, and
`golden_fixture.rs` reads the fixture's `at_m` and drops it ("neither stored nor
drawn, as v4 requires"). The golden minutes (S1 3h00m, S2 5h30m, S3 8h00m) are
the prototype's fake data: the M5 station estimate sums are 165/295/395 min, its
latest merge is 1h57m, its eta 8h00m.

Default taken (option 2 of the question): the milestone rows draw the station
and its state without the minute — `✔/▶/· <id> <what>`, `— now` on the Now
station — and the gate carries four `prototype data: station times` entries
(rows 10–12, cols 38–71). The rows match everything else of the golden; the
minute is a two-line change in `unfolded_rows` once the model carries it.

## Out of scope

- The station minute, as above: it needs `at_min` back in the model (store +
  plan syntax + fixture), which is T-352 v4's reverted design and out of
  "frame.rs only".
- A voyage with more clauses or stations than the screen has rows: as in the
  prototype, the rows run past the bottom and the tab row goes off screen; the
  canvas drops the writes, nothing panics.
- The packet says "one row per clause at 15" after the `Goal check` row, but the
  prototype and the golden frames put the first clause on the label's row (and
  the first station on the `Milestones` row); drawn as the prototype does.
