# T-355 — report (contract v3)

With `d` the header now unfolds under the goal box: one row per clause of the
goal check, one per station — with the minute the board can tell — the time
against the plan and the tasks each with their legend, and `d: fold`. The four
`T-355` skip entries left the golden gate and rows 7–17 of the four open frames
are compared, with the prototype's own station minutes excluded.

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` (the header):

- `header` dispatches on `screen.detail` to `folded_rows` (T-354, unchanged) or
  the new `unfolded_rows`, so `d` now changes the frame.
- `HeaderNumbers` + `header_numbers` + `time_bar` hold the numbers and the bar
  T-354 computed, so the two headers read one set: `used`, `eta`, `expected`
  (used + the largest forecast `end_min`), `sailing`, `diff`, and the task
  counts (cancelled ones excluded). `folded_rows` keeps its `n =
  max(20, min(40, (W − 60)/2))`; `unfolded_rows` uses the contract's
  `n = max(20, min(50, W − 95))`.
- `unfolded_rows`, from the row after the box, in the prototype's put order:
  `Goal check` Bold at 1, then every clause at 15 (`✔ …` Ok, `✗ …` Bad);
  `Milestones` Bold at 1, then every station at 15 (see below);
  `Time` Bold at 1 with the bar at 15 (Ok/Bad as folded) and the words at
  `17 + n` Bold ("used … · planned … · expected … (slowest lane: <lane>), …
  ahead of plan" or "behind plan"; landed: "took … · planned … · … under plan"
  or "over plan"); the legend `█ used   ◆ expected finish   │ planned end` Dim
  at 15; `Tasks` Bold at 1 with the three parts at 15 (Ok / Warn / Dim) and
  `<merged> of <tasks> merged · <going> in progress · <waiting> not started`
  plus ` · moved out: <ids, ", ">` at `17 + n` Bold; its legend Dim at 15; and
  `d: fold` Dim at `W − 20`. The tab row follows after one blank row, as T-354
  left it.

`crates/gear-board/src/stations.rs` (v3):

- `StationProgress.reached_at_min: Option<i64>`: the latest merge of the
  station's tasks, in whole minutes from the voyage's departure, `None` for any
  state but `Reached` (and for a voyage with no departure on record). `progress`
  reads each task's merge time once (`merged_at`, which `is_merged` now uses).

The milestone rows (v3):

- Reached: `✔ <id> <what> — reached at <hm(at)>` Ok, `— reached` when the model
  has no minute.
- Now: `▶ <id> <what> — now, planned by <hm(at)>` Bold; Next and Later:
  `· <id> <what> — planned by <hm(at)>` Dim, where
  `at = used + (projected − used) × (the left_min of every station up to and
  including it) ÷ (the left_min of all stations)`, rounded down, `projected` =
  the measured voyage's `projected_min`. With no projection or no minutes left:
  `▶ <id> <what> — now` and `· <id> <what>`.

Tests:

- `tests/it/golden_contract.rs`: the four `T-355` entries removed; four
  `prototype data: station times` entries, rows 10–12 cols 52–71, where the
  prototype's fixed minutes (3h00m / 5h30m / 8h00m) differ from the board's
  (2h08m / 4h44m / 5h40m). Only the fifteen `<hm>` cells differ; every word and
  colour of the three rows matches.
- `tests/it/header_contract.rs`: `a_reached_station_says_the_merge_that_reached_it`
  (merges 90 and 95 min after departure → "✔ S1 the box — reached at 1h35m"),
  `a_station_ahead_is_placed_between_the_time_used_and_the_projected_finish`
  (60 used, projected 260, 50 and 150 min left → "— now, planned by 1h50m",
  "— planned by 4h20m"), `a_station_ahead_without_a_projection_is_planned_by_nothing`
  ("▶ … — now", "· …"), the new
  `an_unfolded_tasks_row_names_the_tasks_that_moved_out` (a `move` decision
  `T-1 moved to M6`, the Tasks row ends with `· moved out: T-1`), and
  `the_d_key_keeps_the_selection_and_draws_the_same_rows` became

## Out of scope

- A voyage with more clauses or stations than the screen has rows: as in the
  prototype, the rows run past the bottom and the tab row goes off screen; the
  canvas drops the writes, nothing panics.
- The packet says "one row per clause at 15" after the `Goal check` row, but the
  prototype and the golden frames put the first clause on the label's row (and
  the first station on the `Milestones` row); drawn as the prototype does.
- `projected_min` is read from the measured voyage (`workload`, the source of
  the header's eta), not from `voyage.projected_min` of the shown one: a landed
  shown voyage has no open work and no projection; the contract names one
  voyage and the header has always used the measured one for the plan's eta.
- The `Later` stations draw as the `Next` one (`·`, "planned by"): the
  prototype names only done/now/next, and a voyage can carry more.
- `stations::course` and the captain's texts are unchanged: `reached_at_min` is
  the header's.
