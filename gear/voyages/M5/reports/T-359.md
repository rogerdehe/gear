# T-359 — the Now view is one lane table

## What changed

`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs` only (plus the crate's tests):

- `fn view_now` draws the prototype's table (`W = width`, `top` = the view's
  first row): `tw = max(30, min(52, W - 110))`; `LANE` at 3, `NOW DOING` at 9,
  `STAGE` at 11 + tw, `TIME` at 34 + tw, `LANE DONE` at 46 + tw, `ENDS` at
  76 + tw, all Bold, and `(Enter: the lane's work)` Dim at 86 + tw.
- One row per lane, each a pick: the mark (`›` picked, else a space) in Acc at
  1, the name padded to 5 in its lane colour Bold at 3; the lane's first open
  task in plan order (a Cancelled task is not it, the captain's M-6103), its
  `<id> <title>` cut to tw at 9, the stage words cut to 22 in `Stage::color` at
  11 + tw, `<actual>/<est>m` plus ` ⚠` (Bad) when over at 34 + tw.
- A lane with nothing open: the reason cut to tw + 30 at 9, Dim for a landed
  voyage else Bad — `retired at the landing`, the seat's `idle_text` for
  Quota/Parked/Held/Waiting, `nothing left planned for this lane`, or
  `next <n> waits for <dep> (<dep's lane>, <dep's stage words>)` /
  `next <n> is ready but not delivered`. A lane that helps on the captain's
  instruction keeps its `helping …` line in Acc (T-323); the packet's colour
  rule does not name that case, so the merged behaviour was preserved.
- `mine` = the lane's shown tasks but Cancelled ones; `nd` = its merged ones;
  `left` = the estimates of the others; `k = nd·10/max(1, |mine|)`:
  `█`·k + `░`·(10 − k) in the lane colour at 46 + tw, `<nd> of <|mine|> ·
  <left>m left` plain at 57 + tw.
- While sailing, `~<hm(used + the lane's forecast end_min)>` plain at 76 + tw,
  `used` from the new `fn voyage_used_minutes` (the same measure the folded
  header uses; `folded_rows` now calls it too).
- The balance line on `top + lanes + 1`: first/last by end, first in lane
  order on a tie; `✔ balanced: every lane ends within <hm(gap)>` Ok when
  `gap ≤ 30`, else `✗ not balanced: <last> ends <hm(gap)> after <first>` Bad.
- The rest (needs a look, quota, done sentence) starts on
  `top + lanes + 3`, so T-360's block anchor does not move.

New test helpers in `frame.rs`: `project_tw`, `voyage_used_minutes`, `cut`,
`idle_reason_text` (returns words and style). The old `fn mark` and
`fn current_task` are gone (unused after the table).

## Acceptance and the header gap

The packet says the two `T-359` full-frame skips are removed because rows
13-18/21-26 match. They cannot be removed on `origin/dev`: the board's folded
view starts on row 13 and its open view also on 13, while the prototype's
start on 14 and 22. That is exactly T-356's `Layout.top = tab_row + 3`, and
T-356 is `ready` on mast, not on dev. Removing the skips makes `now_folded`
and `now_open` fail on the header's blank/row order, not on this task's cells.
The captain accepted this reading (M-6115: "Accepted: same as T-362 v4. Keep
the two entries relabelled 'T-359 (T-356 removes it)' with the anchored test
at the prototype's rows; T-356 removes them."). So the table's own rows are
verified now by
`the_now_view_paints_the_prototype_rows`, and the two skips are labelled for
T-356 to remove when it moves the view to the prototype's first row. The
captain's M-6103 (skip Cancelled in the NOW DOING pick) is in the code and its
board test.
