# T-317 — report

A board opened without a project (`--project` absent, no seat) now shows the
session's projects one at a time, and `p` walks to the next one. A board of a
single project — `--project`, a seat, or a store of one project — is unchanged,
and so are the JSON and the text printed once.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`, `fn run_with`**

- New `let mut shown = 0usize;` beside the tab and the selection.
- The board of each frame is `let mut board`; after a successful build, when
  `opts.project` is `None` and the board holds more than one project,
  `shown %= board.projects.len()` and `board.projects.rotate_left(shown)`, so
  `board.projects.first()` — what the frame, the view and the panel read — is
  the shown project.
- The key `p` (and `P`) adds one to `shown`, resets `selected` and the panel and
  redraws. The panel's own rule is unchanged: any key closes an open panel
  first.

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`, `fn header`**

- The title becomes `let mut title`; when `board.projects.len() > 1` it ends
  with ` · p: next of <n> projects` (after `GEAR · <project>` or
  `GEAR · <project> · <milestone>: <title>`). The engine-down warning still
  sits after the title, on the same row, and the clock keeps the right edge.

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/board_contract.rs`**

- `world_with_two_projects()`: one store, session `sess`, projects `a` and `b`,
  each with a `data` lane.
- `a_board_without_a_project_shows_them_one_at_a_time`: in a pty with
  `Options { project: None, .. }`, the first frame's title starts
  `GEAR · a · p: next of 2 projects`; `p` gives `GEAR · b · p: next of 2
  projects`; another `p` comes back to `GEAR · a · …`; `q` leaves.
- `a_board_of_one_project_has_no_project_key`: a one-project store without
  `--project` has a title that starts `GEAR · p` and holds no `next of`; a store
  with a second project still shows only `p` when `--project p` names it.
- `a_board_printed_once_promises_no_project_key` (round 1, F1): `run` on a
  two-project store without a terminal prints one frame titled `GEAR · a` with
  no `next of` in it, and its `--json` still holds both projects.

## Round 1 rework (review F1, attempt 2)

F1: the text the board prints once (a pipe, no terminal) went through the same
`header`, so it promised `p: next of <n> projects` — a key a piped caller
cannot press — against the packet's "the printed-once output is unchanged". The
smallest fix is outside v1's two-function scope, so the captain was asked
(question T-317:4) and the reviewer's fix was taken as the default.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`, `fn print_once`** — in the non-JSON branch,
`board.clone()` with `projects.truncate(1)` is what `frame::frame` draws: every
view of the frame reads `projects.first()`, so the static frame is exactly the
one it drew before the board could switch at all. The JSON branch and the
interactive `run_with` are untouched.

Regression: `a_board_printed_once_promises_no_project_key` above — it fails on
the round-1 candidate with `GEAR · a · p: next of 2 projects  ! engine down …`
and passes now (checked by reverting the fix for one run).
