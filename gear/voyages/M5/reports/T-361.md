# T-361 — report

The rows of the git-style task graph are computed from the store, as the
prototype's `view_graph` computes them: `dev` at column 3, one lane column
every six from 9, the text after the last lane at `info_x`. T-362 paints
them.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/graph.rs`**

- `GraphTask` gains `pub rank: i64` (the store's queue position) and
  `pub started: Option<Ts>` (the earliest `started` of its attempts,
  `History::span().0`). `fn tasks` fills both.
- `pub struct GraphRow { pub cells: Vec<(usize, char, Style)>, pub task:
  Option<String>, pub text: String, pub style: Style }`.
- `pub fn git_rows(tasks, lanes, departure, width) -> Vec<GraphRow>`, the
  prototype's `view_graph` without the painting:
  - the tasks stand in rank order; `col(lane) = 9 + 6·index`,
    `info_x = col(last lane) + 6`,
    `tw = max(30, min(56, width − info_x − 52))`, the lane style is
    `Style::fg(lane_color(..))`; a time is `(ts − departure)/60` minutes.
  - `git_info(t, tw)` = id padded to 7 + title cut to `tw` and padded +
    two spaces + `Stage::words(t)` cut to 36 and padded + `time`
    (`"<actual>/<est>m"` when actual > 0 else `"<est>m"`) + `" · <n> rounds"`
    when rounds > 1.
  - waiting = no `started` and stage not `Moved`: two rows each, the `○`
    row (lane colour bold, text, Dim) over the `┆` row (lane colour, no
    text, plain).
  - events = `(started minutes, 0)` and `(finished minutes, 1)`, sorted by
    `(minutes, kind)` from the largest down with a stable sort, so equal
    minutes keep the plan's order.
  - a merge: `verticals(t)` + `●` Ok bold at 3 + `─` in the lane colour for
    4..col + `╯` at col, text `info + "  merged <clock of finished>"`,
    plain, and then t joins the open branches.
  - a start: when t is open first its stage row (`symbol()`,
    `Style::fg(color()).and_bold()`, text bold); then the fork `verticals(t)`
    + `├` Ok at 3 + `─` 4..col + `╮` at col, no task, `7 spaces + "starts
    +<hm>" (+ " after <deps, ", ">")`, Dim, and t leaves the open branches.
  - `verticals(skip)` = `│` in its lane colour at each open task's column,
    but the skipped one. Duplicate columns are kept in the cell list, as the
    prototype's `put` writes them in that order (the painter's last write
    wins).

## The one place I did not follow the packet's words

The packet says the waiting tasks go "the last to run first (reverse rank)";
the acceptance says the first row is T-265's, and the prototype's
`view_graph` iterates its waiting list in its T order, which is rank
ascending (T-265, T-266, T-267). Reverse rank would put T-267 first and fail
the acceptance. I asked (M-5626) and went with the acceptance: waiting tasks
in rank order. Nothing else in the packet is changed.
