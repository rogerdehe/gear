# T-343 — report

A test helper builds the store the golden frames were drawn from: it reads the
prototype's own `gear/voyages/M5/board/golden/fixture.json` and seeds the
project, the voyages and every task through the store's API, so the board under
test and the golden frames share one copy of the agreed data.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/tests/it/golden_fixture.rs`** (new)

- `pub fn load() -> (Store, i64)` — reads the fixture at
  `concat!(env!("CARGO_MANIFEST_DIR"), "/../../gear/voyages/M5/board/golden/fixture.json")`
  with `serde_json` into small `Deserialize` structs/tuples (unknown keys such as
  `BURN`, `DECISIONS`, `DONE` are ignored; only `now`, `review_merge_m`, `when`,
  per voyage `LANES`/`T`/`V` are read), and returns the store and `now`.
- `fn find_now(wanted)` — walks the day around `1_793_197_920` minute by minute
  and returns the one second whose `gear_board::idle::clock(ts)` is the
  fixture's `"now"`, so the clocks are right in any time zone without touching
  `TZ`.
- Seeds, in one write transaction:
  - `testing::seed_project("gear", "s")`; the captain seat (`w1:p0`, `claude`);
    one lane seat per name in the order names first appear in the `LANES` lists
    (hull, mast, keel — lane `i` at `w1:p<i+1>`, `pi`).
  - Voyage `i` (M5, M4.2, M4.1): `departure = now − used_m·60` while sailing,
    else `now − 86_400·i − used_m·60`; `done`/`bar`/`eta` from `V`,
    `departure_tasks` and `departure_estimate_min` over the tasks whose stage is
    not `moved`; `d(m) = departure + m·60` is the voyage's clock.
  - Every task tuple, rank counting its 1-based position through all voyages in
    order; a `moved` task seeds nothing. Each other one gets the task row
    (state by stage: done → Done, review/passed → Verifying, rework/working →
    Running, deps/queued → Ready), the version-1 contract with the title as its
    goal, and the attempts and step times the stage asks for, with the `when`
    minutes as `s`/`m` and `review_merge_m` for the review's stretch before the
    merge:
    - done — attempts `1..=rounds` (all Rejected but the last Accepted); attempt
      1 Delivered and Started at `d(s)`; each Rejected attempt HandedIn at
      `d(s)` with a failed verdict; the last attempt Started at `d(s)` (when it
      is not attempt 1), HandedIn/Verdict/Settled at `d(m − review_merge_m)` and
      Merged at `d(m)`, with a passed verdict.
    - review — Verifying, attempt 1 Submitted, Started at `d(s)`, HandedIn at
      `now − 12·60`.
    - rework — Running, attempts `1..=rounds`: the rejected ones Started and
      HandedIn at `d(s)` with a failed verdict, the last Started at `now`.
    - working — Running, attempt 1 Started at `d(s)`.
    - passed — Verifying, attempt 1 Accepted, Started at `d(s)`,
      HandedIn/Verdict and a passed verdict at `d(s + actual)`.
    - deps and queued — Ready, no attempt.
  - For a task with `tokens_k > 0`: the dev round
    (`(gear, task, 1, "dev")`, seat `gear:<lane>`, generation 1, `pi`, no
    model, start `d(s)`), `set_round_active(actual·60)`, the tokens
    (`input = floor(tokens_k·0.75)·1000`, output/cache 0, `cost·0.75`, source
    `agent`, read at `now`), and `close_round(d(s) + actual·60)` unless the
    stage is working or rework. The review round is T-344's, as is the
    decisions/frictions/quota/reviewer data.
- Three tests carry the packet's acceptance: the clock is `"14:32"`, gear has
  the 12 M5 tasks with T-262 Verifying and T-265 Ready; T-257's Started is
  `now − 207·60` and its Merged `now − 181·60`; T-260 has two attempts, the
  first Rejected; T-264's dev round is open with `active_s` 1320 and input
  285 000; T-182 (moved from M4.2) has no row.
