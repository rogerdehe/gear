# T-426 — report

A Standby reviewer that quit its agent to resume an earlier conversation is no
longer released as `agent-gone` while the switch is under way: the `Standby`
arm of the agent-gone sweep reads `engine.session_step.<seat id>` and skips the
seat while that step is one of its own generation and is neither `done` nor
`failed`.

## What changed

`crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs` only:

- `fn a_session_switch_is_in_flight(store: &mut Store, seat: &SeatRow) -> GearResult<bool>`
  — no `engine.session_step.<seat id>` meta, or one that does not parse, is no
  switch; a `generation` other than the seat's is no switch; a `step` of `done`
  or `failed` is no switch; every other step (`wait_exit`, `start_resume`,
  `fallback_exit`, `fallback_enter`, `fallback_wait_exit`, `start_fallback`) is
  a switch in flight.
- The `SeatState::Standby` arm of `release_finished_reviews`: after the pane is
  read (unchanged: a seat without a live binding still goes to the dead-temp
  sweep, a pane that cannot be read is still an observation failure) the arm
  skips the seat when `a_session_switch_is_in_flight` is true, exactly as it
  skips a seat whose pane cannot be read. The `STANDBY_AGENT_GONE_S` grace, the
  `herdr.agent_get` reading and
  `release_one_ending_its_review(…, "agent-gone")` are unchanged, and so is
  every other arm.

## Tests

New in `release::tests`:

- `a_standby_reviewer_switching_its_session_is_not_released` — Standby since
  `T0`, checked at `T0+120` (past the 60 s grace), pane a bare shell, session
  step `wait_exit` of generation 1: the seat stays `Standby`, its
  `engine.review_pending` review is kept, no pane is closed.
- `a_standby_reviewer_whose_session_step_is_done_is_released` — the same with
  step `done` of its generation: `Releasing`, one pane closed.
- `a_standby_reviewer_whose_session_step_is_of_an_older_generation_is_released`
  — the same with step `wait_exit` of generation 0: `Releasing`, one pane
  closed.

Every existing `release::` test passes (50 of 50, none changed).

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- `crate::session_preparation_pending` (`lib.rs`) asks the same question of the
  same meta (same generation, step not `done`/`failed`) to hold the doorbell.
  The new helper is a second, narrower reading of it; a later task could make
  one function of them.
- The `SeatState::Active` arm has no such guard. A reviewer that reaches the
  session switch while `Active` (its job still under review) and whose agent is
  gone would still be released as `agent-gone`; the packet names the `Standby`
  arm only, so it is left as it was.
