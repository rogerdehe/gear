# T-325 — report

The kickoff now asks, once, whether a lane parked on a long quota wait keeps
its agent or is released until the reset (user, 2026-10-26/27). This is the
config half; the engine's release and comeback are the two hull tasks after it.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/config.rs`**

- `WaitPolicy { Keep, Release }` next to `QuotaPolicy`, with
  `#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq, Deserialize, Default)]`,
  `#[serde(rename_all = "lowercase")]` and `#[default] Keep`.
- `Models` gains `pub on_wait: WaitPolicy` (set to `WaitPolicy::default()` in
  `impl Default for Models`), so it is read from `[models] on_wait` and an
  absent key parses to `Keep`.

**`crates/gear-app/src/kickoff.rs`** — `skeleton_for` writes, right after the
`on_quota = …` line:

    on_wait = "keep"              # "release": a lane parked on a quota wait exits until the reset; its worktree and session are kept

**`skills/gear/references/captain.md`** — the example `[models]` block carries
`on_wait = "keep"` after `on_quota`; the kickoff sheet item "quota policy"
became "quota policy and wait policy (`on_wait`: keep the lanes' agents through
a quota wait, or release them until the reset)", and the agree-with-the-user
list gained the wait-policy line beside the quota policy one.

## Tests

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` —
`wait_policy_parses_and_defaults_to_keep`: a `[models]` block without `on_wait`
parses to `WaitPolicy::Keep`, `on_wait = "release"` to `Release`, and
`on_wait = "sometimes"` is refused with `ErrorCode::BadFormat`.

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/kickoff_contract.rs` —
`the_skeleton_asks_the_wait_policy_and_keeps_the_agent`: the skeleton contains
`on_wait = "keep"`, still parses as a config, its `models.on_wait` is `Keep`,
and the line sits directly below the `on_quota = ` line.
