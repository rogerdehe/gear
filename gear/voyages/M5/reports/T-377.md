# T-377 — report

A review round's budget is no longer built from rounds that were never
measured (F-122, F-123, F-126, F-128, F-129, F-131, F-132: every review round
got the 10 min floor and overran, while its report read "active 0 min · tokens
0").

## What changed

**`crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`** — `fn review_budget_min` only; no other
design.

- The query adds `AND active_s > 0` to its `WHERE`: the sample is the last 10
  ended review rounds of the same model *that measured activity*. A round
  whose usage was never read carries `active_s` 0 and says nothing about how
  long a review takes; before, those zeros filled the sample and the median
  fell to 0, so every budget was the 10 min floor.
- The rest of the function is as it was: fewer than three such rows give
  `DEFAULT_REVIEW_BUDGET_MIN` (30), otherwise 1.5 × the median, at least 10.
- The doc comments of `round_budget_min` and `review_budget_min` say the
  sample is the measured rounds only.

## Tests

`crates/gear-store/tests/it/v6_contract.rs`, where the crate keeps its
round/budget tests:

- `a_review_round_that_measured_no_activity_does_not_enter_the_median` — ten
  ended review rounds with `active_s` 0 give `DEFAULT_REVIEW_BUDGET_MIN`, 30.
  (Before the change this was 10: the median of ten zeros.)
- `only_the_measured_review_rounds_build_the_median` — three rounds of 600 s
  and seven of 0 give 15 (median 10 min × 1.5). (Before the change the seven
  zeros pulled the median to 0 and this was 10.)
- The existing budget tests pass unchanged
  (`the_budget_of_a_review_round_is_measured_and_falls_back_to_thirty`,
  `the_budget_of_a_dev_round_is_the_estimate_of_the_task`,
  `the_budget_of_a_rework_round_is_the_estimate_of_the_verdict_or_half_the_estimate`).

## Out of scope

- Why a Codex reviewer's rounds read `active_s` 0 (its usage not read) is not
  this task, as the packet says. Those rounds still record 0; they are now
  merely left out of the budget's sample.
- A round of exactly 0 s of measured activity is indistinguishable from an
  unmeasured one (`active_s` is `NOT NULL DEFAULT 0`); both are excluded.
  Telling them apart would need a "measured" flag, which is a design change.
