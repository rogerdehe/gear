# T-336 — report

The captain's home page and the two captain station items now carry the voyage's
goal, its bar and how far the voyage is (the current station and the tasks left),
through the one text every captain output uses: `gear_board::stations::goal_text`.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/home.rs` — `build_with_agents`, captain only**

- After the voyage report lines (`gear_board::voyage::reports`) and before the
  empty line that follows them, the page pushes
  `gear_board::stations::goal_text(tx, &ctx.project, &milestone)?` for
  `milestone = crate::plan::current_voyage(tx, &ctx.project)?`.
- Nothing is pushed when the voyage is empty or `goal_text` is `None`, so a
  captain whose project has no departed voyage (and a lane or temp seat, which
  never enter this block) sees the page unchanged.

**`crates/gear-app/src/station.rs` — `checkpoints`**

- New helper `fn with_goal(tx, project, milestone, today)`: the voyage's
  `goal_text` as the first line, the text of today on the next line; the text
  of today alone when `goal_text` is `None`.
- Both captain messages use it: the urgent "Voyage <M> is ready to land" item
  and the station report (including its `Past the eta:` line, which now follows
  the goal line).

## Tests

- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/voyage_contract.rs` —
  `the_captain_home_page_carries_the_goal_and_the_current_station`: a departed
  voyage with two stations shows
  `Your goal, voyage M1: done: … · bar: … · station S1 (0 of 2 reached): … · 2 tasks
  left in S1 · 2 tasks left in the voyage. Judge every decision against it.` right
  after the report lines; without stations the same line ends
  `· 2 tasks left in the voyage. Judge every decision against it.`; a lane's page
  has no such line.
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/station_contract.rs` — the existing body tests now
  expect the goal line first and today's text second: the quarter station is two
  lines (goal, today), the coalesced station is two lines, the exact body test
  carries the goal line as a prefix, and the landing item asserts the goal line
  first and the landing text on the next line.
