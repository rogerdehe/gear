# T-331 — report (contract v3)

After departure, a voyage's `title` and its stations are guarded exactly like
its `done:`, `bar:` and `eta:` already are since T-410: an apply that changes
them is refused without `--confirmed` and nothing is written; with it the
change applies and records a starred decision of kind `user` with
"confirmed by the user". A deposited voyage with no stored stations takes its
first list freely.

This replaces the contract-v2 attempt (GOAL_LOCKED): its tree is superseded,
as the captain directed. This repository refuses force-pushes, so the old
attempt's commits remain in the branch's history behind a `-s ours` merge; the
net diff against `origin/dev` is only the two files below.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs`** — `fn record_voyages`, inside the existing
`if departed.contains(id)` guard:

- The T-410 field loop keeps its `done`, `bar`, `eta` entries and now also
  compares `title` (stored title vs `milestone_parts` title) and, when
  `tx.voyage_stations(project, id)` is not empty, `stations` as text
  `<id>: <needs>` joined by `"; "` (the new `joined_stations` helper). Both go
  through the same refusal (`bad(0, "milestone {id}: {field} changed after
  departure (stored \"{old:.40}\", got \"{new:.40}\"): the goal, the bar and the
  eta change only when the user asks; confirm it with them, then apply again
  with --confirmed")`) and the same starred `user` decision with
  `field of <id> changed from "…" to "…" | why: confirmed by the user`.
- The field order is `done`, `bar`, `eta`, `title`, `stations`: appending the
  two new fields keeps every T-410 refusal text that names the first changed
  field exactly as merged (e.g. the grandfathered test still reports `done`).
- A departed voyage whose stored station list is empty skips the stations
  comparison, so its first list applies without `--confirmed`; the later
  `milestone_stations` parse and `set_voyage_stations` are unchanged.
- The doc comments of `apply`/`record_voyages` name T-331.

No other code changed: `goal.rs`, `GoalLocked`, the `lib.rs` export and the
CLI test edits from the v2 attempt are gone.

## Tests

In `crates/gear-app/tests/it/voyage_contract.rs` (T-410's tests untouched and
passing):

- `a_departed_voyages_title_and_stations_need_the_users_confirmation` — a
  departed M1 with `S1`/`S2`: a title-only change is refused naming
  `title changed after departure (stored "delivery v0.1.0", got "delivery
  v0.2.0")`, and the plan version, title, tasks and stations stay; a
  stations-only change is refused naming `stations changed after departure`,
  with the stored list intact; the same draft with `run_confirmed` applies both
  and records two starred `user` decisions with the exact `what`/`why`/`line`.
- `a_departed_voyage_without_stations_gets_its_first_list` — a departed M1
  with no stations takes `S1` without confirmation and with no decision; the
  unchanged list applies again with no decision; changing it afterwards is
  refused and writes nothing.
- `a_whitespace_only_difference_is_no_change` — surrounding spaces on `done:`,
  the title and a station line keep the apply free of confirmation.

## Out of scope (noticed, not changed)

- The refusal text still says "the goal, the bar and the eta change …" and does
  not name titles/stations; "the same refusal" was requested, so it is kept
  byte for byte (T-410's tests assert it).
- Stations compare trimmed text, not collapsed text, as the packet says
  ("trimmed"): a difference inside a `needs` line is a change, only surrounding
  whitespace is not. The existing T-410 fields use `trim()` too.
- The message truncates each side at 40 characters (T-410), so a long station
  list is abbreviated in the refusal.
- A confirmed change records its decision only the first time that exact
  transition occurs (`insert_decision_if_new`, the T-410 F1 behaviour); a later
  repeat of the same old→new transition records nothing.
- The `--confirmed` flag confirms every changed protected field in the draft,
  including the new title and stations; the CLI test is unchanged and passes.
