# T-258 — a direction check never outlives its attempt's hand-in

`finish_direction_results` (crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs) now treats a job in
state `running` whose attempt is no longer `AttemptState::Started` as dead:

- the job is closed whether or not a result came: its meta becomes
  `{"checked_sha": <the job's sha>}`, exactly as a finished check writes it, and
  its seat goes back to `Standby` with its task cleared under today's condition
  (seat `Active`, same generation, same task);
- a result that did arrive for it is never sent to the lane; when it failed,
  only the captain's `System`/`Info` message is written.

A job on an attempt that is still `Started` behaves exactly as before: it waits
for its result and reports a failure to the lane and to the captain.

## Tests (crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs)

- `a_hand_in_closes_a_direction_check_that_never_answers` (fixtures of
  `a_hand_in_takes_the_seat_a_direction_result_frees`): the hand-in hands in
  while the check runs and the check never answers; `finish_direction_results`
  closes the job with its own sha, the seat is `Standby` holding no task, and
  the hand-in's review takes that seat on the next `start_reviews` pass.
- `a_late_failing_direction_result_reaches_only_the_captain`: a failing `F1`
  result replayed with the job's sha after the hand-in writes nothing to the
  lane (`actionable_for` is empty) and exactly one engine message, to the
  captain, kind `System`, state `Info`, in the job's direction thread.
- `a_running_direction_check_on_a_started_attempt_keeps_waiting`: with no
  result and the attempt still `Started`, the job stays `running` and the seat
  stays `Active`.

`direction_checks_follow_pushes_and_report_results` used to hand the task in
mid-check and then rewind the attempt to `Started` to keep the same job alive;
that rewind is no longer possible (the hand-in closes the job and its seat goes
to the hand-in's review), so that step is gone and the test keeps the attempt
`Started` throughout. Its subject — pushes while a check runs, an old result,
a new check for the new sha, a failing result reaching the lane — is unchanged,
and the hand-in itself is covered by the three tests above plus
`a_hand_in_takes_the_seat_a_direction_result_frees`.

Validation: `make check CRATE=gear-engine` — 628 tests, all passed.

Out of scope: a job in state `starting` is untouched here; `yield_starting_direction_jobs`
already yields it to a hand-in. A direction job whose attempt ends without a
hand-in (cancelled, failed start) is closed by this same rule, which the packet
asks for.
