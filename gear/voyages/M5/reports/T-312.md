# T-312 — report

A rework round's budget grows with the blocking findings it carries: 8 minutes
per blocking finding, never below today's rule.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs` — `fn settle_review`**

- In the rework branch (the one that writes `rework_note`), before
  `tx.update_task(&task_row)`, the task row now carries the budget of the next
  round:
  - a reviewer's fail that is not stale (the branch writing
    `blocking.join("\n")`): `rework_estimate_min = Some(max(10, (estimate + 1) / 2, 8 × blocking.len()))`,
    with `estimate = task_row.estimate_min` or
    `gear_store::DEFAULT_ROUND_BUDGET_MIN` when the task has none;
  - a stale pass (the `stale` branch: a pass that completes an older contract
    than the task carries now) sets `rework_estimate_min = None` — today's
    rule of half the estimate, at least 10.
- Nothing else in the settlement changes. A captain's fail verdict with
  `estimate=` is untouched: `plan.rs` writes its own minutes after its settle,
  so they still win over the 8-per-finding rule.

`round_budget_min` itself is unchanged: it already returns
`rework_estimate_min` for attempt n >= 2 when it is set.

## Tests

**`crates/gear-app/tests/it/settle_review_contract.rs`** (new section
"rework budget (T-312)", helpers `fail_world` and `rework_budget` that read
`Tx::round_budget_min(…, 2, "rework", …)`):

- `a_rework_budget_grows_with_its_blocking_findings` — a 30-min task with five
  blocking findings gets 40; with one it gets 15 (half of 30 is still the
  floor); a 100-min task with two gets 50 (half beats 16).
- `a_rework_budget_without_an_estimate_starts_from_the_default` — no estimate:
  four findings give 32, above half of the default 30.
- `a_captains_fail_verdict_keeps_its_own_minutes` — a captain's fail verdict
  with `estimate=25` applied through `plan::apply` leaves
  `rework_estimate_min = Some(25)` and `round_budget_min` 25, over the 16 the
  two findings would give.
