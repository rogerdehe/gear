  The contract writes `"<time>: <before> → <after> …"` and the prototype (`prototype.py:193`)
  puts `at` verbatim, so a change from a previous day shows `12:40` without its date. This
  matches the decisions log's own shortening and still "says when" for the live board, so it
  is not an error in normal use at the DELIVERABLE bar. Smallest change if the captain wants
  the literal tuple: render `time` instead of `clock_of(time)`.

## Set aside

- A `before`/`after` text containing a `"` cannot be parsed unambiguously from the C6
  record, whose fields are unescaped — that ambiguity lives in the captain's record format,
  outside this board task, and ordinary done/bar/eta wording never hits it.
- "Latest" is `changes.last()` over the seq-ordered (insertion-ordered) `goal_changes`,
  not a max over `at`. In normal use goals are applied in time order, so the two agree;
  an out-of-order re-apply is outside normal use.
- `eta`/`title`/`stations` records also draw as "Goal changed", which follows the broader
  T-335/T-410 design that counts every confirmed change as a goal change, not just `done`.
