Reproduction: `reset_from_text("resets in 9999999999999999d 9999999999999999d 120s", 1793102400)` panics with `attempt to add with overflow` in the checked build, independently reproduced with a temporary caller and catch_unwind. Huge floating-point conversions saturate to i64::MAX, then unchecked accumulation overflows; adding relative seconds to now at lines 206/216 is also unchecked. Smallest fix: in duration_seconds reject nonfinite/out-of-window converted values before casting and use checked_add for accumulation and relative timestamps (roughly 10 lines), with a regression in quota_contract.rs. Logged under DELIVERABLE: these astronomical durations are outside normal provider limit text.

F2 [P2, logged] A numeric sentence after a complete duration discards the valid reset — `crates/gear-usage/src/quota.rs:248`. Reproduction: `reset_from_text("Try again in 2 hours. 429 Too Many Requests.", 1793102400)` returns None rather than Some(1793109600), independently reproduced. The parser strips the period, consumes 429 as another duration number, then propagates failure for the unrecognized unit `Too`, losing the completed two-hour duration. Smallest fix: stop consuming after sentence-ending punctuation on a completed group, preserving the accumulated duration (roughly 5 lines), and add this regression. Logged under DELIVERABLE: the supplied/common duration forms work; this particular same-line status suffix is an edge case.
