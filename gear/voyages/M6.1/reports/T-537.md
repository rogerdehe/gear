# T-537 — report (attempt 1)

A verdict may name its candidate by any sha prefix of the stored one, or the
stored one by a prefix of it (M7 backlog, T-457).

## `crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs` (only file changed, no other design)

`fn same_candidate(a: &str, b: &str) -> bool` — true when `a == b`, or when both
are at least 7 characters of hex and one is a prefix of the other, ignoring
ASCII case; false otherwise (so 6 shared characters, or a 7th character that
differs, are a different candidate).

It replaces the comparison at every site the packet names:

| line | site | now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ~324 | `submit_result`: the hand-in candidate against a rejected attempt's stored candidate | `same_candidate(stored, candidate)` |
| ~546 | `reopened` (the T-90 reopening fail after a failed merge) | `same_candidate(&verdict.candidate, candidate)` |
| ~693 | `settle`: the same verdict replayed | `same_candidate(&settled.candidate, &v.candidate)` |
| ~713 | `settle`: the pass-then-fail conflict a captain may make | `same_candidate(&settled.candidate, &v.candidate)` |
| ~743 | `settle`: the verdict against the attempt's stored candidate | `same_candidate(stored, &v.candidate)` |

The refusal at ~743 keeps its words and still names the stored candidate
(`submitted candidate {:?}, not {:?}` with `attempt.candidate`).

## Tests (`crates/gear-app/tests/it/tasks_contract.rs`, where the verdict tests are)

- `a_verdict_names_its_candidate_by_any_sha_prefix` — a fail verdict with the
  full sha settles the attempt stored with its 8-character prefix
  (`7c9be5e8`), and the reverse (stored full sha, verdict prefix) also settles.
- `a_candidate_that_shares_six_characters_is_refused` — `7c9be5` (6 characters)
  and `7c9be5e9` (a 7th character that differs) are both `BadFormat`, and the
  attempt stays Submitted.
- `a_verdict_replayed_with_the_other_length_changes_nothing` — a pass settles,
  then the same pass with the other length returns Ok and leaves the task, the
  attempt and the verdict count unchanged.
- `a_hand_in_of_the_failed_candidate_by_its_full_sha_is_refused` — the site at
  ~324: after a fail on `7c9be5e8`, a rework hand-in of the full sha of the same
  commit is `BadFormat`.
