## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/dispatch.rs`:
  - `assign_text` takes the attempt number as its new second argument:
    `assign_text(task, attempt, version, branch, packet)`. Its text is now
    `Rework <task> attempt <n> (contract v<version>): the review failed your last
    attempt; what to fix is the Rework section at the top of your task packet.

    new wording and says why: a rework says so and names the packet's Rework
    section; the branch and the worktree are already there.

    (`assign_text(task, attempt.n, contract_version, …)`), the comment above the
    call says a rework now says it is a rework, and the call site passes
    `attempt.n`.
  - Tests: `the_two_packet_writings` asserts the exact new text with attempt 2;
    `a_rework_keeps_the_old_branch_line` (the rework dispatch test) asserts the
    new lead sentence and still that no `git switch` is named.

  and `texts_carry_no_screen_marker` call the five-argument form and assert the
  new text.

`first_attempt_text` and `continue_text` are unchanged.
