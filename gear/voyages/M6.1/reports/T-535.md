# T-535 — report

A delivery whose text still sits in the lane's input box is never confirmed by
the agent's idle-to-busy turn, and the Enter that submits it is retried — up to
three times, 30 s apart — instead of once.

## What changed

- `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`:
  - `held_deliveries(store, herdr, project)` (new, `BTreeSet<String>`): for
    every lane Deliver op in `Submitted` or `Unknown` whose seat is Active at
    the op's generation, the binding's pane tail is read
    (`UNSENT_READ_LINES`) and the op id is listed when
    `crate::composer::holds(&tail, text)`. A failed read lists nothing; an op
    with no binding, no text or an empty needle is skipped.
  - `confirm_pending_held(store, project, budget, grace_s, working, held, now)`
    (new): the confirm step with the held set. `confirm_pending_with` keeps its
    signature and delegates with an empty set, so the existing call sites and
    the existing delivery tests are unchanged — the packet's "change ops.rs and
    the tick call sites in lib.rs" only.
  - `shown_delivery(tx, op, held)`: the `agent_turn_key` branch is skipped for
    an op in `held`. Views, hand-ins and the seat's own messages still confirm
    as before. `confirm_late_views` passes an empty set (a late view is the
    proof there; the packet names only the confirm step).
  - `resubmit_unsent`: up to `UNSENT_ENTERS_MAX` (3) Enters per op, each at
    least `UNSENT_RESUBMIT_AFTER_S` after the previous one, only while the tail
    still holds the text and the agent is Idle or Done. `engine.resubmit.<op
    id>` now stores `<count>|<time>`; a value an earlier binary wrote (a bare
    time) counts as one Enter (`resubmit_state`), a value that makes no sense as
    none. After the third the op is left to the unconfirmed-delivery window and
    its flag to the captain. The T-404 retype path is unchanged.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`: the tick builds `held` with
  `ops::held_deliveries` right before the confirm step (after the resubmit step,
  so an Enter that just took is not held) and passes it to
  `ops::confirm_pending_held`; the step is timed like the others.

## Acceptance

Tests in `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs` `mod tests` with `FakeHerdr`:

- `a_turn_does_not_confirm_a_delivery_still_in_the_input_box`: a turn at
  `T0+10` after a submit at `T0+1` leaves the op `Submitted` while the tail
  shows the text (`held` contains the op).
- `a_turn_confirms_the_delivery_once_the_box_is_empty`: the same op with an
  empty tail is confirmed with the evidence `turn p:heat/1 @ <t>`.
- `a_view_still_confirms_a_delivery_whose_text_is_in_the_box`: a held op is
  confirmed by a view of its task and attempt (the turn branch is the only one
  skipped).
- `a_failed_pane_read_holds_nothing`.
- `an_unsent_delivery_gets_up_to_three_enters_and_never_a_fourth`: the first
  Enter at `T0+40`, none at `T0+69`, the second at `T0+70`, the third at
  `T0+100`, and none at `T0+400`; the meta goes `1|…`, `2|…`, `3|…` and stays
  `3|…`.
- `a_bare_time_resubmit_value_counts_as_one_enter`: a stored bare time is one
  Enter, so two more follow and the record becomes `2|…`, then `3|…`.
- `an_unsent_delivery_gets_its_first_enter_after_the_window` (the former
  one-Enter test, adapted to the new meta format).

Commands:

- The whole crate: `cargo nextest run -p gear-engine` — 971 passed, 0 failed
  (the existing delivery, grace and undelivered-delivery contracts included).

## Out of scope

- A held delivery still spends its delivery window and its check budget: the
  packet asks only that the turn not confirm it and that the Enter be retried.
  After the third Enter the existing window and flag decide.
- `held_deliveries` reads lane seats only, as the packet says: a reviewer's
  delivery has its own evidence rule (`review_delivery_evidence`).
- `confirm_late_views` (a failed delivery confirmed by a later view) gets an
  empty held set: the packet names only the confirm step, and a view is its
  proof there anyway.
