The acceptance ("None under 5 samples and the scaled minutes at 5 or more") is phrased in terms of `samples`, the struct the packet fixes cannot distinguish ratio samples, and the `median_ratio > 0` guard covers the no-ratio case, so this does not block; the report discloses it. If the goal's wording is to be taken literally, the smallest fix is to add a `ratio_samples: u32` field to `ModelSpeed` and gate on it — but that changes the fixed struct, so it belongs to the captain's call.
- F2 [P3, logged] `attempt_model_key` is duplicated with a stale "kept here until it lands" comment — crates/gear-app/src/speed.rs:125-129. T-701 already landed `pub fn attempt_model_key(project, task, n)` in `crate::tasks` (tasks.rs:136) with the identical format, so `speed.rs` could call `crate::tasks::attempt_model_key` instead of carrying a private copy that can drift.
