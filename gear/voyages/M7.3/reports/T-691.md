# T-691 — Config and commit message for the squash

## What changed
- `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs`: `Conventions.merge_method: MergeMethod` (default `Squash`, parsed from
  `[conventions].merge_method` = `"squash"` | `"merge"` through the existing lowercase `MergeMethod`), and
  `Repo.merge_method` resolved from `[conventions]` in both `root_repo` and `repo_of_section` — a
  `[[repos]]` entry has no field for it, so it cannot override it (as `Repo.integration` is resolved).
- `crates/gear-model/src/commit.rs`: `TRAILER_TASK`, `TRAILER_ATTEMPT`, `TRAILER_CANDIDATE`,
  `TRAILER_SOURCE` and `squash_message(task, attempt, goal, candidate, sources, lines)`: subject
  `{task}: {goal's first line cut to a word at 72 chars}`, blank line, `- {line}` per line (none: no body
  block), blank line, then `Gear-Task`, `Gear-Attempt`, `Gear-Candidate` and one `Gear-Source` per source.
- `crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs`: the `OpKind::Merge` payload now carries `"goal"` — the task's current
  contract goal (the goal lives on the contract, not on `TaskRow`; absent contract → `""`).

## Tests
- `config.rs`: `merge_method` defaults to squash and reaches `Repo`; both values parse; a `[[repos]]`
  entry takes the project's method.
- `commit.rs`: exact text with a multi-line goal, two body lines and two sources; no body block without
  lines; a goal over 72 characters cut at a word.
- `settle_review_contract.rs`: the queued merge op's payload carries the contract goal.
- `config_contract.rs`: the `Conventions`/`Repo` fixtures carry the new field.

## Check
`make check CRATE=gear-model` — 28 passed; `make check CRATE=gear-app` — 791 passed; `cargo fmt --all --check` clean.

## Out of scope
- The engine does not yet read `Repo.merge_method` or use `squash_message` (the squash itself is a later
  task of the voyage).
- The 72-character cut applies to the goal part of the subject, as the packet words it; the whole subject
  line can therefore be longer than 72.
