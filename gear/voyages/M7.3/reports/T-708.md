## What changed
- `crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs`: the voyage block's first line is `Voyage <milestone> <title><waits>` — no
  done text; the clock lines below it are unchanged.
- `crates/gear-app/src/home.rs`: the snapshot's voyage line is
  `Voyage <milestone> <title> · eta <eta> · bar <first word of the bar>` instead of `plan::voyage_line`;

  voyage-contract board test expects `Voyage M2 course` and its clock lines.

  eta 8h`).
