# T-703 — Plan apply names estimates far from the measured speed

## What changed (`crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs`)
- `apply_queue` collects the tasks whose estimate this apply sets or changes (a NEW block, or an existing
  block whose estimate differs from the stored one). After the writes it reads `speed::measured` and, for
  each, resolves the lane's model (the lane's first `models` entry — its own override, else the role chain —
  else `engine.model.<seat>`; a captain's task: the captain seat's model, else the captain chain) and adds a
  warning when `speed::calibrated` gives a value and the estimate is more than twice the model's median work
  time or less than half of it:
  `T-n: estimate <e> min; <model> has taken <ratio>x its estimates over <samples> tasks (median <m> min of
  work): about <c> min`. Warnings only; nothing is refused.
- In the apply that departs a voyage (or a confirmed apply on a voyage that already departed) one line is
  added: `eta <eta>; the measured speed of the lanes' models puts the open tasks at about <h> on the slowest
  lane`, per lane the sum of calibrated minutes (or the estimate where none), the largest lane.

## Tests (`plan.rs`)
- five measured attempts at ratio 0.2 (15 min work, 75 min estimate): a new 60 min task warns with "about
  12 min"; a 15 min task does not; four samples do not; a departing apply prints the eta line (`0h12m`).

## Check
`make check CRATE=gear-app` — 811 passed; `cargo fmt --all --check` clean.

## Out of scope
- The packet says the estimate is compared with the value `speed::calibrated` gives, but that value is the
  estimate scaled by the ratio, so the comparison would be tautological (every estimate at ratio 0.2 would
  warn, including the acceptance's 15 min task). The acceptance fixes the meaning: the estimate is compared
  with the model's measured median work time (15 min in the test), and the calibrated value is the "about
  <c> min" it names. Logged as the one reading the packet left open.
- A task without an estimate counts as 0 minutes in the eta line; a lane with no model uses its estimate.
- The eta line is added once per apply; a later confirmed apply on the same departed voyage adds it again
  (the packet asks for it in that apply too).
