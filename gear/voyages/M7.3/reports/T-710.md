# T-710 — A missing seat branch names the captain branches the remote has

## What changed (`crates/gear-engine/src/finished.rs`)
- `merge_records`'s `Recorded::Missing` arm lists the remote's `<project>/captain/*` branches
  (`git for-each-ref --format=%(refname:short) refs/remotes/<remote>/<project>/captain/`, the `<remote>/`
  prefix cut) through a new `remote_captain_branches` helper. With any, it opens or keeps the captain item on
  thread `records:<milestone>` through `tell_records_failed`, with the error text
  `no branch <branch> on <remote>; the remote has <list>: push this voyage's records to <branch>`, and does
  not resolve. With none, it resolves as before.

## Tests
- `a_missing_seat_branch_names_the_captain_branches_the_remote_has`: with `p/captain/M1` deleted and
  `p/captain/M0` on the remote, one open urgent item names `p/captain/M0` and nothing is merged.
- `a_landing_with_no_captain_branch_at_all_resolves_as_before`: no item, nothing merged.
