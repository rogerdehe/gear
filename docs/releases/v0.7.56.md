# v0.7.56

## Added

- The fleet stands by while a finished voyage waits for its landing word.
  When every task is done and merged, the landing order ("Voyage M is ready
  to land") waits for the user's acceptance, often for hours, and every lane
  and reviewer sat idle in its pane all that time. While that order is open
  and the voyage has no unfinished task, GEAR now parks and releases every
  lane (its pane, worktree and branch kept) and releases every reviewer
  without a job, and tells the captain once on thread `standby:<M>`.
  Aftercare is the captain's: a small fix it makes itself, a bigger one is a
  task as usual, and the lane it names comes back by itself in its pane. A
  landing order answered without a landing (`keep course`) brings every
  lane back; the landing retires them as before. A captain working alone is
  not told.
