# v0.7.50

## Changed

- The board opens on the Tasks view, and the Now view is gone. A snapshot of
  what each lane does this minute answered nothing about how far the voyage
  is, and Herdr already shows the panes. The tabs are 1 Tasks, 2 Cost, 3 Log.
  The header still carries the voyage's progress: the goal check, the time
  against the expected, the milestones and the tasks bar.
- The quota line moved to the foot of the Cost view, and the voyage's labeled
  issues to the head of the Tasks view, above the graph.
- The lane table, the balance line, the "to handle" items and the overlap
  lines are no longer drawn. Every item there was the captain's, and the
  captain reads them in its inbox.
