- **Merge items of work that landed another way close:** a remerge that found the work already on the branch, and a task carried by a merged task (`carried-by`), no longer leave merge-failed items that neither a reply nor a plan line could close; the periodic scan closes them, and a reply may. A finished task's overrun items close too.
- **Items about settled tasks close themselves** (T-569).

Why: Prometheus, 2026-11-05: M-8886, M-8629 and M-9797 kept ringing after their work had landed.
