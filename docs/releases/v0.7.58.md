# v0.7.58

## Fixed

- `gear reply --false-alarm` now marks every item GEAR raised, the ones
  settled elsewhere too. A station item (settled by a plan `station` line),
  a friction item (closed with its reports) or a review fail (settled in
  `# Verdicts`) was refused with "needs a plan line" before the mark was
  written, and the station items are where most false alarms are. Such an
  item now takes the mark and keeps its state: the place that settles it
  still does.
