# v0.7.52

## Changed

- A voyage of many loose tasks is refused at departure. Each task outside a
  feature is reviewed and gated on its own, so a voyage cut into dozens of
  small loose tasks queues a review and a gate for every one, and the
  reviewers fall behind the lanes (alfred M1, 2026-11-19: every hand-in
  waited 40 to 50 minutes for a reviewer). A departing voyage, or an apply
  that adds loose `NEW` tasks to a voyage, whose loose tasks exceed
  `[conventions] standalone_max_tasks` (default 8, 0 = no check) is refused
  and names the way out: cut one capability's tasks as `feature: F-<n>`, or
  say why the tasks share no capability with a `standalone:` line in
  `# Decisions`. Audits, mechanical tasks, feature members and feature
  integration tasks do not count.

## Fixed

- A carried task is no longer named lost while its carrier's merge-failed
  item is open. When the carrier's merge failed, each task it carried rang
  "nothing will merge it" beside the carrier's own item (alfred M1: T-26 and
  T-27 beside T-39). The carrier's open item speaks for them, and an open
  lost item about them is answered; once the carrier's item is settled with
  the carrier still unmerged, they are named again.
