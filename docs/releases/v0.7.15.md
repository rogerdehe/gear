# GEAR v0.7.15

A seat follows the charter its captain set now, not the one it started with, and projects that share a store keep their rules files apart.

## Fixed

- **A running seat is told its changed rules.** A lane or reviewer on a CLI that takes its rules at start (pi, Claude, Codex) kept that text for its whole run, a new conversation included. A captain who rewrote a reviewer charter mid-voyage saw its reviewers keep quoting the old one: they named another lane's `.env` hours after the charter stopped saying so. When the charter, or the voyage's done and bar, changed since a seat started, its next packet now carries the new rules once, under "Your rules changed". A seat started by an older engine gets them once after the upgrade.
- **A reviewer on a CLI with no rules flag gets its charter.** OpenCode, grok and agy reviewers were never given the reviewer charter; their packets now carry it, as lane packets already did.
- **Each project has its own rules files.** The rules files lived in one folder per store, keyed by lane name only, so projects sharing a store wrote over each other's `review.md` and any lanes of one name. They now live under `rules/<project>/`.

## Upgrading

Install the new binary (`herdr plugin install rogerdehe/gear --ref v0.7.15`) and restart the engine. Nothing to configure.
