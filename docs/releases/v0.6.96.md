# GEAR v0.6.96

A merge that waits for another feature's members stays at the back of the queue.

Fixed:
- **A member merge waiting for another feature's members ran first on every tick** (Lawrence M2: T-94 of F-5 carries F-3's T-71, T-73 and T-83; their merges into lawrence/feature/f-3 counted as "landed", so T-94 went back to the front, took the merge worktree, and waited again, while T-105 (F-4) and T-108 (F-1) never ran). A waited-for member counts as landed only once its feature's integration task merged, as the merge's own check counts it.
