# T-199 direction checks

The engine detects new pushed code outside `ops/` on a started lane attempt and starts one direction job at a time after waiting hand-ins. The reviewer receives the prescribed packet; direction results record the checked SHA and send problems as an urgent lane instruction and captain information. Reviewer release and session handling recognize direction jobs, and direction results use a narrow read-only store query.

Validation: Git-fixture and fake-Herdr tests cover successive pushes, one in flight, `ok`, problems, an ops-only push, and hand-in priority. The full gate is reported at hand-in.
