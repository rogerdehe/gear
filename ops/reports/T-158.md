## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs`** — the projection is **per lane**
(§10.7 row B4):

- every lane of the voyage gets its own measurement: the minutes it measured
  on its finished work, the estimate of that work, the estimates of its open
  work, and its **own pace** (its measured minutes over its estimated ones);
- the lane's projection is its open estimates times that pace — a lane that
  measured nothing has no pace, so its projection stays unknown (the station's
  "unknown measurements" case keeps saying so);
- the **slowest lane sets the finish** and is named (`slowest_lane`), and the
  voyage's projected finish is the elapsed time plus that lane's projection.
  The report keeps the slowest *task* (`slowest`) for the lines that name it.

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`** — the third view, as the prototype draws
it:

- the heading "WORK DONE AND LEFT, BY LANE (minutes of estimate)";
- one row per lane: the mark of the selected one, the lane's name, the bars
  (the minutes of estimate done and left) and the counts;
- the totals: "all lanes: done {m}m · left {m}m · at the measured pace the
  last lane ends at {h}";
- the **burn-up**: the minutes of estimate merged per hour of the voyage (the
  hours since the departure, the estimate of the work merged in each), with
  its bars and the hours' labels.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — `Enter` on the third view opens the lane's
panel too, as in the first view (done, now, left in order).

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/workload_contract.rs` — a fixture with two very
different lanes (`hull` works at its estimate and has 20 minutes left, `deck`
works twice as slow and has 60 minutes of estimate left):

- `the_slower_lane_sets_the_finish_and_is_named` — each lane's own pace and
  projection, and the slower lane (`deck`) named as the one that sets the
  finish;
- `the_burn_up_counts_the_estimate_merged_per_hour` — the estimate merged per
  hour of the voyage;
- `the_workload_view_shows_the_lanes_and_the_burn_up` — the rows, the bars
  and the burn-up;
- `the_lane_panel_shows_what_it_did_and_what_is_left` — the panel of a lane.
