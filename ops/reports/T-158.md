## Attempt 2 — the rework of the review of attempt 1 (F1–F5)

**F1 — the station names the lane that sets the finish.**
`crates/gear-app/src/station.rs` rendered the report's `slowest` (the slowest
*task*); it now renders `slowest_lane` with an explicit label: "slowest lane
heat 180m". The station's fixture asserts the whole line, so it was updated
with it.

**F2 — an unmeasured lane leaves the finish unknown.**
`voyage.rs` filters the lanes without a projection out of the voyage's finish
and published the maximum of the rest: a new lane with open work and no
measured history (or an open task without an estimate) was dropped, and an
unknown estimate was counted as zero work. Now every lane keeps the fact that
its open work is known (`LaneProjection::left_known`), and the finish
(`slowest_lane`, `projected_min`) is published only when **every** lane with
open work has a pace and known estimates. A lane with no open work needs no
pace; no lane borrows another's pace.

**F3 — the burn-up is cumulative.**
The hours were bucketed by `merged_at` and the buckets were plotted as they
were (two 30-minute merges in consecutive hours gave `[30, 30]` — a flat
chart). The series is now summed: the buckets are extended through the
current hour of the voyage (the hours without progress stay in it) and summed,
so the prototype's `BURN = [0, 0, 95, 170, 260, 330]` is what the board
draws.

**F4 — the lane panel shows the running work once.**
`LEFT, in order` listed every task that was not terminal, so the running and
the reviewing tasks appeared in `NOW` **and** again in `LEFT` (and their
estimates were counted as queued work). `LEFT` now holds the queued tasks
only, in the plan order; the running and the reviewing tasks stay in `NOW`.

**F5 — the Workload view measures a finished voyage too.**
The view read `project.voyages`, which holds the active voyages only: when the
last task of the voyage was done, the view showed `done 0 (0m)` per lane and no
burn-up. The board model gains `ProjectBoard::workload`: the active voyage, or
else the latest departed voyage that is finished. The home page and the station
keep their active-only selection.

F6 (logged by the review) is not touched: a done task without a recorded span
still vanishes from the completed scope.

## What changed in attempt 1 (kept here for the whole)

**`crates/gear-board/src/voyage.rs`** — the projection is **per lane**
(§10.7 row B4): every lane of the voyage gets its own measurement (the minutes
it measured on its finished work, the estimate of that work, the estimates of
its open work) and its **own pace** (its measured minutes over its estimated
ones); the lane's projection is its open estimates times that pace; the
**slowest lane sets the finish** and is named (`slowest_lane`).

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`** — the third view, as the prototype draws
it: the heading "WORK DONE AND LEFT, BY LANE (minutes of estimate)", one row
per lane (the mark of the selected one, the name, the bars of the estimate
done and left, the counts), the totals and the **burn-up** with its bars and
the hours' labels.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — `Enter` on the third view opens the lane's
panel too, as in the first view (done, now, left in order).

- `the_lane_panel_shows_the_running_and_the_queued_work_once` — a running, a
