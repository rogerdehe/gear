# T-99 — prepare v0.3.0 (M3's release), attempt 2

Scope: T-99 rework — F1: the notes said a flaky gate retries by itself, which
is not what the engine does; F2: the notes were paragraphs, not one line per
change, and two lines were imprecise (packet kinds, the model-label benefit, the
empty-model-list caveat).

Branch: base/t99 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before
verification, gate green on the merged result)

What changed (`docs/releases/v0.3.0.md` only — the version bump of attempt 1
stands):
- **F1** — the merge bullet is now two honest lines: **a push race or a gate
  that could not run** (a missing command, a broken shell) retries by itself;
  **a red gate needs a remerge** — `- remerge T-<n>` under `# Staff` retries the
  accepted candidate once the cause is addressed. `merge.rs` retries only a
  gate that cannot start; an ordinary failing test is `Stuck` until the captain
  acts, and the notes now say so.
- **F2** — the notes are one line (one sentence) per change, renumbered around
  the correction above:
  - the standard line names **both packet kinds** — every task packet and every
    review packet;
  - the OpenCode line is the real benefit: **a lane's model label comes from
    OpenCode's own list** (the text the in-place switch types and the status bar
    shows is derived from it, so a new model needs no hand-written labels);
  - the empty-list line no longer promises that no lane is ever parked: an
    empty first list is "not loaded yet" and waited for, bounded, and only a
    list that is there without the model parks the lane, with that reason.
- The four groups, the adoption, fixtures, push safety, unattended-operation
  and known-limits content from attempt 1 are unchanged in substance.
