# T-156 — the new board, part 1 (the frame, the keys, the first view)

Scope: T-156 — `gear board` is rewritten to the prototype
`ops/plan/m4-board/prototype.py`: the frame, the keys, the overlay panel and
the first view (Now) on store data; the graph of T-157 is wired into the
second view.

Branch: `deck/t156` (code @ the hand-in commit; `origin/dev` merged before
verification)

**On the base of the branch.** The branch carries T-157's graph view (its
attempt 2), because the second view of the new board draws it. T-157 is in
review; when it lands, this branch merges the rest of it alone.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`** (new) — the frame of the new board:
- the head: the title (`GEAR · <project> · <milestone>: <title>`), the time
  bar, the tasks bar, the five tabs (` 1 Now   2 Graph   3 Workload   4
  Decisions   5 Time & tokens `) and the footer with the keys;
- the first view **Now**: the lanes with their idle reason (the `idle_reason`
  of T-155), the done and left counts, **NEEDS A LOOK** (a task over its
  estimate, a task that was reworked, a lane that has much more work left than
  the idle ones beside it), the **QUOTA** readings (the windows of the
  providers, read from the meta `quota.reading.*`) and the **DONE SENTENCE**
  (the clauses of the voyage's done text);
- the second view is T-157's graph, with the selection wired in; the third to
  the fifth show the next task;
- the **overlay panel**: a box drawn over the frame, as the prototype draws
  it.

**`crates/gear-board/src/screen.rs`** — the terminal speaks events now: a
key, the arrows, the shift-tab and a **click** (the xterm SGR mouse). The
board asks the terminal for the mouse (the SGR mode) when it takes the screen
and gives it back when it leaves.

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — the loop of the board: ←/→ and the tab
key change the view, the numbers 1 to 5 pick one, ↑↓ (and j/k) move the
selection, `Enter` opens the panel of the selected row (the lane's work in the
first view, the rounds of the task in the graph) and `q` leaves. The model
carries what the frame needs: the idle reason of each lane and its text, the
estimate, the actual time and the rounds of each task, the minutes the lane
has left, and the quota readings.

**`crates/gear-store/src/lib.rs`** — one small helper: `meta_keys_with_prefix`,
so the board finds the quota readings of the providers.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/board_contract.rs`:

- `the_new_board_draws_its_views_and_the_keys_move_between_them` — the board
  runs in a **pty**; the test writes the keys and reads the frames **by poll**
  (no sleep), stopping at the footer line. It checks that the frame and the
  first view come from the store (the title, the five tabs, the lanes), that
  the right arrow moves to the graph, that a **click on the tab row** picks
  the view, and that **Enter opens the panel** of the selected lane (the lane's
  work, the tasks it has left in order).

The old tests of the board follow the new frame (the lanes of the first view,
the graph of the second).
