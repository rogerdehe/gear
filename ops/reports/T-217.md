# T-217 — A lane restart carries its Delivered attempt to the new generation (contract v3)

Contract v3 (captain 07:35, M-3577) adds `crates/gear-app/src/seats.rs` to the
allowed edits: a retry and a re-onboarding reserve the new generation there.
The code of attempt 1 (reviewed, pass) is unchanged; this hand-in is the same
candidate against contract v3.

Scope: T-217 — when a lane seat's generation advances (a restart, a retry, a
session step), the attempt the seat is working on (`Delivered` or `Started`)
moves to the new generation in the same transaction, so the hand-in of the new
generation is accepted instead of refused as "not owned by this seat and
generation".

Branch: keel/t217 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before the
final verification)

## The bug

M4 06:20: hull was restarted twice while its T-175 rework was `Delivered`
under generation 5. The hand-in from generation 7 was refused — "not owned by
this seat and generation" — and the captain moved the attempt by hand.

`gear-app/src/mail.rs` checks, for a hand-in, that the attempt's seat is the
caller's and that a `Delivered` attempt's generation is the caller's
generation. The attempt kept the generation it was started in, so every
generation the seat gained (a restart, a retry, a session step) left the
attempt behind: the lane could not hand in its own work until a human moved
the row.

## The fix

- `crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs` — a new `carry_attempt_generation(tx,
  seat_id, from_generation, to_generation, now)`: the seat's own attempts that
  are `Delivered` or `Started` under the older generation move to the new one.
  An attempt of another seat, or one that is already settled, is untouched.
  The ownership check in `mail.rs` is unchanged.
- `crates/gear-app/src/seats.rs` — `begin_onboard`, where a re-onboarding
  reserves the new generation, carries the attempt with it in the same
  transaction. This is the path of a `- retry <lane>` and of a session step
  that re-onboards the seat.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/restart.rs` — `reserve`, where the engine's
  restart (a cross-CLI switch, a config change, a quota fallback) reserves the
  new generation, carries the attempt with it in the same transaction.

## The tests

- `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/lane_restart_generation_contract.rs` (new):
  - `a_retry_carries_the_delivered_attempt_to_the_new_generation` — a lane
    at generation 5 with a `Delivered` attempt is retried (the seat goes back
    to `Queued` and is onboarded again); the seat is at generation 6, the
    attempt is at generation 6, the attempt of the other seat stays at
    generation 5, and the hand-in from generation 6 is accepted (the attempt
    goes to `Submitted`).
  - `a_hand_in_of_the_old_generation_is_still_refused` — the old generation
    is still refused (`StaleGeneration`): the seat's generation is the
    identity, the carry does not loosen it.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/restart.rs` (engine tests):
  `a_restart_carries_the_attempt_to_the_new_generation` — a config change
  restarts the lane, the seat moves to generation 2 and its attempt moves
  with it.

Red before green: with the carry removed from `seats.rs`, the first test
fails (`left: 5, right: 6` — the attempt stays at the old generation).
