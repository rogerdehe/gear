# T-151 — Rounds bookkeeping (design §4.1, §13.5)

A round owns every agent record on its seat from its `start_at` until its
`end_at`: the start of the next round on the same seat, or the seat's release.
This task makes that bookkeeping true in the engine, and records the session a
check-in proves (§3.2 step 5).

## What changed

**`crates/gear-engine/src/rounds.rs` (new).** `pub fn tick(store, herdr, cfg,
now)` walks every project and:

- **the session joins the open round.** The seat's binding carries the session
  its agent is in; when it changed inside the round (a `/new`, a restart, a
  resume), the new session joins the round's `sessions` list — once, however
  many ticks run (the round keeps the list of the sessions it owns, so §4.3
  can read the records of each of them).
- **a round ends.** When the next round on the same seat starts, the open one
  ends **at that start** (§4.1: "A round owns every agent record on its seat
  from its `start_at` until its `end_at`"); when the seat is released (or
  retired), the open round ends at the seat's release. There is no "between
  rounds" bucket: what a seat does after a hand-in belongs to the round it
  handed in.

**`crates/gear-engine/src/overrun.rs` (new)** and
**`crates/gear-engine/src/waits.rs` (new).** The tick of each is empty: the
overrun question (§5.2) and the declared waits (§5.3) belong to their own
tasks, and until they land no round is asked, no task is parked and no message
is written.

**`crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`.** The three ticks are called from the
engine's tick (after the projects, so a round of any project is kept true in
one place); the tick report gains `rounds` (the rounds that ended and the ones
that learned a session).

**`crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`.** The check-in confirmation now records the
session the attempt ran in: `attempts.session_ref` and `attempts.session_cli`
come from the seat's binding (§3.2 step 5) — the binding knows the session
the agent is in, because the observation of this tick refreshed it.

## Files

- `crates/gear-engine/src/rounds.rs` (new) — the rounds bookkeeping: the
  session of a seat joins its open round, a round ends at the next round's
  start or at the seat's release.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/overrun.rs` (new), `crates/gear-engine/src/waits.rs`
  (new) — the ticks of §5.2 and §5.3, empty until their tasks land.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs` — the three modules, their calls in the tick
  and `TickReport::rounds`.
- `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs` — `record_attempt_session`: the check-in
  sets the attempt's session and CLI from the binding.
- `crates/gear-engine/tests/it/rounds_contract.rs` (new, registered in
  `main.rs`) — the four rules of the acceptance.
- `ops/reports/T-151.md` — this brief.

## Verification

- `scripts/test.sh` → **1042 tests run: 1042 passed, 0 skipped** (63 s; inside
  the 300 s red line). `cargo build --workspace --all-targets` → clean.
- The acceptance, in `rounds_contract.rs`:
  - `a_session_change_mid_round_lands_in_the_rounds_sessions_once` — the
    binding carries a new session inside an open round: the tick adds it once
    (the second tick adds nothing).
  - `a_second_round_on_the_seat_closes_the_first_at_its_start` — two rounds on
    the seat: the first ends at the second one's start, the newest stays open.
  - `a_release_closes_the_open_round` — a released seat: its open round ends
    at the release.
  - `the_check_in_sets_the_attempts_session` — a delivery confirmed by a
    check-in view: the attempt is `Started` and records the session
    (`ses_A`) and the CLI (`opencode`) of the binding.

## Open questions

none — two decisions worth naming:

- **The token counting of a closed round** (§4.3-4.4) is not here: it needs
  the usage reader, which lands with its own task. The tick keeps the
  bookkeeping (the sessions, the ends) and leaves the counting to it.
- **The session of a round** is the one the binding carries while the round is
  open. A session that ends before the round does stays in the round's list:
  its records belong to the round that was working when they were written
  (§4.1).

## Out of scope

- **The round row itself** is created by the session action of the round
  (§3.2 step 2 — hull's `session.rs`, T-144/T-145). This task keeps the rows
  true; it does not create them.
- **The seat's release** closes its round through the seat's state (the
  `Released`/`Retired` transition the release path writes), not through a
  change in the release code itself (hull's `release.rs`).
- **The budget of a round** (§5.1) and the overrun question (§5.2) belong to
  the task that fills `overrun.rs`.
