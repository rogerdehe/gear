# T-69 — A re-review on another seat does not fight the previous checkout

Attempt 1, contract v1.

Trial G10: the re-review of a task started on a second reviewer seat while the
first seat's worktree still held `review/t<n>`, so `git checkout -B` refused
and the checkout was retried every tick. Now a clean holder of the branch is
detached (its HEAD kept) and the review goes on; a holder with uncommitted
changes is left alone, and the engine stops after three tries and tells the
captain. The temp slots are shown as running / starting / blocked.

## What changed

- **`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`, the checkout command** — before the
  branch is checked out, every worktree of the repo is looked at: one that
  holds the job's branch and is **clean** is detached (`git checkout
  --detach`, its HEAD kept) and the checkout goes on. One that holds the branch
  with **changes** is left untouched and the chain reports the failure marker,
  so the engine does not fight it.
- **`crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`, `checkout_ready`** — a failed checkout
  is counted per seat generation; after `CHECKOUT_TRIES` (3) the seat is
  **parked** (NeedsCheck, for a human) and the captain gets **one item** naming
  the branch, the candidate and the worktree with the last line the pane showed.
  Below that, a fresh token resubmits as before.
- **`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs` and `crates/gear-app/src/home.rs`** — the
  temp slots are no longer one number: `Temps` gains `running`, `starting` and
  `blocked` (a temp seat is blocked while the engine's blocked timer for it is
  live), the board prints `Temp slots: N running · M starting · B blocked (I of
  Q in use)` and the captain's snapshot line reads `temps N running · M
  starting · B blocked (I of Q)`.

## Acceptance evidence

- `a_clean_holder_of_the_review_branch_is_detached_a_dirty_one_is_not` (real
  git, in `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs`) — a scratch repo with `origin`,
  `review/t1` at a candidate, the previous seat's worktree checked out on it and
  a second seat's free: the checkout command run in the second seat's worktree
  reports success, the old worktree is detached with its HEAD unchanged, and
  the new one is on `review/t1` at the candidate. Then the old worktree is put
  back on the branch **with an uncommitted file**: the command reports the
  failure marker, the old worktree is not detached and its change is kept.
- The temps line: `the_captain_home_starts_with_the_snapshot_before_the_inbox`
  (gear-app) and `the_board_shows_the_captains_inbox_and_the_temp_slots`
  (gear-board) now assert the three counts.

## Validation

- `cargo test --workspace` → **812 passed, 0 failed**.
- `cargo build --workspace --all-targets` → exit 0, no warnings.
- `git diff --check` → clean.

## Out of scope

- **"start the review in that worktree's seat"** — when the holder is dirty the
  engine does not move the review to the old seat; it stops after three tries
  and hands the decision to the captain (one item). Moving the job to the old
  seat needs the review's seat/binding bookkeeping to accept a job it did not
  start; logged for the captain rather than guessed at.
- **"belongs to a reviewer seat with no live agent"** — the checkout runs in a
  shell, which cannot see Herdr's agents. It detaches a holder only when that
  worktree is **clean**; a live reviewer whose worktree is clean could be
  detached, and the next job there re-checks out what it needs. The engine's
  own seats are the ones that hold review branches, and a released reviewer's
  worktree is clean by construction.
- The **three-try stop** has no test of its own yet: the counter, the park and
  the captain item are wired and built (the item goes through the review's
  existing one-per-task `tell_captain`), but the acceptance's real-git tests
  cover the detach and the dirty refusal only.
