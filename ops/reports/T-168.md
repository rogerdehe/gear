# T-168 — friction reports in the CLI (design §6.1-§6.3, the CLI half of §6.4)

Scope: T-168 — a lane or a reviewer reports what cost it time that the
captain could fix (`--friction` on a hand-in and on a review, design §6.1);
the report is required when the round is over its budget (§6.2, refused with
`FRICTION_REQUIRED` exactly as the design writes it); each report is stored
with its round's own facts (§6.3); the captain reads the reports with `gear
friction`, `--all` and `gear friction close F-<n> -m "<outcome>"` (§6.4, the
CLI half).

Branch: mast/t168 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before final
verification)

Files:
- `crates/gear-app/src/mail.rs` — `--friction` on a result or a review; the
  check (§6.2) runs in one place, before the attempt is submitted and before
  the verdict is stored: the round's active time (now − the attempt's
  `started_at` − the open round's `paused_s`) against `round_budget_min`, and
  a round over its budget without a report is refused with
  `FRICTION_REQUIRED` and the numbers that say so — nothing is written. The
  report is stored through `insert_friction` with the round's facts (§6.3).
- `crates/gear-app/src/friction.rs` — the captain's side (§6.3, §6.4): the
  list (the open reports, or every one of them) with the round's own facts
  (budget, active time, paused time, rounds so far, tokens), and the closing
  of a report with its outcome (fixed, lesson or not worth it).
- `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs` — the lane's packet says the round's budget
  and what a hand-in past it must carry (§6.2):
  `Budget: <budget> min. Past it, your hand-in must carry --friction "…"`,
  with the words that belong in a report (§6.1).
- `crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs` — `gear send` takes `--friction`; the new
  `gear friction` command (the list, `--all`, and `gear friction close F-<n>
  -m "<outcome>"`). The CLI tests: an over-budget hand-in without a report is
  refused with the text (and nothing is written), with a report it is accepted
  and the report is stored with its facts, a hand-in under the budget needs no
  report, the captain lists the reports and closes one, and an outcome that is
  not one of the three kinds is refused.
- `crates/gear-model/src/error.rs` — the `FRICTION_REQUIRED` error code (§6.2
  names it); two lines in the enum and its name.
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/packet_contract.rs` — the packet says the budget
  and the flag.
- the `SendArgs` constructions in the tests (this lane's and the engine's)
  carry the new field.
- `ops/reports/T-168.md` — this hand-in.
