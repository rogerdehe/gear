# T-168 — friction reports in the CLI (design §6.1-§6.3, the CLI half of §6.4)

Scope: T-168 — a lane or a reviewer reports what cost it time that the
captain could fix (`--friction` on a hand-in and on a review, design §6.1);
the report is required when the round is over its budget (§6.2, refused with
`FRICTION_REQUIRED` exactly as the design writes it); each report is stored
with its round's own facts (§6.3); the captain reads the reports with `gear
friction`, `--all` and `gear friction close F-<n> -m "<outcome>"` (§6.4, the
CLI half). Attempt 2 fixes the review's F1-F4.

Branch: mast/t168 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before final
verification)

## Rework (attempt 2: F1, F2, F3, F4)

- **F1 (P1, blocking) — a review is charged from the reviewer's own clock.**
  The check read the writer's check-in for every role, so a review that took
  five minutes was refused because the writer had checked in an hour before
  (and the report was stored as required). A review is now charged from its
  own start — the review packet's delivery confirmation (`sent_at`), else the
  review round's start — and never from the writer's check-in; the round's own
  pauses are subtracted. The dev and rework rounds keep the attempt's check-in.
- **F2 (P1, blocking) — the budget is compared in seconds.** The active time
  was divided by 60 before it was compared with the budget, so a round that was
  one second over its budget slipped through. The active seconds are now
  compared with `budget * 60`, and the minutes are for the message only.
- **F3 (P1, blocking) — the reviewer's packet carries the budget.** Only the
  writer's packet had the §6.2 line; the reviewer's own packet now carries
  its budget (`round_budget_min(..., "review", model)`) and the words that
  belong in a report, so a reviewer learns the requirement from the packet
  and not from a refusal.
- **F4 (P2, logged) — the outcome of a closed report is checked.** The word
  must end at a word boundary (`fixedwhatever` is not `fixed`) and something
  must follow it (a bare `fixed` says nothing).
