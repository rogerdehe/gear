# T-96 — the captain guide covers what a fresh captain had to dig for

Scope: T-96 — three facts from the live acceptance are now where a captain reads
them at kickoff, in the existing sections of `skills/gear/references/captain.md`
(no new section, and no change to `conventions.md`: the config is described in
the captain's guide only):

1. **A model entry on another CLI, with its own args.** The model-chain bullet
   in §1 says an entry may replace the plain model id:
   `{ model = "gpt-6.1-sol", cli = "codex", args = ["-c", "model_reasoning_effort=medium"] }`,
   and the `[models]` block of the config example shows it once, in `review`.
   The shape is the config's own (`ModelEntry`/`ModelSpec`: `model`, optional
   `cli`, `args` after the model flag), verified against `gear-app::config`.
2. **`quota.lanes` is the lanes the project has, not those running at once.**
   The `[quota]` block says so on the `lanes` line, and the lanes bullet says
   the sheet's lane count and the quota agree before the user sees it — `init`
   refuses a quota below the lanes configured, and a larger one leaves lanes the
   project does not have.
3. **No `deps:` between two tasks of one lane.** The `# Queue` field list in §2
   says it, with why: a lane works its own queue in order, on its own branch,
   and its second task starts after the first lands (`gear --skill decompose`
   rule 3, unchanged and pointed at).
