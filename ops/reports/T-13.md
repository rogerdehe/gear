## Rework (review R-67, three P1s)

- **Obsolete reviews no longer stop the engine.** A review of a task that is
  finished (cancelled, failed, done by another path) is consumed: the task
  keeps its state, the attempt is closed (Submitted → Rejected, so the lane is
  not blocked by it) and the review items are resolved. A settlement that still
  fails is recorded in `TickReport.settle_failed` and never aborts the tick —
  the rest of the tick (observation, dispatch, other projects) runs.
  Regressions: `a_cancelled_task_s_review_does_not_stop_the_tick` (with
  `recover_on_start` and unrelated ready work dispatched in the same tick) and
  `a_failing_settlement_does_not_abort_the_tick` (a Waiting task's review fails
  and another lane's delivery still happens).
- **The current-contract rule is kept.** A pass on an attempt whose adopted
  contract is older than the task's current one cannot complete it
  (design/06): the attempt is rejected, the task goes back Ready with the
  contract move spelled out in the rework note, and no merge is queued — the
  same rule `tasks::settle` enforces for the captain's own verdict. Regression:
  `a_pass_on_an_older_contract_is_a_rework`.
- **Duplicate reviews leave no action item.** Settlement resolves *every*
  matching review item (not the first), and a review that arrives after its
  attempt is settled is refused with `BAD_TRANSITION` before any message is
  written, so a late duplicate creates no captain work. Regression:
  `duplicate_reviews_leave_no_action_item` (duplicate before settlement, and a
  refused duplicate after it, with one merge and one information line).
