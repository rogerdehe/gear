# T-148 — the quota of the providers (design §9.2)

Scope: The screen is not the source of quota facts. A quota marker or a provider error on the screen is only a signal: the engine reads the provider's API (`gear_usage::read_quota`) with the key the config names (`[[quota.sources]]`, §9.1) and the reading decides — a spent window parks the lane until the window's reset plus a minute (the round's clock paused, a `quota` pause row opened and closed, `Continue T-n: your quota is back.` in the same session at the reset), quota left is not a quota stop (the lane waits 5 min and continues; the second time in a row is a captain item), and a source that cannot be read parks the lane for today's `quota_reset_h` hours. Every source is read every 5 min, the last reading is kept in the meta `quota.reading.<provider>`, and a reading older than 15 min is marked stale.

Files: `crates/gear-app/src/config.rs` — the `[[quota.sources]]` parse of §9.1 (provider, reader, key_file, key_path) and a config finding for a reader the engine does not know (the captain's decision M-2781: the parse moved from T-147 to this task). `crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` — the sources and their readings (§9.1), the three branches of §9.2, the quota pause rows (§5.4), the round clock's pauses (§5.1), the `Continue` delivery and the captain items. `crates/gear-app/tests/it/config_contract.rs` — the sources parse and the finding for an unknown reader. `crates/gear-engine/src/quota.rs` (tests) — the three branches and the stale reading with a fake reader.

Verification: `cargo test -p gear-engine --lib quota` → 29 passed (four new: a spent window parks the lane until the reset plus a minute and continues in the same session; quota left waits 5 min and the second time in a row is a captain item; an unreadable source parks for `quota_reset_h` hours; a reading older than 15 min is marked stale). `cargo test -p gear-app --test it config_contract` → 27 passed (two new: the sources parse, an unknown reader is a finding). `scripts/test.sh` → 1142 passed, 0 skipped; suite 69 s, build 1 s warm (the suite is above the 60 s mark since the other lanes' tasks landed on dev; no test of this task is over 10 s).
