Scope: T-170 — the decision log: the line form of §7.1 with the optional ★
right after the dash, the kinds of §7.2, the note fallback with its warning,
the idempotent key of §7.3, and the entries GEAR records itself (§7.4), all
stored through `insert_decision_if_new`. Attempt 2 fixes the review's F1-F6.

Branch: mast/t170 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before final
verification)

## Rework (attempt 2: F1, F2, F3, F4, F5, F6)

- **F1 (P1, blocking) — the user's own correction at the landing.** The dated
  form `- <date> user: <text>` (§7.1) is now recognized before the general
  check: it is stored as kind `user` with its written date and its text, with
  no why and no warning.
- **F2 (P1, blocking) — the why of a cancelled task.** The decision that
  names the task is matched with whole identifiers: `T-1` never names `T-10`,
  so the cut of `T-1` takes the why of the line that names `T-1` (and
  `(no why written)` when no line names it).
- **F3 (P1, blocking) — a staff line that changed nothing.** A staff line
  whose seat is already at its target (a second `hold` on a held lane, a park
  on a parked lane) is still the captain's decision and is logged — once.
- **F4 (P1, blocking) — a plan without a milestone block.** A decisions-only
  or staff-only apply belongs to the voyage the project is in: the one that
  departed last, the same rule the config logging uses. The lines of such a
  plan are no longer orphaned with an empty voyage.
- **F5 (P1, blocking) — the JSON output carries the warnings.** The warning
  list is merged once (the apply's own and the lanes that would stand idle)
  and used for the text and the JSON alike; the note warning of §7.2 is in
  the JSON too.
- **F6 (P2, logged) — the date of a structured line.** The form needs the
  date §7.1 writes (`YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`); anything else (`yesterday scope: …`)
  is a note with the warning and without a star.
