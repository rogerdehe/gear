# T-170 — the captain's decision log (design §7.1-§7.4)

Scope: T-170 — the decision log: the line form of §7.1 with the optional ★
right after the dash, the kinds of §7.2, the note fallback with its warning,
the idempotent key of §7.3, and the entries GEAR records itself (§7.4), all
stored through `insert_decision_if_new`.

Branch: mast/t170 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before final
verification)

Files:
- `crates/gear-app/src/decision.rs` — the decision log's own rules: the line
  form of §7.1 (`<date> <kind>: <what> | why: … | else: … | changes: …`), the
  kinds that keep their own form (`tail`, `station`, `user`), the star the
  captain writes right after the dash, the note fallback of §7.2 (a line that
  is not structured is kept as kind `note`, without a star), and the key of
  §7.3 — the sha256 of the line with its whitespace collapsed. The sha256 is
  implemented here (no crypto library in the tree) and checked against the
  published test vectors.
- `crates/gear-app/src/plan.rs` — the apply records every line of
  `# Decisions` (§7.3: the same line is stored once, an edited line is a new
  decision, the table is append-only) and GEAR's own entries of §7.4: a pass
  over a failed review is an `override` (with the why the captain wrote), a
  fail verdict a `rework` (the rework text), a cancelled task a `cut` (with
  the why of the decision line that names the task, else `(no why written)`),
  and a staff line a `staff` (the line as the captain wrote it). The apply
  says which of its decision lines were not structured.
- `crates/gear-app/src/admin.rs` — a config re-apply is GEAR's own entry too:
  kind `config`, what `config v<k>` (§7.4).
- `crates/gear-cli/src/main.rs` — the apply prints its warnings, so the captain
  sees "warning: decision line <k> is not structured; logged as a note".
- `crates/gear-app/tests/it/decision_contract.rs` — the contract tests: a
  structured line stored once over two applies, an edited line a new entry, an
  unstructured line a note with the warning, a ★ read, the kinds that keep
  their own form, and GEAR's entries (a rework, a staff, an override, a config)
  each appearing once.
- `ops/reports/T-170.md` — this hand-in.

Verification:
- `scripts/test.sh` → 1072 tests run: 1072 passed, 0 skipped.
- `cargo build --workspace --all-targets` → clean (the gate's first half).
- `git diff --check` → clean.

Open questions: none.

Out of scope: the voyage report and the landing review that read the log
(design §7.5) are the next task's; the log is written here with the facts
§6.3/§7.3 name (the parts, the star, the key, the source), so that report can
read it. The board's view of the log is not part of this contract.
