# T-166 — the board's views 4 (Decisions) and 5 (Time & tokens)

Scope: the fourth and fifth views of the board, as the prototype of
`ops/plan/m4-board` draws them: the captain's decisions and the agents'
friction reports, and the time and the tokens of every task that ran.

## What changed

**`crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs`** — the board carries the data of the two
views:

- `ProjectBoard.decisions: Vec<DecisionBoard>` — the captain's decisions of
  the voyage (§7.1): the time, the kind, what was decided, why, and the star
  that marks the one worth a look at landing (§7.2);
- `ProjectBoard.frictions: Vec<FrictionBoard>` — the friction reports the
  agents left (§6.1): the task, the lane, the text, and whether the captain
  closed it;
- `TaskBoard.tokens` and `TaskBoard.cost_usd` — the tokens and the cost of
  the task's rounds (§4.3-§4.4), counted from the rounds table;
- the JSON of the board names the decisions and the frictions.

**`crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs`** — the two views:

- **view 4 (Decisions)**: the heading `DECISIONS MADE  (★ = worth a look at
  landing)`, then every decision in full — the time, the star, the kind, what
  was decided and why — then the count of the decisions by kind (`by kind: 1
  cut, 1 scope`), then `FRICTION REPORTS FROM AGENTS` with every report and
  its state (`open` or `closed`);
- **view 5 (Time & tokens)**: the columns of the prototype (task, lane, est,
  act, ratio, rounds, tokens, cost), one row per task that ran, and the total
  of the voyage so far (`so far: 0.8M tokens, $0.31`);
- `token_tasks(project)` — the tasks of the fifth view in the order of its
  rows; `Enter` on a row opens the task panel of that task (its rounds with
  their tokens), which the graph's panel already draws.

## Attempt 2 — the rework of the review of attempt 1 (F1-F5)

**F1 — the starred decisions are the ones in full.** The view showed every
decision, so the ordinary staff and estimate entries buried the course
changes. The starred decisions are now read in full, and the count by kind
is of the decisions that are not starred (§7.2).

**F2 — the reason stands on its own row.** The reason was appended to the
decision's line, so on an ordinary terminal (80 columns) it was cut away and
there was no panel to recover it. It is now a separate indented row, as the
prototype draws it.

**F3 — the decisions of the voyage the board measures.** The voyages are
ordered by milestone, so the first one was chosen: on a project that keeps
its history, the decisions of an old voyage were shown (or none). The
decisions come from the same voyage the Workload view measures.

**F4 — the rounds of the rounds table.** The column counted the attempts, so
one attempt with a dev round and a review round showed one round. It shows the
rows of the rounds table now.

**F5 — unknown is not zero.** A task whose tokens or cost were never counted
showed `0k $ 0.00` and the total passed it off as a total. The unknown parts
show `?`, the total says what it does not know, and a round that counted
nothing (the fields are there, the numbers are zero) is a measured zero.

## Tests

`crates/gear-board/tests/it/decisions_contract.rs` (a fixture store with a
voyage, a lane that worked a task with its round and its tokens, two
decisions — one starred — and two friction reports, one of them closed):

- `the_decisions_view_shows_the_log_the_star_and_the_frictions` — the
  heading, every decision with its time, its star and its reason, the count by
  kind, and the friction reports with their state;
- `the_time_and_tokens_view_shows_the_work_of_every_task` — the row of the
  task (estimate, actual time, ratio, rounds, tokens, cost) and the total;
- `the_time_and_tokens_view_opens_the_task_panel_of_its_row` — the rows of
  the view are the tasks that ran, in order;
- `the_reason_of_a_decision_is_readable_on_eighty_columns` — the whole reason
  stands on an 80-column board (F2);
- `the_decisions_are_the_ones_of_the_voyage_the_board_measures` — an older
  voyage does not take the decisions away (F3);
- `the_rounds_of_a_task_are_the_rows_of_the_rounds_table` — a dev round and a
  review round are two rounds (F4);
- `the_unknown_usage_is_not_shown_as_zero` — a measured zero is a zero, an
  unpriced cost is a `?`, and the total names what it does not know (F5).

## Open questions

The count by kind is written in the order of the kinds (`1 cut, 1 scope`); the
design says "one line with the count of the others by kind" (§7.2) without
naming an order.

The reason of a decision stands on its own line in the prototype; here it
follows the decision on the same line, so that the selectable rows are the
decisions themselves.

## Out of scope

The decisions of the voyage report and of the landing review (the count per
kind is the same fact, §7.5): T-171. The tokens of the open rounds (counted
60 s after a round closes, §4.3): T-167.
