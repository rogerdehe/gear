# T-237 — Lowering `[quota] temps` retires the extra reviewer seats

Scope: `crates/gear-app/src/admin.rs` (the config-change path of `re_register`
plus `fn retire_excess_temps`), the new test
`crates/gear-app/tests/it/temps_quota_contract.rs` (registered in
`tests/it/main.rs`) and this report.

Candidate: the tip of `mast/t237` — the change `64fbb2cf`, merged with
`origin/dev` as `ee61fee8`, and this report on top.

## What changed

`fn re_register` (the config-change path of `gear admin init`), right after
`tx.update_project(&row)?` — the row that carries the new `quota_temps` — now
calls `retire_excess_temps(tx, &project_id, config.quota.temps, now)?`.

`fn retire_excess_temps`:

1. lists the project's seats with role `Temp` whose state is not `Retired`;
2. does nothing when there are no more of them than `config.quota.temps`;
3. keeps every `Active` or `Starting` seat — a working reviewer is never
   retired, and it holds a slot of the quota (the same accounting as the
   engine's `seats_free_for_new`, where every non-retired temp seat counts);
4. fills the slots that are left with the `Released` / `Standby` seats, newest
   `state_since` first (the id breaks a tie, so a run is reproducible);
5. retires every `Released` / `Standby` seat past them with
   `tx.set_seat_state(&seat.id, SeatState::Retired, Some("temps quota lowered to <n>"), now)`.

A seat in any other state (`Held`, `Parked`, `NeedsCheck`, `Releasing`, …) is
neither kept by rule 4 nor retired: the packet retires only `Released` and
`Standby`. Nothing new is printed.

## The tests

`crates/gear-app/tests/it/temps_quota_contract.rs`, on the same `admin init`
fixture as `config_change_contract` (a registered `harbor` project, one lane,
one captain; four reviewer seats inserted with `insert_seat`):

- `a_lower_temps_quota_retires_the_oldest_released_reviewers`: seats
  `review-7` (Active, `state_since` T0+10), `review-9` (Released, T0+20),
  `review-10` (Released, T0+30) and `review-11` (Released, T0+40); re-applied
  with `temps = 2`. The Active seat stays `Active` with no reason, `review-11`
  stays `Released`, and `review-9` / `review-10` are `Retired` with the reason
  `"temps quota lowered to 2"`.
- `a_quota_that_fits_the_seats_retires_nothing`: the same four seats re-applied
  with `temps = 4` — every seat keeps its state.

## Two defaults taken (asked, not waited for)

Both are packet-internal contradictions; the questions are the messages of
thread `T-237:1` and `T-237:2` on `gear:captain`.

1. **The count rule.** The packet says "keep the `config.quota.temps` seats with
   the newest `state_since` plus every seat whose state is Active or Starting",
   which for the acceptance fixture (one Active at T0+10, three Released at
   T0+20/+30/+40, `temps = 2`) keeps three seats and retires one. Its acceptance
   says the Active one and the newest Released stay and the two older Released
   are retired. Default, and what is implemented: the quota is a total — the
   Active/Starting seats are never retired and count in it, the newest idle
   seats fill the rest (option A of `M-4086`). That is the only reading that
   satisfies the acceptance test, and it agrees with the engine's own quota
   accounting.
2. **Standby → Retired.** `SeatState::can_transition(Temp, Standby, Retired)`
   is false (the Temp arm has `Standby -> Releasing` only), and
   `Tx::set_seat_state` documents that its caller has checked the transition.
   Default: literal — a `Standby` seat is retired with the raw write the packet
   names, and the model's table is not touched (the packet scopes the change to
   `admin.rs`).

## Out of scope

- A `Standby` reviewer has a live agent and an `Active` binding. Retiring it
  with the raw `set_seat_state` leaves that pane and binding behind: the
  engine's own retire path (`release.rs`) closes the pane and voids the
  binding, and nothing reaps a `Retired` seat that still has an active
  binding. The packet's call is `set_seat_state` only, so the pane of a retired
  `Standby` reviewer stays open (see question 2 above; option 2 of it would let
  the engine walk such a seat out through `Releasing`).
- The extra seats' worktrees and build caches are left on disk, as the engine's
  own retire leaves them.
- `gear admin init --check` and the rest of M4.2 are untouched.
