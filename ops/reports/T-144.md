Scope: T-144 part 1 — `crates/gear-engine/src/session.rs`: the signatures of
design §13.4, `choose` as a pure function of the facts for the rules L2-L7,
V1-V2 and S1 of §3.3, and `prepare_lane`/`prepare_review`: the round row of
§4.1 (role `dev` | `rework` | `review`), the actions `keep` and `fresh` run

Branch: hull/t144 (from origin/dev; code @ the hand-in commit)

## The module

`crates/gear-engine/src/session.rs`:

- `Prep` — `Ready` (the round's session is the one it needs: the packet may
  go) or `Wait` (the step runs one tick at a time), as §13.4 says.
- `Role` — `Dev`, `Rework`, `Review`: the role of the round row.
- `Action` — `Keep`, `Fresh`, `New`, `Compact`, `Resume(session)`: the
  actions of §3.3-§3.4, with the name the attempt records (`keep`, `fresh`,
  `new`, `compact`, `resume`).
- `Facts` — the facts the rules read: the seat and its state, the role, the
  attempt (`n ≥ 2` is a rework), the follow-up `F`, the earlier work (its
  seat, its CLI, its session), the seat's current session and CLI, whether
  the agent is alive, and the session of the round that is open.
- `choose(facts) -> Action` — the rules of §3.3 as a pure function: the same
  facts always give the same action.
- `prepare_lane(...)` / `prepare_review(...)` — the signatures of §13.4.

## The rules, as they are read

The rules are read top to bottom, and the first match wins.

- **S1** — the round is open and its agent died or was restarted: the round
  resumes its session, in the same round (no new round row).
- **L2-L4** — a rework (`n ≥ 2`) continues the session of attempt `n−1`:
  the same seat and the same CLI and the same session → `keep` (L2); the same
  seat and the same CLI but another session → `resume` that session (L3);
  another seat, another CLI or no session recorded → `new` (L4).
- **L5-L6** — a follow-up (`followup-of: F`) continues the session of F's
  last accepted attempt: the same session → `keep` (L5), another session →
  `resume` it (L6).
- **L7** — an unrelated task → `new`.
- **V1** — an earlier review round of this task ran on this seat with the
  same CLI, and the seat waits (`Standby`) or is free (`Released`): the same
  session → `keep`, another session → `resume` it.
- **V2** — any other case → `new`.
- **L1** is not a rule of its own: a seat without a live agent has its agent
  started plainly (with the resume args when the rule says `resume`), and the
  action becomes `fresh`.

## The round row

`prepare_lane` and `prepare_review` create the round row of §4.1 on the
first call and keep it over the ticks: the seat's own open round ends when
this one starts, so a seat never has two open rounds. The row's role is
`dev` (attempt 1), `rework` (attempt `n ≥ 2`) or `review`.

The action is recorded: on the attempt for a lane round
(`attempts.session_action`), in the meta store for a review round — a
reviewer has no attempt of its own, and the attempt row of a task belongs to
the lane that wrote it (this is the question M-2629 asks; the default I went
on with).

A round that is open goes on with the action it has; only S1 changes it.

  round make one row, and the action is the same both times.
- `a_rework_round_is_a_rework` and `a_review_round_is_recorded_on_its_own_key`
  — the role of the row and where the action is recorded.

## Out of scope

- The actions `new`, `compact` and `resume` are chosen but not run: part 2
  runs them (one step per tick, §3.4).
- The callers: keel's dispatch (lanes) and hull's review start call
  `prepare_lane`/`prepare_review` before the packet. They are not wired here:
  a caller that waits for `Prep::Ready` would block every review until part 2
  runs the actions.
- The seat a re-review takes (design §3.3 V1/V2) is T-146's.
