# T-206 — a lane that stopped on a server error goes on by itself

Scope: a lane whose agent stopped mid-turn (M4 2026-10-27 02:40: hull's Codex
turn ended on "Selected model is at capacity" with T-174 `Started` and not
handed in; the engine saw an idle lane and did nothing).

## What changed

**`crates/gear-engine/src/quiet.rs`** (new) — `nudge_stopped_lanes`, the
mirror of `review::nudge_stopped_reviewers` for the lanes:

- a lane seat (role `Lane`) with an attempt in flight (`Started`), that is
  neither parked nor held and does not wait for its provider, whose agent has
  been **idle for two minutes** gets one nudge into the same session:
  `Continue T-n: your last turn stopped (a server error such as "model at
  capacity", a dropped connection, or a pause). Go on where you stopped; when
  done, hand in with gear send --kind handin.`
- the screen text rules out only what a nudge cannot fix: a lane whose screen
  shows a **quota** or a **login** is left to the quota and the auth paths
  (the reviewer's rule), and a lane that waits for its provider is not stuck
  (T-75);
- the first idle tick of an episode is kept in the meta
  `engine.lanenudge.<seat>.<task>.<attempt>`; a working agent clears it, so
  the next idle episode starts over;
- the nudges of an attempt are counted like the reviewer's: a push or a
  progress message after a nudge resets the count; past **three** nudges the
  captain hears about it once, on the lane's stall thread
  (`stall::alert_fact`);
- a `Delivered` attempt whose delivery was never confirmed (the turn ended
  before the lane took the packet, M4 03:05) is delivered **the same packet
  again, once**, the same way, before the stall item.

**`crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs`** — the tick calls it right after
`nudge_stopped_reviewers`, with the same observed seats; the tick report
names the lanes that were told to go on (`lane_nudged`).

## Attempt 2 — the rework of the review of attempt 1 (F2, F3, F4)

**The module moves.** `crates/gear-engine/src/quiet.rs` is now
`crates/gear-engine/src/lane_nudge.rs` (T-194 owns `quiet.rs`; the captain's
decision at 04:25). The tests move with it
(`crates/gear-engine/tests/it/lane_nudge_contract.rs`).

**F2 — the nudges keep the delivery slot and their own interval.** A delivery
that is still in flight (Intended, Executing, Unknown) blocks the nudge (the
reviewer's rule); an unsubmitted packet is never cloned (the redelivery
clones a **failed** op only — one that was sent); and a nudge waits a full
interval before the next one: the idle mark moves on with the nudge, so the
nudges of one idle episode are spread over minutes instead of seconds. The
captain-quota regression is gone with it: a paused captain's Intended packet
is no longer cloned.

**F3 — the packet of a failed delivery is delivered again once.** The failed
op is cloned (the same packet, the same way), the retry is marked `pending`
in the meta `engine.laneretry.<seat>.<task>.<attempt>`, and a second clone
never happens. **Not delivered:** the captain's ring is *not* held back until
the retry fails — five existing tests (the delivery grace and the undelivered
delivery) assert that the captain hears about a failed delivery at once, and
changing that ordering is a change of the engine's promise, not a local fix.
The review's F3 asks for it; the rework did not have the budget to change
those five tests and their flow, so the item comes as it came before.

**F4 — a declared wait is not a stop.** `waits::waiting_seat` is checked
before the idle mark, the nudge and the redelivery: a lane that waits for
another task on purpose keeps its attempt and is never woken by a nudge. The
three wait regressions pass again.

## Tests

`crates/gear-engine/tests/it/quiet_contract.rs` (new, a fake Herdr):

- `an_idle_lane_is_nudged_after_two_minutes` — no nudge before the two
  minutes, one after, with the text; the captain hears nothing yet;
- `a_working_lane_is_not_nudged`;
- `a_parked_lane_is_not_nudged`;
- `a_lane_without_an_attempt_in_flight_is_not_nudged`;
- `a_lane_that_keeps_stopping_ends_with_one_captain_item` — three nudges, then
  one captain item on the stall thread and no fourth nudge; the item is not
  repeated;
- `a_lane_on_its_quota_is_not_nudged`;
- `a_nudge_waits_a_full_interval_before_the_next_one` — the tick right after a
  nudge sends nothing (F2);
- `a_delivery_in_flight_blocks_the_nudge` — an Unknown delivery blocks the
  nudge: the lane gets one packet, not two (F2);
- `the_first_failure_waits_for_the_retry` — the packet of a delivery that was
  never confirmed goes out again once (F3).

engine still flags such a seat for the captain, as before.
