Scope: T-8 — when `plan apply` bumps the contract of a task with an attempt in progress, the engine delivers one line at the lane's next idle point ("T-<n> contract changed to v<k>: run `gear` and `gear --show T-<n>`") and the lane's next `gear` view adopts it (the attempt's adopted version moves to the new one).
Branch: data/t8 (code @ c4e310a, branch head b8f9193)
Files:

- crates/gear-app/src/tasks.rs — `adopt_contract`: the attempt's `adopted_version` moves to the pending version and the marker is cleared; never lowers the version, and a missing/older marker is a no-op.

- A lane that never runs `gear` keeps the pending marker (the delivery is not resent; the normal check budget flags the seat).
