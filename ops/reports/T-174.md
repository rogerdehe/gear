# T-174: lane rebind plan

`# Staff` accepts `- rebind <lane> repo=<name> owns=[<path>, ...]`. Apply validates the lane, target repo and ownership, records a durable request, and prints the open attempts it waits for. Once no non-terminal attempts remain, the engine reconciles the request into the stored config, preserving old tasks' repo/base/gate, including legacy tasks without snapshots. An idle lane's change applies immediately.

The captain guide documents the instruction. Pending rebinds permit existing attempts to finish but prevent new assignments; after the config moves, dispatch waits for T-175 to move the worktree and clear `lane-rebind-applied:<seat>`. This prevents work from being sent into the old repo between the two tasks. T-175 was informed of the durable marker through the task progress message.
