# T-202: quota signal freshness and Codex reviewer selection

Quota and provider-error signals now keep separate episode-start timestamps. A continuing signal retains its start through confirmation, and an in-flight counter with a missing or zero timestamp seeds a safe new start. A cached reading older than that start cannot decide the park after a signal read fails.

Codex quota polling selects a Temp reviewer with an Active Codex binding, preferring Active seats over Standby and excluding Released seats and Void bindings. If none is eligible, it passes no session. Fake-reader regressions cover quota-only and error-only signals with both quota-left and spent cached readings, failed signal reads, released and void reviewers, standby priority, and the no-eligible-reviewer case.

The new regressions passed in `scripts/test.sh`, which passed all 1,206 tests (257 s build, 145 s suite). Shared load produced slow-test warnings in existing Git-heavy fixtures.
