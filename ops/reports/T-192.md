# T-192 — A late check-in records its session (contract v2)

Scope: T-192 (T-151 round 2 F4) — in `crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`,
`confirm_late_views` records the session of the attempt it starts, and it
reports an attempt only when it changes it.

Branch: keel/t192 (code @ the hand-in commit; origin/dev merged before the
final verification)

## What was built

`crates/gear-engine/src/ops.rs`:

- **The session of a late check-in.** A delivery whose answer was lost fails
  after the grace window; the lane checks in after all, and the attempt
  starts. Right after `start_confirmed_attempt` returns that it started the
  attempt, `record_attempt_session(tx, project, &op)` writes the session the
  seat's binding knows (design §3.2 step 5) — the same call the ordinary
  check-in makes, with its guards: a review delivery names the writer's
  attempt but the reviewer's binding, so it writes nothing; a delivery of
  another seat's round leaves the attempt alone. An attempt that is already
  Started keeps the session it has.
- **The scan reports only what it changes.** Before, every tick reported a
  late-confirmed attempt that was already Started (the 19:54 engine logged
  `late_confirmed T-160/1` on every tick). The report now carries the
  attempts the scan itself changed: the one it started, and the late hand-in
  it settled (that one keeps its own meta marker, so it is reported once).

## The test

`crates/gear-engine/tests/it/delivery_grace_contract.rs`:
`a_late_check_in_records_the_session_and_is_reported_once` — a Delivered
attempt with no session, a binding with the session `ses_A` and the agent
kind `opencode`, a Submitted Deliver at T0+10, `confirm_pending_with` with a
grace of 180 s at T0+200 (the op fails), a matching view at T0+210, and
`confirm_late_views` at T0+220: the attempt is `Started` with
`session_ref = ses_A` and `session_cli = opencode`. The second scan reports
nothing, and the recorded session is the one that stays.

The test fails on the code of attempt 1 (checked: the old `ops.rs` makes it
fail) and passes with the fix.
