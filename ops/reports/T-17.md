## What counts as "no longer under review"

- the task is not `Verifying` (cancelled, done, waiting …), or
- its latest attempt is not `Submitted` (settled, rejected, failed back to ready), or
- the latest attempt is not the one the reviewer was started for — the initial `Deliver` op names it, so a newer attempt handed in makes the old reviewer stale.

A reviewer that is already `Released`/`Retired`, or one a human has to look at (`NeedsCheck`, `Missing`, `Unknown`), is skipped: the engine does not quietly take a slot back from a seat under check.

## Why two ticks

The exit keys and the removal are separate ticks. A restart between them must not send `ctrl+c` twice (the second would land in the shell), so the seat's own state is the guard: only an `Active` seat sends keys, only a `Releasing` one (or a reviewer that never started) removes the worktree. A removal that is refused for a stale id counts as already gone; any other refusal leaves the seat `Releasing` for the next tick and is reported, and the loop carries on with the next reviewer.
