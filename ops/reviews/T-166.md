## Findings

F1 [P1, blocking] Decisions renders all unstarred entries in full and includes starred entries in the summary count — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:395`, `:437`. Contract v1 asks for “★ decisions in full, then a count per kind”; design §7.2 identifies the count as the other decisions. The submitted fixture renders the unstarred scope decision before the starred cut and reports `by kind: 1 cut, 1 scope`; it should render the starred cut in full and summarize the unstarred scope. On a real voyage ordinary staff/estimate entries bury the course changes. Breaks done (2) and the STABLE usability bar. Smallest fix: in `view_decisions`, filter the full rows to starred decisions, enumerate after filtering, and in `counts_of_kind` count only unstarred decisions (about 5–10 lines); update the fixture assertions for mixed starred/unstarred entries.

F2 [P1, blocking] Appending the reason to the decision line clips it out of the ordinary terminal view — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:404`. With the submitted fixture at 80 columns, the starred line ends at `why: needs token data from T`; `needs token data from T-141 first` is not visible in full. Longer normal decision text hides the reason completely. Decisions has no Enter panel to recover the omitted reason. The prototype deliberately puts `why:` on a separate continuation line. Breaks done (2), the task's “in full” requirement, and the STABLE usability bar. Smallest fix: emit the reason as a separate indented row in `view_decisions` instead of appending it (about 5–10 lines), preserving selection/render alignment, and assert that the fixture's full reason remains visible at 80 columns.

F3 [P1, blocking] Decisions selects the oldest milestone by identifier rather than the voyage the board is showing — `crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs:863`. `voyages_of_project` is ordered by milestone, so `.first()` selects M1 on an ordinary project retaining historical voyages. Probe: keep M4 active with its two decisions and add a completed M1 with no decisions; Workload remains M4 but Decisions becomes empty. Breaks done (2) and the STABLE normal-use bar. Smallest fix: choose the same active/latest-departed voyage as `workload_voyage` (or reuse its selected report) when calling `decisions_of`, removing the alphabetic-first helper (about 5–10 lines). Add the two-voyage fixture assertion.

F4 [P1, blocking] Time & tokens counts attempts instead of the task's stored rounds — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:470` (source: `crates/gear-board/src/lib.rs:428`). One attempt normally has both a dev round and a review round. Probe: one attempt with those two rows returns `store rounds=2`, but the board displays `rounds=1`; the task panel correctly lists both. Contract v1 explicitly requests rounds from the rounds table. Breaks done (2) and the STABLE normal-use bar. Smallest fix: supply the Tokens view with `rounds_of_task(...).len()` (about 1–5 lines; preserve any separate attempt-based rework indicator), and test one attempt containing dev and review rounds.

F5 [P1, blocking] Unknown usage and price are displayed as measured zero — `crates/gear-board/src/frame.rs:462`. A task with `tokens=None` and `cost_usd=None` renders `0k  $  0.00`, and the footer also presents a zero/partial sum as the total. Missing session logs or model prices are normal supported states; design §4.3 explicitly preserves unknown counts instead of zero. Breaks done (2) and the STABLE bar by misleading the user about consumption/cost. Smallest fix: format absent task values as `?`/`unknown`, and mark totals as unknown or explicitly label them as known subtotals when any component is missing (about 15–25 lines in `view_tokens`). In `tokens_of`, use presence of token fields rather than `tokens > 0` to distinguish a measured zero from unknown (about 3–5 lines). Add absent-usage and measured-zero assertions.
