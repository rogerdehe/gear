## Findings
F1 [P1, blocking] An earlier verdict ends a re-review before its current verdict — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs:218` — `handed_a_verdict` accepts any attempt of the task judged by this seat. Normal reproduction: attempt 1 fails; its reviewer is released when W=0; the warm seat is reused for attempt 2 of the same task. Once that reviewer becomes Active, the next release step finds attempt 1's verdict, sets the seat to Standby and clears task_id even though attempt 2 has no verdict. With W=0 it also closes the active reviewer's pane; otherwise it leaves the review unbound. This breaks the STABLE bar (normal rework/re-review cannot complete reliably) and the done-sentence's working rework/review loop. Smallest fix: in `handed_a_verdict`, replace the all-attempt scan with the attempt identified by the seat's current review Deliver operation, validating its task; use the latest task attempt only when no job operation exists for legacy/test seats (about 15–25 lines). Do not simply check the latest task attempt: the writer may already have started rework when the previous review's completion is processed. Add regression tests with a real attempt-1 verdict and current attempt-2 Deliver: no attempt-2 verdict must leave the seat Active, bound and its pane open; attempt-2 verdict must put it on Standby. Also preserve completion of a delivered attempt 1 when a newer writer attempt exists. The existing `a_rereview_resumes_the_jobs_session` test omits the first verdict and never runs release after activating attempt 2, so it does not catch this failure.
