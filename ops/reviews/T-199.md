## Findings

F1 [P1, blocking] A hand-in during direction startup strands the only temp slot — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs:234` (and `start_reviews:175`). Reproduce with the existing one-slot direction fixture: set `FakeHerdr.refuse_starts = Some(("agent_pane_busy", 1))`, tick once so the direction meta is `starting` and review-1 is Starting, submit the lane's attempt, then tick repeatedly. Full review excludes the direction seat and cannot allocate another; direction startup is skipped both because the attempt is Submitted and because the hand-in is waiting. No reviewer ever starts. The release guard also regards this starting job as live. This breaks §14's “never blocks a hand-in” and the STABLE bar for normal startup timing. Smallest fix: before scheduling hand-ins, cancel/release or safely convert a still-starting direction reservation when a hand-in needs its slot, clear the direction reservation/pending-job state, and let the hand-in take that seat (about 25–40 lines in review.rs); add the one-busy-start/submit regression.

F2 [P1, blocking] A refused direction packet is never recovered while Started, and becomes a full-review packet after submission — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs:1522–1528`, reached by the new running direction job at `:1095`. Reproduce by starting a direction check, marking its Deliver op Failed with `agent_not_ready`, and ticking: the attempt has no candidate, so recovery skips it forever while the running marker suppresses scheduling. If the lane submits, recovery instead prepares role review and emits a verdict packet to the still-bound direction seat. This violates the exact direction packet/result contract and the STABLE bar for a single normal delivery refusal. Smallest fix: resolve the active job's role and SHA from direction meta in recover_missing_packets (or use the direction-aware job lookup), call prepare_job_session/job_packet for that role, and preserve direction authorization (about 15–25 lines in review.rs). Cover retry before and after lane submission.

F3 [P1, blocking] The full review reuses the direction checkout without switching to its review branch — `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs:812`, interacting with the role-free checkout cache at `:589–590`. Reproduce: complete a direction check with ok, submit that same SHA, and let the same Standby reviewer take the full review. Its cached task/attempt/SHA/repo identity matches, so prepare_seat_tree returns before checkout; the packet tells the reviewer to write on review/t1 but its worktree stays on direction/t-1. This is a normal transition introduced by this change and breaks the STABLE bar by making the review handoff require a manual branch fix. Smallest fix: include the target branch in the checkout cache identity (one line), and assert a direction-to-review transition at unchanged SHA checks out review/t1.
