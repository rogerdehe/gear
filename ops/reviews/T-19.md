Slice 1 only intends doorbells (`intend_doorbells` writes `OpKind::Doorbell`), the deviation is documented in the Result, and the task's text is explicitly the doorbell text — future kinds are later packets; see the question.
- A failure of the phase-3 transaction after prompts were sent leaves those ops `Executing` until the process restarts, when `recover_on_start` turns them `Unknown` with no resend. Consistent with design/08's "unknown is not resent"; only reachable through a DB error, and the packet does not cover mid-run recovery — no change requested.

- Two Intended doorbell ops for one seat cannot both execute in practice: `slot_busy` includes `Intended`, so `intend_doorbells` does not write a second one while the first waits.
- The stricter Void guards (missing/inactive binding, missing text) are extra relative to the packet; voiding with a stored reason is safe and keeps "nothing sent".
- The packet's Status line, checkboxes and Lane record edits are within the allowed scope; no `pub` signature changed (the only `+pub fn execute_intended` is the same signature re-wrapped), no Cargo.toml or other crate touched.
