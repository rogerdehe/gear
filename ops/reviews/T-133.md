Otherwise choose Queued; fail clearly if the observation cannot be obtained rather than treating an unknown observation as absence. About 40–60 implementation lines plus a fake-Herdr test. Extend the release integration test to remove the fake agent while leaving its binding Active, then verify Queued and engine onboarding/next-task delivery. Keep the existing running-agent and non-held refusal checks. No lifecycle redesign is needed.
