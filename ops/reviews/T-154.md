## Findings
F1 [P1, blocking] A captain-resolved delivery stall rings again after the lane successfully checks in — `crates/gear-engine/src/stall.rs:65` (consumed by `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs:459`). Reproduction using the existing `undelivered_delivery_contract.rs` World: tick at offsets 1 and 9 (8-second delivery grace), resolve the resulting stall at 10, insert the lane's task/attempt view at 20, set its FakeHerdr status to Working, tick at 21, assert the attempt is Started, then tick at 910. Actual: a new urgent Open stall appears at 910, although the lane recovered at 20. `confirm_late_views` calls `resolve(..., Some(view_ts))`; with no open message this branch leaves `stall-active` set, and the retained failure record makes the new reminder loop flag the lane. Breaks the contract's cause-gone behavior and the STABLE bar's normal-use stability/usability requirement. Small fix: in `stall::resolve`, inspect the latest stall incident even when its message is already resolved, and clear its active marker when that incident predates the bounded check-in; preserve the guard against an old view clearing a newer incident (about 8–15 lines). Add the above full-tick regression alongside the existing delivery tests.

F2 [P1, blocking] Cancelling the failed task leaves its stall cause active — `crates/gear-engine/src/lib.rs:457`. Reproduction with the same World: tick at 1 and 9, cancel T-1 at 10 through `gear_app::plan::apply` (the normal cancelled Queue entry used in `cancel_tick_contract.rs`), then tick at 21 with no next task queued. The attempt is Abandoned, but its original urgent stall remains Open. The loop treats any saved failed-delivery record plus `stall-active` as a current failure; no path checks whether its referenced attempt/task is terminal. It can continue reminding about work the captain intentionally cancelled. Breaks the cause-gone requirement and the STABLE normal-use bar. Small fix: in tick's failed-delivery revisit, load the referenced task/attempt; when it is cancelled/terminal, clear that failure record and resolve its associated stall before deciding whether to flag it (about 15–25 lines). Preserve an independently observed missing-agent flag or another current stall cause on that seat. Add a cancellation regression asserting the abandoned attempt's stall closes and stays closed beyond the reminder period.
