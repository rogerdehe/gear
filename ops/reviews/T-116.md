F2 [P1, blocking] Releasing a parked checkout resets its three-try budget and restarts the branch-held loop — `crates/gear-engine/src/release.rs:231`, interacting with `crates/gear-engine/src/review.rs:588` and `:984`. Reproduced by starting one review in the supplied fixture, reporting `GEAR_CK_FAIL_<current token>` and calling start_reviews three times: the seat is Unknown and exactly three checkout commands were submitted. Then call release_dead_temps and start_reviews once more: a fourth checkout command is submitted. Further failures repeat this indefinitely. This regresses the ordinary persistent checkout-failure path, breaks done clause (3)'s backlog item 2 requirement that T-69 stop after three tries, and violates the DELIVERABLE bar by reintroducing a normal-use loop. Smallest fix: retain an exhausted checkout marker keyed by project/task/attempt when Checked::Stuck occurs, and have start_reviews skip that attempt before assigning a seat (roughly 20–35 lines in review.rs). Keep releasing the dead seat so other reviews can use its slot; a new attempt must have a fresh budget. Extend the existing three-tries regression through subsequent engine ticks, asserting no fourth submission and one captain item while another task can use the freed seat.
